# Preview and Export Research Data

Preview and Export are the final steps in the EcoData workflow. Treat this stage as data quality validation before importing into quantitative analysis software.

## What to Check in Preview

| Item | Question |
| --- | --- |
| Entity scope | Does the data cover the right indicators, countries, provinces, tickers, or commodities? |
| Administrative boundary | For Vietnam provincial data, have you selected `63 Provinces` or `34 Provinces` under Resolution 202/2025/QH15? |
| Time coverage | Is the year or reporting-period range sufficient across the 1995–2003, 2003–2008, and 2025 milestones? |
| Measurement unit | Are measurement units consistent across series? |
| Missing values | Are important years or entities missing? |
| Metadata | Are source, definition, methodology, aggregation rule, and citation available? |
| Table structure | Is the table long, wide, pivoted, panel-style, or record-level? |

## Dashboard Export

1. Select indicators.
2. Select countries, country groups, or provinces.
3. Choose the research year range.
4. Choose the output table structure.
5. Enable metadata sheets to guarantee research reproducibility.
6. Preview the sample table.
7. Download directly or track a full export request.

## Exports in Specialized Modules

| Module | Export type |
| --- | --- |
| GSO and NSO Vietnam Explorer | Multi-sheet CSV or Excel with indicator metadata and `63 Provinces` or `34 Provinces` boundary regime tags. |
| Vietnam Customs Explorer | CSV or Excel across 11 trade forms `1X..PTVT` and selected analytical dimensions. |
| Commodity Market Explorer | CSV or Excel across 5 strategic commodity sectors with primary source footnotes. |
| Governance Survey Explorer | CSV or Excel for provincial PCI, PGI, PAPI, PII, ICT, PAR, and SIPAS indices. |
| VHLSS Variable Hub | Dataset by single-wave, fixed-panel, or pooled mode. |
| Stock Hub | Historical prices, financial statements across 4 accounting templates, notes, or corporate events. |

## Account Permissions

The platform distinguishes preview and full export by subscription entitlement. Free accounts have preview permissions to inspect sample data structures; research plans can generate full exports with complete metadata workbooks.

## Post-Download Checklist

- The file has the expected number of rows and columns.
- Metadata or description sheets are saved alongside the data.
- Indicator names and `VARCHAR(18)` short codes are preserved to match Stata, R, and Python scripts.
- Measurement units, `63` or `34` provincial boundary mode tags, and primary source codes are preserved when importing into analysis software.
- For multi-source datasets, keep a record of merge and transformation scripts.
