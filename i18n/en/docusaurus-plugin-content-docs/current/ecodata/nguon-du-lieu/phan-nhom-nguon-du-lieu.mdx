# Data Source Groups and Seven Specialized Subsystems

EcoData unifies international and domestic economic feeds into a single empirical workflow. When selecting a data source, start from your **unit of analysis** and **administrative boundary mode** rather than searching by individual agency names.

## Source Map by Unit of Analysis

| Unit of Analysis | Suitable Sources and Subsystems | App Module |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Country and Eight Global Focus Regions | World Bank, IMF, OECD, Eurostat, ILO, FAO, WTO, UNCTAD, ADB, FRED, OWID, Eight Global Focus Regions | Dashboard |
| Vietnam Province in 63-Province or 34-Province Mode | National Statistics, State Budget, Socio-Economic Reports, 34-Province Series, Provincial Yearbook, VHLSS aggregates | GSO Explorer |
| Strategic Commodities and Trade Partners | Vietnam Customs across 11 forms, Commodity Market across five sectors | Customs Explorer |
| Provincial Governance and Institutional Indices | Provincial Governance Indices covering PCI, PGI, PAPI, PII, PAR Index, SIPAS, ICT Index | Survey Explorer |
| Household and Individual Microdata | VHLSS, VARHS, and VES survey microdata | VHLSS Variable Hub |
| Listed Firms on HOSE, HNX, and UPCoM | 30-year financial statements across four accounting regimes, notes, corporate events | Stock Hub |

## Global Data and Eight Focus Economic Regions

This group supports cross-country comparisons, multi-country panel econometrics, and global macroeconomic control variables. EcoData connects directly to the World Bank, IMF, OECD, Eurostat, ILO, FAO, OWID, FRED, WTO, UNCTAD, and ADB, and organizes the **Eight Global Focus Economic Regions** subsystem across eight strategic global regions:
- **Vietnam**
- **United States**
- **China**
- **India**
- **Europe**
- **East Asia**
- **ASEAN**
- **Americas, Middle East, and Oceania**

Core indicator families include:
- GDP growth and GDP per capita in current USD, constant USD, and purchasing power parity.
- CPI inflation, exchange rates, policy interest rates, money supply, and balance of payments.
- Labor force participation, employment, wages, and labor productivity.
- Merchandise trade, tariffs, foreign direct investment, and logistics performance.
- Demographics, education, health outcomes, human development, and national institutional quality.

## Seven Specialized Vietnam Data Subsystems From Finco and Commodity Markets

EcoData integrates seven specialized domestic economic and financial subsystems standardized across both 63-province and 34-province administrative regimes:

| Subsystem Code | Subsystem Name | Scope and Data Coverage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **National Statistics** | National Statistics Office PX-Web and 34-Province Appendices | 28 provincial PX-Web statistical tables and the official 2024 Statistical Yearbook Appendices for 34 provinces comprising 14 thematic appendices. |
| **State Budget** | Ministry of Finance State Budget Revenue and Expenditure | State budget outturn and estimate series from 1995 to 2026 published by the Ministry of Finance covering total revenue, domestic revenue, trade tax revenue, total expenditure, capital expenditure, and recurrent expenditure. |
| **Socio-Economic** | Periodic Socio-Economic Statistical Reports | Monthly, quarterly, and annual series from 2000 to 2026 covering GRDP, IIP, CPI, realized investment, retail sales, and transport. |
| **Vietnam Customs** | Vietnam Customs Statistics Across 11 Official Forms | Official merchandise trade statistics across 11 operational forms: `1X`, `1N`, `2X`, `2N`, `3X`, `3N`, `4`, `5X`, `5N`, `HTX`, and `PTVT`. |
| **Governance Indices** | Provincial Governance Survey Indices | Longitudinal provincial panel combining seven governance indices: `PCI`, `PGI`, `PAPI`, `PII`, `ICT Index`, `PAR Index`, and `SIPAS`. |
| **Commodity Market** | Five-Sector Commodity Market Intelligence | Supply-demand balances, production, trade flows, and prices across five strategic commodity sectors and five analytical tabs. |
| **34-Province Series** | 34-Province Historical Panel Rebuild Engine | Harmonized harmonized 34-province panel from 2000 to 2026 resolving the 1995–2003, 2003–2008, and 2025 boundary milestones across five mathematical rules. |

## Commodity Market Intelligence Across Five Sectors and Five Analytical Tabs

The Commodity Market subsystem provides end-to-end visibility into domestic and global commodity value chains:
- **Five Commodity Sectors:** Energy; Metals and Minerals; Agriculture and Food; Aquaculture and Livestock; Industrial Materials and Chemicals.
- **Five Analytical Tabs:** Overview; Production; Export; Import and Consumption; Price and Market.
- **Collapsible Primary Data Source Footnotes:** Placed at the bottom of each analytical card and collapsed by default, allowing researchers to inspect source table codes such as National Statistics, Vietnam Customs, and State Budget along with update schedules.

## Governance Surveys and VHLSS Household Microdata

The Provincial Governance Indices subsystem consolidates seven provincial governance indices used as institutional explanatory variables in regional studies. Complementing provincial aggregates, the **VHLSS Variable Hub** provides household-level microdata organized by survey wave, questionnaire module, and variable dictionary for custom merging and appending.

## Stock Hub Corporate Financials

Stock Hub powers corporate finance and capital market research through a three-tier cache and database architecture:
- Historical daily prices, trading volumes, market capitalization, and foreign investor flows.
- 30-year financial statements comprising Balance Sheets, Income Statements, Cash Flow Statements, and Financial Notes automatically routed across **four Vietnamese accounting regimes**: Non-Financial Corporates, Commercial Banks, Securities Brokerages, and Insurance Companies.
- Corporate event calendars, cash and stock dividends, and insider transactions.

## Practical Guidelines for Source Selection

1. For cross-country or regional macroeconomic comparisons, start with **Global Data** and **Eight Global Focus Regions**.
2. For longitudinal Vietnam provincial research, open **GSO Explorer**, select **34-province** or **63-province** mode, and combine **National Statistics**, **State Budget**, **Periodic Socio-Economic Reports**, and **Provincial Governance Indices**.
3. For commodity supply chain and international trade analysis, use **Vietnam Customs** and **Commodity Market Intelligence**.
4. For household or individual microeconometric research, use **VHLSS Variable Hub**.
5. For capital structure, firm performance, or equity valuation studies, use **Stock Hub**.
6. To automate multi-source retrieval and regression via AI assistants, use **AI Chat** or download the **37-tool EcoData MCP Skills Pack** at `/account/connect-apps`.
