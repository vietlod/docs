# Provincial Governance Indices and VHLSS 2004–2024 Microdata

This group combines two complementary empirical layers: the **Provincial Governance Indices** subsystem capturing provincial institutional quality and public administration, and the **VHLSS Variable Hub** paired with the **`analyze_vhlss_microdata`** MCP tool providing standardized individual, household, and commune microdata from 2004 through 2024.

## Seven Provincial Governance Indices in the Provincial Governance Subsystem

The Provincial Governance Indices subsystem standardizes longitudinal provincial panels across seven major institutional evaluation programs in Vietnam:

| Index Code | Survey Program Name | Issuing Agency and Empirical Scope |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **`PCI`** | Provincial Competitiveness Index | Published by VCCI to measure economic governance quality and business environment from the perspective of private enterprises. |
| **`PGI`** | Provincial Green Index | Published by VCCI to evaluate environmental governance, climate resilience, and green transition policies at the provincial level. |
| **`PAPI`** | Provincial Governance and Public Administration Performance Index | Jointly conducted by UNDP and CECODES to measure citizen experiences with public governance, transparency, and public service delivery. |
| **`PII`** | Provincial Innovation Index | Published by the Ministry of Science and Technology to measure regional innovation inputs, knowledge infrastructure, and socio-economic outputs. |
| **`ICT Index`** | Vietnam ICT Readiness Index | Published by the Ministry of Information and Communications to track digital infrastructure, human capital, and e-government readiness across provinces. |
| **`PAR Index`** | Public Administration Reform Index | Published by the Ministry of Home Affairs to evaluate administrative procedure reform, organizational streamlining, and public finance management. |
| **`SIPAS`** | Satisfaction Index of Public Administration Services | Published by the Ministry of Home Affairs to measure citizen and enterprise satisfaction with administrative service delivery. |

### 34-Province Boundary Harmonization Note for Provincial Governance Indices

Under EcoData's **non-aggregable observation preservation** rule, institutional perception scores collected prior to July 1, 2025 are preserved at their original 63-province observation level to prevent ecological fallacy when combining non-linear perception composites. When switching to **34-province mode**, EcoData attaches 34-province crosswalk keys and optional enterprise-weighted or population-weighted composite series for robustness checks.

## VHLSS Aggregates in GSO Explorer

Provincial aggregates published in official VHLSS reports — including monthly per-capita income, household expenditure, Gini inequality coefficients, and poverty rates — are available in **GSO Explorer** across both 63-province and 34-province modes under the denominator-weighted average rule.

## VHLSS 2004–2024 Microdata on Variable Hub and MCP `analyze_vhlss_microdata`

Conducted biennially by the National Statistics Office of Vietnam, the Vietnam Household Living Standards Survey follows a stratified two-stage probability-proportional-to-size sampling design across enumeration-area primary sampling units. Because sparsely populated mountainous provinces in the Northern Midlands and Central Highlands are intentionally oversampled for provincial precision, household inclusion probabilities vary substantially across strata.

### Survey Weights Decision Matrix for VHLSS Microdata

| Analytical Objective | Weighting and Variance Rule | Canonical VHLSS Weight Variable | Econometric Rationale and Diagnostic Gate |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Descriptive Means, Proportions, and Population Totals** | **Mandatory `pweight` + `psu` clustering** | `wt45` for 45,000-household income-demographic modules; `wt9` for itemized expenditure modules | Guarantees Horvitz–Thompson design consistency; unweighted sample means over-represent rural and oversampled mountainous strata. |
| **Poverty `FGT P0/P1/P2` and Inequality `Gini`, `Theil`, `P90/P10`** | **Mandatory `pweight` scaled by household size for per-capita welfare** | `wt45` for income inequality; `wt9` for consumption poverty | National Lorenz curves and poverty headcounts depend directly on inverse-probability stratum expansion factors. |
| **Single-Wave Causal and Structural Regressions: OLS/WLS, Logit, Probit, Tobit, PPML, IV-2SLS** | **Report Weighted and Unweighted in Parallel + `psu` cluster-robust SE** | `wt45` or `wt9` with `compare_unweighted=true` and `dumouchel_duncan_test=true` | Under Solon–Haider–Wooldridge and Deaton, unweighted estimators are more efficient by Kish's Unequal Weighting Effect $\text{UWE} = 1 + \text{CV}(w)^2$ when weights are exogenous; if the DuMouchel–Duncan $F$-test rejects $H_0$ ($p < 0.05$), endogenous stratification or unmodeled heterogeneity requires survey-weighted estimation. |
| **Multi-Wave Pooled Cross-Sections `mode="append"`** | **Normalize wave weights + wave fixed effects + `psu` clustering** | `multi_wave_weight_mode="wave_normalized"` or `"equal_wave"` | Prevents waves with larger sample sizes or population totals from dominating pooled pseudo-likelihood functions across 2014–2024. |
| **Rotating Household Longitudinal Panels `mode="merge_panel"`** | **Lock base-wave longitudinal weights within the same Census master frame** | `multi_wave_weight_mode="longitudinal_base"` within `2004–2008`, `2010–2018`, or `2020–2024` | Preserves initial target-population representativeness for Fixed Effects and Mundlak CRE models while blocking invalid cross-Census household ID links. |

### Declarative Microdata Feature Engineering in Web App and MCP

Both the **Variable Hub (`/data/vhlss-micro`)** and the **`analyze_vhlss_microdata`** MCP tool expose five declarative primitives so complex household and individual studies run end-to-end without ad-hoc external scripts:

1. **Conditional `1:m` Aggregations With Recall Scaling (`conditional_aggregations`):** Filter itemized child records by commodity or attribute codes and multiply 30-day regular recall outlays in Module 5A2 by `scale_factor=12` before summing with 12-month festive outlays in Module 5A1.
2. **Exclude-Self Peer Household Covariates (`exclude_self=true`):** When analyzing individual records `entity="individual"`, compute household sums or means strictly across *other* household members ($j \neq i$), eliminating mechanical reflection bias in migration and intra-household labor supply models.
3. **Official GSO CPI Deflation (`cpi_deflation`):** Automatically deflate nominal monetary columns to constant prices of any chosen reference wave such as `base_year=2020` across 2004–2024.
4. **Leave-One-Out Contextual Instruments (`contextual_variables` with `leave_one_out=true`):** Construct jackknife PSU-, commune-, or province-level peer prevalence and unit-value instruments excluding household $i$ for IV-2SLS, Control-Function IV-Probit, and 3SLS QUAIDS demand systems.
5. **Survey-Aware Triangle Runtime Replication:** Automatically generates `svyset psu [pweight=...]` scripts for Stata 18 MP, `survey::svydesign` scripts for R, and PSU-clustered Sandwich estimators for Python.
