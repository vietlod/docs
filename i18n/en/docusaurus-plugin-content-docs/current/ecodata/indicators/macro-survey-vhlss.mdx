# Provincial Governance Indices and VHLSS Household Microdata

This group combines two complementary empirical layers: the **Provincial Governance Indices** subsystem capturing provincial institutional quality and public administration, and the **VHLSS Variable Hub** providing household and individual microdata.

## Seven Provincial Governance Indices in the Provincial Governance Subsystem

The Provincial Governance Indices subsystem standardizes longitudinal provincial panels across seven major institutional evaluation programs in Vietnam:

| Index Code | Survey Program Name | Issuing Agency and Empirical Scope |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **`PCI`** | Provincial Competitiveness Index | Published by VCCI to measure economic governance quality and business environment from the perspective of private enterprises. |
| **`PGI`** | Provincial Green Index | Published by VCCI to evaluate environmental governance, climate resilience, and green transition policies at the provincial level. |
| **`PAPI`** | Provincial Governance and Public Administration Performance Index | Jointly conducted by UNDP and CECODES to measure citizen experiences with public governance, transparency, and public service delivery. |
| **`PII`** | Provincial Innovation Index | Published by the Ministry of Science and Technology to measure regional innovation inputs, knowledge infrastructure, and socio-economic outputs. |
| **`ICT Index`** | Vietnam ICT Readiness Index | Published by the Ministry of Information and Communications to track digital infrastructure, human capital, and e-government readiness across provinces. |
| **`PAR Index`** | Public Administration Reform Index | Published by the Ministry of Home Affairs to evaluate administrative procedure reform, organizational streamlining, and public finance management. |
| **`SIPAS`** | Satisfaction Index of Public Administration Services | Published by the Ministry of Home Affairs to measure citizen and enterprise satisfaction with administrative service delivery. |

### 34-Province Boundary Harmonization Note for Provincial Governance Indices

Under EcoData's **non-aggregable observation preservation** rule, institutional perception scores collected prior to July 1, 2025 are preserved at their original 63-province observation level to prevent ecological fallacy when combining non-linear perception composites. When switching to **34-province mode**, EcoData attaches 34-province crosswalk keys and optional enterprise-weighted or population-weighted composite series for robustness checks.

## VHLSS Aggregates in GSO Explorer

Provincial aggregates published in official VHLSS reports: including monthly per-capita income, household expenditure, Gini inequality coefficients, and poverty rates: are available in **GSO Explorer** across both 63-province and 34-province modes under the denominator-weighted average rule.

## VHLSS Microdata and Variable Hub

The VHLSS Variable Hub enables researchers to search variable dictionaries and build custom microdata extracts across living standards survey waves:

| Step | Action on Variable Hub |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Select target VHLSS survey waves from 2002 through 2024. |
| 2 | Choose questionnaire modules such as Demographics, Education, Health, Employment, Income, Expenditure, or Housing. |
| 3 | Filter variables by Vietnamese or English keywords or native Stata variable names. |
| 4 | Preview sample records and summary statistics to inspect distributions and missingness. |
| 5 | Export datasets using single-wave merge, cross-wave panel, or pooled append modes, or query directly via the MCP tool `get_vhlss_survey_data`. |
