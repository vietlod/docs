# Vietnam National and 34-Province Statistics

GSO Explorer is EcoData's central module for official national and provincial statistics of Vietnam. It integrates primary feeds from the National Statistics Office and the Ministry of Finance while supporting instant switching between **63 historical provinces** and **34 consolidated provinces and cities** under Resolution 202/2025/QH15.

## Integrated Datasets and Subsystems in GSO Explorer

| Dataset Code | Dataset Name | Research Scope and Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **National Statistics** | PX-Web Tables and 14 Statistical Yearbook Appendices for 34 Provinces | 28 provincial statistical tables from the official PX-Web portal and the 14 Statistical Yearbook 2024 Appendices compiled under the 34-province structure. |
| **State Budget** | Ministry of Finance State Budget Revenue and Expenditure | Ministry of Finance budget outturn and estimate series from 1995 to 2026 across both 63-province and 34-province modes. |
| **Socio-Economic** | Periodic Socio-Economic Statistical Reports | Monthly, quarterly, and annual indicators from 2000 to 2026 tracking GRDP, IIP, CPI, investment, and consumption. |
| **`NGTK-CN`** | National Statistical Yearbook | Long-horizon national macroeconomic time series for Vietnam. |
| **Provincial Yearbook** | Provincial Statistical Yearbook | Traditional 63-province panels alongside the harmonized 34-province panel in the 34-Province Harmonized Panel Series. |
| **`VHLSS Aggregate`** | Living Standards Survey Aggregates | Provincial income, expenditure, inequality, and poverty aggregates from official VHLSS publications. |

## Harmonization Across Three Historical Boundary Milestones From 1995 to 2026

When querying long provincial time series spanning 1995 to 2026, GSO Explorer and the MCP tool `get_gso_data` automatically handle three administrative boundary reforms:

1. **1995–2003 Milestone Under Resolution 22/2003/QH11:** Effective January 1, 2004, Lai Chau split into Lai Chau and Dien Bien; Dak Lak split into Dak Lak and Dak Nong; and Can Tho split into Can Tho City and Hau Giang. In **63-province mode**, EcoData backcasts 1995–2003 series using empirical 2004–2006 share weights. In **34-province mode**, Can Tho, Hau Giang, and Soc Trang belong to the same consolidated Can Tho City cluster, allowing exact summation of pre-2004 values without allocation error; Dien Bien and Lai Chau remain independent units in the 34-province catalog and retain empirical 2004–2006 share weights.
2. **2003–2008 Milestone Under Resolution 15/2008/QH12:** Effective August 1, 2008, Ha Tay Province, Me Linh District from Vinh Phuc, and four communes of Luong Son District from Hoa Binh merged into Hanoi Capital City. EcoData automatically detects official series already backcasted for expanded Hanoi prior to 2008 to prevent double-counting with Ha Tay.
3. **2025 Milestone Under Resolution 202/2025/QH15:** Consolidates 63 provinces into **34 provinces and cities** comprising 11 unchanged units and 23 consolidated units formed from 2 to 3 former provinces. EcoData preserves original 63-province tables and reconstructs the 34-province panel series using five mathematical rules:
   - **Additive Scale Variables** for extensive scale variables such as area, population, labor force, nominal GRDP, constant-price GRDP, budget revenue and expenditure, and investment capital.
   - **Weighted Mean and Ratio Variables** for intensive ratios and per-capita metrics such as GRDP per capita, population density, monthly income, and poverty rate.
   - **Real Scale Growth Rate Variables** recomputing growth rates from summed constant-price output of the consolidated cluster in period $t$ and period $t-1$.
   - **Price and Production Index Variables** chaining CPI and IIP series using retail sales or industrial value added weights.
   - **Qualitative Institutional Ranking Variables** preserving original institutional survey observations prior to July 2025.

## Core Indicator Groups in GSO Explorer

| Indicator Group | Representative Variables | 34-Province Aggregation Rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **National Accounts** | Nominal GRDP, constant-2010 GRDP, three-sector economic structure | Additive scale summation for GRDP levels; constant-price scale growth recomputation for growth rates |
| **Ministry of Finance State Budget** | Total state budget revenue, domestic revenue, trade tax revenue, total local budget expenditure, capital expenditure | `sum` |
| **Population and Labor** | Land area, average population, population density, labor force aged 15 and above, unemployment rate | Additive scale summation for area, population, labor; Denominator-weighted average for density and rates |
| **Industry and Investment** | Industrial Production Index IIP, total realized social investment, registered and implemented FDI | Additive scale summation for investment capital; index chaining for IIP |
| **Trade, Services, and Prices** | Retail sales of goods and consumer service revenue, freight traffic volume, Consumer Price Index CPI | Additive scale summation for retail and freight; index chaining for CPI |
| **Social, Education, and Health** | Monthly per-capita income, multidimensional poverty rate, hospital beds, schools, and teachers | Denominator-weighted average for income and poverty rate; Additive scale summation for facilities and beds |

## Workflow in GSO Explorer and EcoData MCP

1. Open **GSO Explorer** from the main navigation bar.
2. Select your boundary regime using the **[63 Provinces | 34 Provinces]** toggle in the top header.
3. Choose the target dataset: National Statistics, State Budget, Socio-Economic Reports, National Yearbook, Provincial Yearbook, or VHLSS Survey.
4. Select indicators, provinces, and the target year range from 1995 to 2026.
5. Inspect the Province × Year pivot table, interactive charts, and expand the **Primary Data Source Footnotes** panel at the bottom to verify source badges from the National Statistics Office, Ministry of Finance, and Vietnam Customs.
6. Export an Excel or CSV workbook with a dedicated metadata sheet, or query directly via MCP tools `get_gso_data` and `get_yearbook_data` using `boundary_mode="34"` or `"63"`.
