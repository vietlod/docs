# Customs 11-Form Trade Statistics and Five-Sector Commodity Intelligence

Customs Explorer unifies two core data subsystems: **Vietnam Customs** from official statistical reports of Vietnam Customs and **Commodity Market Intelligence** for commodity value-chain analysis. These feeds support research on international trade, partner market concentration, sector supply chains, and regional trade dynamics across both 63-province and 34-province regimes.

## Eleven Official Customs Forms in the Vietnam Customs Subsystem

| Form Code | Report Category | Granular Coverage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **`1X` and `1N`** | Major Commodity Aggregates | Volume, value, and average unit price for exports `1X` and imports `1N` by major commodity group across monthly, quarterly, and annual periods. |
| **`2X` and `2N`** | Partner Country Reports | Export value `2X` and import value `2N` segmented by partner country and territory. |
| **`3X` and `3N`** | Country × Commodity Matrix | Two-way cross-tabulation of partner markets and exported `3X` or imported `3N` commodities. |
| **`Form 4`** | Trade Balance and Enterprise Sector | Total merchandise trade and trade balance disaggregated between FDI enterprises and domestic enterprises. |
| **`5X` and `5N`** | Provincial Trade Reports | Provincial export value `5X` and import value `5N` supporting both **63 historical provinces** and **34 consolidated provinces**. |
| **`HTX`** | Trade Regime Breakdown | Trade flows segmented by commercial trade, processing trade, export manufacturing, and export-processing zones. |
| **`PTVT`** | Transport Mode Statistics | Merchandise trade value and volume across maritime, air, road, and rail transport modes. |

## Five-Sector Commodity Market Intelligence in Commodity Market Intelligence

The Commodity Market subsystem synthesizes domestic output, trade flows, and price series across **five strategic commodity sectors**:
1. **Energy:** Crude oil, refined petroleum products, natural gas, coal, and commercial electricity.
2. **Metals and Minerals:** Construction steel, hot-rolled coil, iron ore, copper, aluminum, and rare earths.
3. **Agriculture and Food:** Export rice, coffee, cashew nuts, black pepper, natural rubber, cassava, and sugar.
4. **Aquaculture and Livestock:** Shrimp, pangasius, processed seafood, live hogs, and animal feed.
5. **Industrial Materials and Chemicals:** Fertilizers, primary plastics, basic chemicals, cement, clinker, timber, and wood products.

Every commodity is presented across **five standardized analytical tabs**:
- **Overview:** Supply-demand balance summary, trade market share, and latest price momentum.
- **Production:** Domestic extraction, manufacturing output, and sector capacity by reporting period.
- **Export:** Export volume, value, and average unit price segmented by partner market and province.
- **Import and Consumption:** Imported input volume, value, and apparent domestic consumption estimates.
- **Price and Market:** Domestic spot prices, border export-import unit values, and global benchmark spreads.

## Collapsible Primary Data Source Footnotes

At the bottom of each commodity chart and table, EcoData includes a **Primary Data Source Footnotes** panel collapsed by default. Expanding the panel reveals the exact source badges such as Vietnam Customs, National Statistics, and State Budget, official versus preliminary reporting status, and 34-province aggregation rules.

## Workflow in Customs Explorer and EcoData MCP

1. Open **Customs Explorer** or select the Commodity Market view.
2. Choose your primary dimension: Commodity, Partner Country, Province in 63-province or 34-province mode, Transport Mode, or one of the five Commodity Market analytical tabs.
3. Filter by monthly, quarterly, or annual reporting period and official or preliminary status.
4. Inspect data tables, interactive charts, and collapsible source footnotes.
5. Export CSV or Excel workbooks with metadata, or query programmatically via MCP tools `get_customs_trade_data`, `get_customs_commodity_trade`, `get_customs_country_trade`, and `get_customs_province_trade`.
