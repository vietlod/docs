# Login, Filter Indicators, Preview, and Export Data

This is the standard workflow for creating a research dataset from EcoData. The goal is to avoid downloading overly broad data, missing metadata, or selecting indicators that do not match the research unit.

## Step 1. Sign In and Select a Module

1. Open the EcoData application at `https://ecodata.io.vn`.
2. Sign in with your account.
3. Check your account permissions: free accounts can preview or export sample data; paid accounts have full-export permissions.
4. Go to the right module: Dashboard for global indicators, Vietnam Statistics for Vietnam National Statistics with the 63-province and 34-province toggle, Customs for 11-form trade data, Commodity Market for 5 strategic commodity sectors, Survey and VHLSS for provincial governance and household microdata, or Stock Hub for listed firms.

:::tip
If you are unsure which source to use, open AI Chat or connect to EcoData MCP and describe your research question first. Example: "I want to study the effect of FDI on provincial GRDP growth in Vietnam from 2005 to 2025 under the new 34-province regime. Which indicators should I select?"
:::

## Step 2. Filter and Select Indicators

In Dashboard, filter indicators by:

| Filter | When to use it |
| --- | --- |
| Keyword | When you know an indicator name, short code, topic, or term such as GDP, inflation, trade, or FDI. |
| Category | When browsing by macro, finance, labor, trade, population, or governance topics. |
| Source | When limiting results to World Bank, IMF, OECD, ADB, National Statistics, State Budget, Customs, or another source. |
| Frequency | When your model needs annual, quarterly, or monthly data. |
| Year range | When you need enough observations for a specific research period. |
| Completeness | When you want indicators with fewer missing values. |
| Favorite | When saving indicators you often reuse across projects. |

Open each indicator card to inspect definition, coverage, frequency, completeness, academic citation, and related indicators. Select an indicator only after the metadata fits your research design.

## Step 3. Preview Data

Preview helps verify data quality before export. Check:

1. Whether the data covers the correct countries, provinces, tickers, commodities, or other entities.
2. Whether Vietnam provincial data is set to `63 Provinces` or `34 Provinces` to match your analytical regime.
3. Whether the start and end years are sufficient for the model, especially across the 1995–2003, 2003–2008, and 2025 boundary milestones.
4. Whether measurement units are consistent, such as current USD, constant USD, percent, index, or billion VND.
5. Whether important years or entities have too many missing values.
6. Whether metadata includes source, citation, methodology, and technical notes.

## Step 4. Run Supporting Tools

Before the final download, users can use:

| Tool | Role |
| --- | --- |
| AI Chat and EcoData MCP | Suggest indicators, check dependent and explanatory variables, and propose alternatives when coverage is incomplete. |
| Econometric Analysis | Run preliminary estimations across 105 models in 12 econometric families including OLS, WLS, GLS, FE, RE, System GMM, DiD, VAR, and ARIMA. |
| Indicator Details | Read metadata, academic citation, related indicators, and quality notes. |
| Export Preview | Inspect sample rows and table structure before downloading. |

## Step 5. Export Data

Before exporting, define:

1. Selected indicators.
2. Countries, country groups, 63 or 34 provinces, firms, commodities, or other entities.
3. Research year range.
4. Output file format and table structure.
5. Metadata sheet and reproducible Stata, R, and Python script bundle options.

## Pre-Download Checklist

- The research question defines dependent variables, explanatory variables, and unit of analysis.
- Indicators have sufficient temporal and spatial coverage.
- The `63 Provinces` or `34 Provinces` boundary mode is selected consistently.
- Metadata has been reviewed and saved.
- Preview shows no unit, geography, or missing-data issue.
- If quantitative modeling is planned, the indicator set has been checked in Econometric Analysis or AI Chat.
