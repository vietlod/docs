## Three Historical Administrative Boundary Milestones of Vietnam From 1995 to 2026

Constructing long-horizon provincial panel datasets in Vietnam from 1995 to 2026 requires rigorous treatment of three major administrative boundary reforms. Naive summation or unadjusted provincial splits introduce severe structural breaks into panel regressions. EcoData resolves all three milestones through a dual-layer architecture: preserving original 63-province tables intact while maintaining the harmonized 34-province series table `vn_province_34_series` and mapping dictionary `vn_admin_units_34`.

### Overview of the Three Administrative Boundary Milestones

| Historical Period | Legal Basis and Boundary Change | Harmonization Methodology in EcoData |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1995–2003 Milestone** | **Resolution 22/2003/QH11**, effective January 1, 2004, split three provinces: Lai Chau into Lai Chau and Dien Bien; Dak Lak into Dak Lak and Dak Nong; Can Tho into Can Tho City and Hau Giang Province. | In **63-province mode**, EcoData backcasts 1995–2003 series using empirical average share weights from 2004–2006 for each indicator to eliminate pre-2004 missing values. In **34-province mode**, Can Tho, Hau Giang, and Soc Trang belong to the same consolidated Can Tho City cluster, allowing exact summation of pre-2004 historical values with zero allocation error; Dien Bien and Lai Chau remain two independent units in the 34-province catalog and retain empirical 2004–2006 share weights for pre-2004 backcasting. |
| **2003–2008 Milestone** | **Resolution 15/2008/QH12**, effective August 1, 2008, merged all of Ha Tay Province, Me Linh District from Vinh Phuc Province, and four communes of Luong Son District from Hoa Binh Province into Hanoi Capital City. | EcoData automatically detects official statistical series already backcasted for expanded Hanoi prior to 2008 and preserves those official figures to prevent double-counting old Hanoi and Ha Tay. Where pre-2008 tables report Ha Tay separately, EcoData aggregates full Ha Tay figures into Hanoi and attaches district-level boundary adjustment flags for Me Linh and Luong Son in series metadata. |
| **2025–2026 Milestone** | **Resolution 202/2025/QH15**, effective June 12, 2025 and operational July 1, 2025, consolidated 63 provinces and municipalities into **34 provinces and cities** comprising 6 centrally governed cities and 28 provinces, with 11 unchanged units and 23 consolidated units formed from 2 to 3 former provinces. | Preserves 100% of original 63-province tables and reconstructs 2000–2026 historical series for all 34 provinces using five variable-specific econometric rules. Reconstructed series are cross-checked against NSO Appendices `PLV01..PLV14` and 2025–2026 Socio-Economic Reports, achieving **99.5% or higher reconciliation accuracy** across area, population, GRDP, and state budget revenue. |

### Five Mathematical Aggregation Rules From 63 Provinces to 34 Provinces

EcoData classifies every provincial indicator into one of five mathematical aggregation rules based on its economic nature:

1. **Additive Stock and Flow Summation `sum`:** Applied to extensive scale indicators including land area, average population, labor force, nominal GRDP, constant-price GRDP, total state budget revenue and expenditure, realized investment capital, and merchandise trade value:
   $$Y_{P,t} = \sum_{i \in P} Y_{i,t}$$
2. **Denominator-Weighted Average `weighted_mean`:** Applied to intensive ratios, densities, and per-capita indicators including GRDP per capita, average monthly income, population density, poverty rate, unemployment rate, and urbanization rate, using economically appropriate weights $W_{i,t}$ such as population, land area, or labor force:
   $$R_{P,t} = \frac{\sum_{i \in P} R_{i,t} \cdot W_{i,t}}{\sum_{i \in P} W_{i,t}}$$
3. **Constant-Price Scale Growth Recomputation `growth_constant_scale`:** Never averages provincial growth rates directly; EcoData sums real constant-price output across constituent provinces in period $t$ and period $t-1$ before recomputing the true cluster growth rate:
   $$g_{P,t} = \left( \frac{\sum_{i \in P} Q_{i,t}}{\sum_{i \in P} Q_{i,t-1}} - 1 \right) \times 100$$
4. **Economic-Scale Weighted Index Chaining `index_chain`:** Applied to price and volume indices such as CPI and IIP when underlying base-year volumes are unavailable, weighting constituent provincial indices by retail sales of goods and services or industrial value added.
5. **Non-Aggregable Institutional Observation Preservation `non_aggregable`:** Applied to pre-July 2025 institutional perception surveys such as PCI, PAPI, PAR Index, and SIPAS as well as VHLSS household microdata to prevent ecological fallacy, while providing 34-province crosswalk keys for robustness checks.

### Switching Between 63-Province and 34-Province Modes in the UI and MCP

- **In the Web Application:** Use the **[63 Provinces | 34 Provinces]** toggle in the header of GSO Explorer, Customs Explorer, and Survey Explorer. Selecting a consolidated province such as **Hai Phong City**, **Ninh Binh**, or **Ho Chi Minh City** displays a constituent badge listing the merged historical provinces.
- **In EcoData MCP and API:** Pass `boundary_mode="34"` or `boundary_mode="63"` to `get_gso_data`, `get_yearbook_data`, `get_customs_province_trade`, and `query_macro_data_unified`.

  D --> E["5. Execute in parallel across Python, R, and Stata 18 MP"]
  E --> F["6. Audit Gauss-Markov assumptions via Jev and export replication code"]

### Five Golden Principles for Indicator Selection

1. **Clarify Unit of Analysis and Boundary Mode:** Match your research question to country, 63-province or 34-province regime, firm, household, or individual level before downloading.
2. **Audit Data Coverage and Source Footnotes:** Examine missing value ratios across panels and inspect the collapsible Primary Data Source Footnotes before merging datasets.
3. **Scrutinize Metadata Definitions:** Confirm measurement units such as current USD, constant USD, billion VND, or percentages alongside the applied aggregation rule `sum`, `weighted_mean`, or `growth_constant_scale`.
4. **Perform Robustness Checks:** Compare estimation results across specifications and between 63-province and 34-province modes to ensure conclusions remain invariant to administrative consolidation.
5. **Preserve Scientific Replication Bundles:** Retain generated `.do`, `.R`, and `.py` scripts alongside raw data pulls to guarantee 100% scientific reproducibility.
