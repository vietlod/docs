# Step-by-Step Guide: Input Resource Auditing, Custom Survey Simulation, and AI Skill Integration

When you attach or describe a **reference paper**, **thesis/dissertation outline**, **work-in-progress manuscript**, **empirical dataset (`.csv`, `.xlsx`)**, **public article URL**, or **custom survey questionnaire** in your initial prompt, the **EcoData MCP × EcoLab** ecosystem automatically runs a **4-Layer Resource Audit** to detect inconsistencies, standardize the research context, and lock all inputs for downstream econometric estimation.

---

## Step 1: Prepare and Attach Your Initial Research Resources

You can provide one or more of the following resources directly in your opening prompt on **Gemini Connected Apps** or AI research assistants:

| Attached Resource Type | What You Provide | Automated Standardization Outcome |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Research Outline / Draft / Reference Paper** | Research objectives, research questions `RQ1..RQk`, hypotheses `H1..Hk`, and proposed model | Audits alignment across objectives, questions, and hypotheses, identifies missing controls, and locks the research question ledger. |
| **2. Empirical Dataset (`.csv`, `.xlsx`)** | Your actual dataset or an EcoData dataset identifier | Activates the **Empirical Dataset Lock**, preserving 100% of your real observations and variable names without substituting simulated data. |
| **3. Custom Likert Survey Questionnaire** | Construct names, indicator codes, item wordings, and Likert scale points (5-point or 7-point) | Locks your exact construct structure so simulation and psychometric testing match every indicator and reverse-coded item. |
| **4. Public Scholarly Article URL** | Public DOI link or open-access article URL | Extracts methodological specifications, baseline models, and verified bibliographic metadata. |

---

## Step 2: Choose Your Survey Questionnaire Lock Mode

When working with behavioral or survey questionnaires, you can choose between **two questionnaire handling modes**:

1. **100% Strict Questionnaire Lock (`Strict Questionnaire Lock`)**:
   - Preserves your exact construct codes, item counts, and question wording 1:1.
   - Never adds or removes any survey indicator.
2. **Guided Psychometric Standardization**:
   - Retains all of your custom constructs while optionally recommending a reverse-coded attention check item or supplemental indicator if a construct has fewer than 3 items.

---

## Step 3: Personalize Your Academic Writing Style

During context standardization, you can select one of **four academic style profiles** for the final manuscript:

- **International Journal Article (Q1–Q3 IMRaD 6-Section Format):** Concise academic prose emphasizing causal identification, multi-model robustness checks, and theoretical contributions.
- **Master's Thesis / Doctoral Dissertation (3–5 Chapters):** Comprehensive exposition covering theoretical foundations, scale development, pilot and main survey stages, and managerial implications.
- **Replication & Extension Study:** Direct side-by-side comparison between your newly estimated coefficients and the benchmark findings in your attached reference paper.
- **Executive Policy & Analytical Report:** Focuses on economic magnitudes, sectoral sensitivity, and actionable policy roadmaps.

## Step 4: Connect the 4-Skill Suite on Gemini Connected Apps & AI Agents

To enable seamless end-to-end orchestration, download the four zero-overlap skill files from the **AI Integration Hub** on `https://ecodata.io.vn`:

1. **Skill 1 — Data & Econometric Orchestration (`gemini-ecodata-mcp-skill.md`):** Handles initial resource auditing, macroeconomic and corporate panel assembly, and estimation across 201+ econometric specifications.
2. **Skill 2 — Behavioral & Survey Research Studio (`behavioral-research-ecodata-skill.md`):** Handles Likert questionnaire locking, latent-variable survey simulation, and full EFA, CFA, SEM, and PLS-SEM diagnostics.
3. **Skill 3 — Manuscript Authoring & 9-Gate Verification (`gemini-ecolab-ecodata-mcp-skill.md`):** Authors complete manuscripts 100% locked to executed results, embeds 300-DPI structural diagrams, builds bidirectional citation hyperlinks, and exports Word, PDF, and HTML packages.
4. **Skill 4 — VHLSS 2004–2024 Microdata & Complex Survey Weights (`vhlss-micro-ecodata-skill.md`):** Governs cross-wave VHLSS questionnaire harmonization, `pweight` (`wt45`/`wt9`) survey weighting, DuMouchel–Duncan weight-exogeneity testing, and five declarative primitives on `analyze_vhlss_microdata`.

## Step 5: Ready-to-Use Step-by-Step Prompt Template

Copy the template below and paste it alongside your outline or questionnaire in Gemini:

```text
I am attaching my research outline and survey questionnaire with 4 constructs (Transformational Leadership TL: 4 items, Psychological Empowerment PE: 4 items, Innovation Climate IC: 4 items, Innovative Work Behavior IWB: 4 items, 5-point Likert scale, N = 400).
Please execute the following steps:
1. Audit the input resources, standardize research questions and hypotheses H1–H3, and strictly lock 100% of my custom survey constructs.
2. Generate the survey dataset matching my locked questionnaire and run full Cronbach's Alpha, EFA, CFA, HTMT, VIF, and PLS-SEM structural estimation alongside robust competing models.
3. Produce the complete academic report embedding all 3 300-DPI structural & measurement diagrams in Sections 3 and 4, bidirectional citation hyperlinks to the References section, and R, Stata, and Python replication scripts.
```
