# Using AI Chat, the 37-Tool EcoData MCP Server, and AI Agent Skills

EcoData provides two complementary pathways for AI-assisted economic research: using the built-in **AI Chat** workspace directly in your browser, or connecting the production **EcoData MCP Server** at `https://ecodata.io.vn/mcp` with **37 specialized tools** to external AI assistants and coding IDEs including Gemini Spark, Gemini Gems, AntiGravity, Claude Code, OpenAI Codex, and Cursor IDE.

## When to Use AI Chat and EcoData MCP

| Research Scenario | How to Prompt Your AI Assistant | Automatically Invoked MCP Tools |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Unsure which data sources or variables to select | Describe your research question, national or provincial unit of analysis, and target period. | `analyze_request`, `search_macro_indicators` |
| Need Vietnam provincial data under 34-province or 63-province boundaries | Request GRDP, Ministry of Finance state budget revenue, or public investment with `boundary_mode="34"` or `"63"`. | `get_gso_data`, `get_yearbook_data`, `query_macro_data_unified` |
| Need merchandise trade and five-sector commodity intelligence | Specify the commodity, HS code, partner market, or trading province. | `get_customs_commodity_trade`, `get_customs_country_trade`, `get_customs_province_trade` |
| Need 30-year financial statements across VN30, HNX30, or sector baskets | Request constituent tickers for the basket and batch-extract balance sheets, income statements, or ratios. | `list_stock_groups`, `get_batch_financial_data`, `get_financial_ratios` |
| Need an end-to-end econometric study | State the dependent and explanatory variables and request a one-click study with Stata, R, and Python code. | `run_complete_econometric_study`, `generate_econometric_code` |

## Actionable Prompt Examples for AI Chat and Gemini Spark

```text
@EcoData Analyze the impact of public investment and state budget revenue from the State Budget subsystem on GRDP growth across Vietnam's 34 provinces from 2020 to 2025 using boundary_mode="34". Execute the one-click study via run_complete_econometric_study and generate Stata 18 MP replication code.
```

```text
@EcoData Retrieve coffee and rice export series from the Commodity Market and Vietnam Customs subsystems from 2018 to 2025 segmented by top partner countries, and evaluate export unit price trends.
```

```text
@EcoData Fetch the VN30 constituent tickers via list_stock_groups, batch-extract ROE, ROA, and net profit margin from 2015 to 2025 using get_batch_financial_data, and estimate a Fixed Effects panel model.
```

## Integrated Workflow Across AI Chat, Dashboard, and Econometrics

1. Submit your research question to **AI Chat** or an AI assistant equipped with an **EcoData MCP Skill** to identify candidate indicators and choose between 34-province and 63-province boundary modes.
2. Inspect metadata definitions, sample coverage, and expand the **Primary Data Source Footnotes** panel in the UI to verify official source tables from the National Statistics Office, Ministry of Finance, and Vietnam Customs.
3. Execute preliminary estimations and diagnostic batteries through the **Econometrics** engine or `run_complete_econometric_study`.
4. Review outputs audited by the three-tier **Jev Co-Processor** and download the complete Stata, R, and Python replication bundle alongside cleaned data.
