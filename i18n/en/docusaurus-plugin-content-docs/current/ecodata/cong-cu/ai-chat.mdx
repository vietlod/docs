# Step-by-Step Guide to AI Chat, EcoData MCP & the 3-Skill Suite

EcoData and EcoLab enable you to conduct end-to-end economic, financial, and behavioral research directly in your browser or through your preferred AI assistant (Google Gemini, Claude, Cursor, AntiGravity, OpenAI Codex) via a single connection to `https://ecodata.io.vn/mcp`.

---

## 2. Step-by-Step Workflow from Attached Resources to Publication-Ready Report

### Step 1 — Connect and Attach the 3-Skill Suite
1. Open **Connected Apps (`/account/connect-apps`)** on EcoData or EcoLab to copy your personal connection key.
2. Connect `https://ecodata.io.vn/mcp` in your AI assistant and attach **Skill 1, Skill 2, and Skill 3** to your assistant's Knowledge or Instructions panel.

### Step 2 — Submit Your Prompt with Reference Papers, Outlines, Questionnaires, or Data
In your initial prompt, you can attach or paste:
- **A seed paper or research proposal:** Your AI assistant automatically extracts and locks your research questions (`RQ1..RQk`) and hypotheses (`H1..Hk`) while auditing methodological alignment.
- **Your own survey questionnaire:** Specify if you require **strict 100% preservation of your questionnaire items** so the simulation and psychometric tests run on your exact indicators without adding or dropping items.
- **An empirical dataset (`.csv` / `.xlsx`):** The system locks estimation strictly to your real dataset and runs the full diagnostic battery.

### Step 3 — Inspect Structural Diagrams, One-Way Citations & Export Your Manuscript
1. For factor and structural equation studies (`EFA / CFA / SEM / PLS-SEM`), inspect the three automated 300-DPI diagrams embedded once per section:
   - **`Figure 3.1: Diagram M1`**: Measurement and conceptual model with concise indicator range boxes (`ENV1–ENV4`).
   - **`Figure 3.2: Diagram M2`**: Side-by-side comparison of the Baseline Model (`Panel A`) and Extended Alternative Model (`Panel B`).
   - **`Figure 4.1: Diagram R1`**: Estimated structural path diagram displaying standardized coefficients and significance stars directly on arrows.
2. Click any color-highlighted in-text citation to smooth-scroll one-way to the matching entry in **Section 6. References** and verify its clickable DOI source.
3. Download the publication-ready **Word `.docx`** manuscript along with the `.csv` dataset and `R · Stata · Python` replication scripts.
