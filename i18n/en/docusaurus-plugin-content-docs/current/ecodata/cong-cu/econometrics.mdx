# Econometrics Engine Across 12 Model Families and 105 Estimators

EcoData's integrated Econometrics Engine enables sample preparation, parameter estimation, diagnostic testing, and replication script generation directly in your browser or through the EcoData MCP Server.

## Architecture of 12 Model Families, 40 Sub-Models, and 105 Estimators

EcoData provides a comprehensive econometric suite designed for academic publishing and policy evaluation:

### Twelve Core Model Families

1. **Classical Linear Regression:** OLS, WLS, GLS, and Total Least Squares TLS.
2. **Regularized Regression:** Ridge, Lasso, Elastic Net, and Adaptive Lasso.
3. **Linear Panel Data:** Pooled OLS, Entity and Two-Way Fixed Effects, Random Effects, and Between Effects.
4. **Dynamic Panel Data:** Arellano-Bond Difference GMM and Blundell-Bond System GMM.
5. **Discrete Choice and Limited Dependent Variables:** Binary Logit, Binary Probit, Multinomial Logit, Ordered Probit, Censored Tobit, Truncated Regression, and Heckman two-step selection.
6. **Count Data Models:** Poisson Regression, Negative Binomial, Zero-Inflated Poisson ZIP, and Zero-Inflated Negative Binomial ZINB.
7. **Quantile Regression:** Linear Quantile Regression and Fixed-Effects Panel Quantile Regression.
8. **Univariate Time Series and Conditional Volatility:** AR, MA, ARMA, ARIMA, SARIMAX, ARDL, ARCH, GARCH, and EGARCH.
9. **Multivariate Time Series:** Vector Autoregression VAR, Vector Error Correction VECM, Structural VAR, Granger causality tests, and Impulse Response Functions IRF.
10. **Instrumental Variables and Simultaneous Equations:** Two-Stage Least Squares 2SLS, Three-Stage Least Squares 3SLS, and Seemingly Unrelated Regressions SUR.
11. **Non-Linear, Semi-Parametric, and Bayesian Machine Learning:** Non-Linear Least Squares NLS, Generalized Additive Models GAM, Bayesian MCMC, and Causal Forest.
12. **Causal Inference and Spatial Econometrics:** Difference-in-Differences DID, Event Study, Propensity Score Matching PSM, Regression Discontinuity Design RDD, and spatial models SAR, SEM, and Spatial Durbin SDM.

### Breakdown of 40 Sub-Models and 105 Estimators

The engine exposes **40 mathematical sub-models** and **105 distinct estimators** parameterized across:
- **Optimization Methods:** OLS, FGLS, Maximum Likelihood MLE, Quasi-MLE, and one-step or two-step GMM with Windmeijer finite-sample correction.
- **Variance-Covariance Standard Error Structures:** Classical homoskedastic, White heteroskedasticity-robust HC0, HC1, HC2, HC3, Newey-West HAC, and one-way or two-way clustered standard errors by province, firm, or time.

## Estimation Workflow in the Web App and MCP

1. Open the **Econometrics** module or invoke the MCP tool `run_complete_econometric_study`.
2. Select a model family and specific estimator.
3. Choose the dependent variable and explanatory variables from the indicator catalog or 34-province, 63-province, or firm panels.
4. Configure the sample period and log, difference, or lag transformations.
5. Execute the model in parallel across **Python, R, and Stata 18 MP**.
6. Inspect parameter estimates, standard errors, $t$-statistics, $p$-values, diagnostic tests including $VIF$, Breusch-Pagan, Wooldridge, Hausman, and Hansen $J$, and export executable `.do`, `.R`, and `.py` replication scripts.

## Academic Table Formatting and Citation Styles

Regression output tables switch instantaneously across five international publication standards: **APA 7th**, **Chicago 17th**, **Harvard**, **IEEE**, and **MLA 9th**: while automatically rendering three-tier LaTeX mathematical variable notation.
