## Personal Data Protection Policy Under Law 91/2025/QH15 and Decree 356/2025/ND-CP

The TNSAI ecosystem processes personal data in full compliance with Personal Data Protection Law `91/2025/QH15` and Decree `356/2025/ND-CP` effective January 1, 2026.

### Purpose and Scope of Data Collection

The platform collects only the minimum personal data necessary for explicitly stated purposes:

- **Basic Personal Data**: Full name, email address, telephone number, institutional affiliation, billing address, and single sign-on account identifiers.
- **Sensitive and Financial Data**: Payment transaction records, bank transfer reconciliation references, and uploaded research documents when users actively invoke analytical workflows.

### Principles of Valid Consent

Pursuant to Article 9 of Law `91/2025/QH15` and Article 6 of Decree `356/2025/ND-CP`:

- User consent is established voluntarily with separate checkboxes for each distinct processing purpose and without bundling non-essential processing as a condition of service.
- All registration, authentication, and newsletter forms use unchecked checkboxes by default.
- Silence or non-response from a data subject is never treated as valid consent.

### Data Subject Rights and Statutory Processing Timelines

Under Article 8 of Law `91/2025/QH15` and Article 5 of Decree `356/2025/ND-CP`, users may exercise self-service data rights at [https://tnsai.vn/dashboard/privacy](https://tnsai.vn/dashboard/privacy) under strict statutory timelines:

- **Request Acknowledgment**: Within `2 business days` from receipt of a valid request.
- **Consent Withdrawal or Processing Restriction**: Completed within `10 to 15 days`.
- **Personal Data Export and Access**: Completed within `10 to 15 days`.
- **Account and Personal Data Deletion**: Completed within `20 to 30 days` with automated signed webhook propagation across all connected ecosystem applications.

### Cross-Border Data Transfer and Data Localization in Vietnam

Certain quantitative synthesis and artificial intelligence features utilize cloud infrastructure or large language model application programming interfaces hosted outside Vietnam. The operating entity maintains a Cross-Border Personal Data Transfer Impact Assessment Dossier pursuant to Article 20 of Law `91/2025/QH15` and Articles 17 through 21 of Decree `356/2025/ND-CP`. Core identity records and system logs are stored on servers located in Vietnam pursuant to Article 24 of Cybersecurity Law `116/2025/QH15`.

## Terms of Service and E-Commerce Compliance Under Law 122/2025/QH15

Digital subscriptions, datasets, and educational publications are governed by E-Commerce Law `122/2025/QH15`, Decree `248/2026/ND-CP`, and Law on Protection of Consumer Rights `19/2023/QH15`:

- **Transparent Total Pricing**: All displayed subscription and publication prices include applicable value-added taxes and transaction fees.
- **Pre-Checkout Contract Review**: Customers review complete plan specifications, billing duration, and total payable amount before confirming payment.
- **Refund and Cancellation Policy**: Users may cancel recurring subscriptions at any time and request a full refund within `7 days` if an unresolvable platform defect prevents access.

## Cookie Policy and Online Tracking Governance

Pursuant to Article 28 of Personal Data Protection Law `91/2025/QH15`:

- **Essential Cookies**: Maintain authenticated single sign-on sessions, cross-site request forgery protection, and interface language preferences.
- **Analytics and Marketing Cookies**: Disabled by default upon initial visit and loaded only after the user explicitly clicks accept on the Cookie Consent Banner. Users may update preferences at any time from the footer.

## Artificial Intelligence Transparency Disclosure Under Law 134/2025/QH15

Pursuant to Article 11 of Law on Artificial Intelligence `134/2025/QH15` and Article 18 of Decree `142/2026/ND-CP`:

- **Clear AI Interaction Identification**: All virtual assistants, academic copilot widgets, and automated analytical agents display clear AI identification badges so users know they are interacting with an artificial intelligence system.
- **AI Content Labeling and Machine-Readable Metadata**: Automated research manuscripts, synthesized articles, documents, images, audio, and video outputs generated by AI systems carry visible disclosure labels and embedded machine-readable metadata.
- **Professional Non-Advisory Disclaimer**: Quantitative outputs, financial metrics, and AI-assisted insights are provided strictly for academic and informational reference and do not constitute securities investment advice, financial advisory services, or formal legal opinions.

## Content Violation Reporting and Child Data Protection

- **24-Hour Content Takedown Mechanism**: Under Article 25 of Cybersecurity Law `116/2025/QH15` and Article 17 of E-Commerce Law `122/2025/QH15`, copyright, privacy, or unlawful content reports submitted via [https://tnsai.vn/legal/compliance](https://tnsai.vn/legal/compliance) are processed within `24 hours`, or within `6 hours` for emergency national security notifications.
- **Child Personal Data Protection and Playtime Safeguards**: Under Article 24 of Law `91/2025/QH15` and Articles 38 and 39 of Decree `147/2024/ND-CP`, educational games for children require verifiable parental or guardian consent for users under `16 years of age` and enforce playtime alerts at `60 minutes` per session and `180 minutes` per day.
