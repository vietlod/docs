## Personal Data Protection Policy Under Law 91/2025/QH15 and Decree 356/2025/NĐ-CP

The TNSAI ecosystem processes personal data in full compliance with Personal Data Protection Law `91/2025/QH15` and Decree `356/2025/NĐ-CP` effective `01/01/2026`.

### Scope and Purpose of Personal Data Collection

The platform collects only the minimum fields required for registered processing purposes:

- **Basic Personal Data**: Full name, email address, telephone number, institutional affiliation, billing address, and single sign-on account identifier.
- **Sensitive and Financial Personal Data**: Payment transaction records, bank transfer reconciliation references, and uploaded academic files when users actively initiate quantitative analysis workflows.

### Principles of Valid Consent

Under Article 9 of Law `91/2025/QH15` and Article 6 of Decree `356/2025/NĐ-CP`:

- Consent is established voluntarily and separately for each data processing purpose without bundling non-essential processing as a condition of service.
- All registration, authentication, and subscription forms use unchecked opt-in checkboxes by default.
- Silence or non-response is never treated as valid consent.

### Data Subject Rights and Statutory Response Timelines

Under Article 8 of Law `91/2025/QH15` and Article 5 of Decree `356/2025/NĐ-CP`, users can exercise self-service privacy rights at [https://tnsai.vn/dashboard/privacy](https://tnsai.vn/dashboard/privacy) under binding statutory timelines:

- **Request Acknowledgment**: Within `02 working days` of receiving a valid request.
- **Consent Withdrawal or Processing Restriction**: Completed within `10 to 15 days`.
- **Personal Data Copy and Export**: Completed within `10 to 15 days`.
- **Personal Data Erasure and Account Closure**: Completed within `20 to 30 days` with automated synchronization across all connected applications via signed security webhooks.

### Cross-Border Data Transfer and Domestic Storage

Certain academic analysis features and AI assistants utilize cloud infrastructure or large language model application programming interfaces hosted outside Vietnam. The operator maintains a Cross-Border Data Transfer Impact Assessment Dossier under Article 20 of Law `91/2025/QH15` and Articles 17 through 21 of Decree `356/2025/NĐ-CP`. Core identity databases and security audit logs are stored on servers located in Vietnam under Article 24 of Cybersecurity Law `116/2025/QH15`.

## Electronic Commerce Terms Under Law 122/2025/QH15

Digital services, academic publications, and software subscriptions comply with E-Commerce Law `122/2025/QH15`, Decree `248/2026/NĐ-CP`, and Consumer Protection Law `19/2023/QH15`:

- **Total Price Transparency**: All prices displayed on pricing and checkout pages represent the final total payable amount inclusive of value-added tax and transaction processing fees.
- **Pre-Purchase Contract Review**: Buyers review complete subscription specifications, billing duration, and total payable amount before confirming payment.
- **Cancellation and Refund Policy**: Users may cancel subscription auto-renewal at any time and request a refund within `07 days` in the event of an unresolvable platform technical failure.

## Cookie Policy and Online Tracking Controls

Under Article 28 of Personal Data Protection Law `91/2025/QH15`:

- **Essential Cookies**: Maintain secure authentication sessions, cross-site request forgery protection, and interface language preferences.
- **Analytics and Marketing Cookies**: Disabled by default on initial visit and loaded only after explicit opt-in consent via the Cookie Preference Banner.

## Artificial Intelligence Transparency Under Law 134/2025/QH15

In accordance with Article 11 of Artificial Intelligence Law `134/2025/QH15` and Article 18 of Decree `142/2026/NĐ-CP`:

- **Direct AI Interaction Disclosure**: All virtual assistants, academic query interfaces, and automated analysis agents display a clear AI system badge informing users of artificial intelligence interaction.
- **AI Content Labeling and Machine-Readable Metadata**: Automated research reports, synthesized articles, documents, images, audio, and video generated by AI systems carry visible disclosure labels and embedded digital identification metadata.
- **Professional Disclaimer**: Quantitative outputs, financial indicators, and AI-generated insights are provided strictly for academic and research reference and do not constitute securities investment advice, financial advisory services, or formal legal opinions.

## Content Compliance Reporting and Child Data Protection

- **Notice and Takedown Within 24 Hours**: Under Article 25 of Cybersecurity Law `116/2025/QH15` and Article 17 of E-Commerce Law `122/2025/QH15`, copyright, privacy, or unlawful content reports submitted via [https://tnsai.vn/legal/compliance](https://tnsai.vn/legal/compliance) are processed within `24 hours`, or within `06 hours` for urgent national security matters.
- **Child Data Protection on Educational Platforms**: Under Article 24 of Law `91/2025/QH15` and Articles 38 and 39 of Decree `147/2024/NĐ-CP`, educational applications for students require parental or guardian consent verification for users under `16 years of age` and enforce usage alerts at `60 minutes` per session and `180 minutes` per day.
