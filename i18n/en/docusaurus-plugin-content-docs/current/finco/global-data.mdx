# Exploiting Global Macroeconomic Data Across 9 International Institutions

The **Global Data** module on [Finco](https://www.finco.vn) curates **347 active macroeconomic indicators** comprising **421,301 empirical observations** harmonized by ISO3 country codes across **9 premier international organizations**.

<VideoTutorial title="Retrieving 347 Global Macro Indicators from 9 International Organizations on Finco" src="https://youtu.be/FtiqlHQ7054?list=PLYrLDn3rIFnesSAJW8uy9VAPq-j6IVKO3" opened={false} />

## Directory of 9 International Organizations Synchronized on Finco

| Institution Code | Organization Name | Core Empirical Domain | ISO3 Panel Harmonization |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :---: |
| **WB** | World Bank | GDP growth, capital formation, poverty reduction, WGI governance, and development | <Available /> |
| **IMF** | International Monetary Fund | Balance of payments, foreign reserves, inflation, exchange rates, and IFS/WEO series | <Available /> |
| **ADB** | Asian Development Bank | Key indicators for Asia and the Pacific, regional infrastructure, and public finance | <Available /> |
| **WTO** | World Trade Organization | MFN tariff profiles, merchandise and commercial services trade, and non-tariff measures | <Available /> |
| **ILO** | International Labour Organization — ILOSTAT | Labor force participation, unemployment rates, labor productivity, and real wages | <Available /> |
| **OECD** | Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development | R&D intensity, taxation structures, innovation patents, and digital economy metrics | <Available /> |
| **UN** | United Nations Statistical Division | Demographic transitions, urbanization, Human Development Index HDI, and SDG indicators | <Available /> |
| **FAO** | Food and Agriculture Organization — FAOSTAT | Agricultural production, food security balances, land use, and agri-environmental emissions | <Available /> |
| **OWID** | Our World in Data — University of Oxford | Renewable energy transition, per-capita CO2 emissions, and climate change series | <Available /> |

## Step-by-Step Workflow for Constructing Cross-Country Panel Datasets

<Steps>

### Step 1: Filter by International Institution or Macroeconomic Theme

Open the **Global Data** tab on [Finco](https://www.finco.vn) and select indicators across one or multiple international organizations to merge diverse institutional sources into a unified panel.

### Step 2: Select Country Groupings by ISO3 Code and Sample Horizon

Choose regional blocs such as ASEAN, G20, OECD, or the full universe of over 200 economies. The platform automatically aligns observations on the composite key of `iso3` and `year`.

### Step 3: Export Cross-Country Panels for Econometric Estimation

Download the 2-Sheet SPSS Excel workbook to estimate endogenous growth regressions, Environmental Kuznets Curve EKC specifications via Pooled Mean Group PMG/ARDL, Dynamic Common Correlated Effects DCCE, or Panel Quantile models guided by [VietLOD](https://vietlod.tnsai.vn) and [EcoData](https://ecodata.io.vn).

</Steps>
