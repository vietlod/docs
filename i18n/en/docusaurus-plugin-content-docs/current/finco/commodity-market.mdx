## Academic Variable Naming Standard Under 14 Characters

To guarantee seamless compatibility with **Stata 18 MP**, **SPSS**, **EViews**, **R**, and **Python**, all 3,448 commodity indicators across [Finco](https://www.finco.vn) and [EcoData](https://ecodata.io.vn) enforce a strict academic variable code convention capped at 14 characters:

- `rank_cmd_prod`: Global country ranking by physical commodity production volume.
- `rank_cmd_exp`: Global country ranking by commodity export volume or value.
- `rank_cmd_imp`: Global country ranking by commodity import volume or value.
- `cmd_spot_usd`: Standardized spot price denominated in US dollars per international unit.
- `cmd_inv_ratio`: Ending inventory stocks as a percentage of total domestic consumption and exports.

## Step-by-Step Workflow for Price Transmission and Time-Series Research

<Steps>

### Step 1: Select Target Sector Among the 5 MXV Commodity Groups

Open the **Commodity Market** module on [Finco](https://www.finco.vn) and choose Agricultural, Energy, Metals, Industrial Raw Materials, or Fertilizers and Chemicals.

### Step 2: Navigate Across the 5 Functional Analytical Tabs

Select the analytical tab matching your empirical specification: use **Overview & Prices** for volatility modeling, or **Supply, Demand & Stocks** and **Global Trade Flows** for Structural Vector Autoregression SVAR analysis.

### Step 3: Verify Primary Publisher Metadata in Variable View

Switch to **Variable View** to inspect source attribution across the 48 primary publishers including USDA, EIA, IEA, World Gold Council, ICO, IRSG, FAO, and the World Bank Commodity Markets Outlook.

### Step 4: Export Series for Econometric Modeling on VietLOD and EcoLab

Download the harmonized time-series or cross-country panel dataset to run Johansen Cointegration, Vector Error Correction VECM, or Multivariate DCC-GARCH estimations guided by [VietLOD](https://vietlod.tnsai.vn) and [EcoLab](https://ecolab.io.vn).

</Steps>
