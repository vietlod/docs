# Exploiting Stock Market Intelligence and Financial Notes

The **Stock Market** module on [Finco](https://www.finco.vn) delivers institutional-grade corporate financial statements, equity trading series, corporate governance structures, and covered warrant analytics across all enterprises listed on **HOSE**, **HNX**, and **UPCOM**. This dataset serves as the empirical foundation for corporate finance research, asset pricing studies, and panel econometrics on [EcoData](https://ecodata.io.vn) and [VietLOD](https://vietlod.tnsai.vn).

## Automated Harmonization Across Four Vietnamese Accounting Regimes

The [Finco](https://www.finco.vn) database automatically classifies and harmonizes financial statement schemas across four distinct institutional accounting frameworks in Vietnam, preventing schema mismatches when constructing multi-sector panel datasets:

| Regime Code | Institutional Sector | Regulatory Accounting Standard | Core Sector-Specific Metrics | Financial Notes TM Support |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :---: |
| **Corp** | Non-financial manufacturing, trade, and services | Circular 200/2014/TT-BTC | Net revenue, Cost of goods sold, Inventories, Fixed assets, EBITDA | <Available /> |
| **Bank** | Joint-stock and state-owned commercial banks | State Bank of Vietnam Chart of Accounts | Net interest income, NIM, Demand deposits CASA, Loan tiers N1 to N5 | <Available /> |
| **Sec** | Securities brokerages and investment banks | Circular 210/2014/TT-BTC | FVTPL financial assets, Margin lending balances, Brokerage and proprietary revenue | <Available /> |
| **Ins** | Life, non-life, and reinsurance corporations | Circular 199/2014/TT-BTC | Gross written premiums, Technical reserves, Insurance claim payouts | <Available /> |

## Deep Decoding of Financial Statement Notes TM

Unlike conventional portals that only provide headline balance sheets and income statements, [Finco](https://www.finco.vn) extracts granular **Financial Statement Notes** designated as **TM**, unlocking high-value microeconomic variables:

- **Commercial Bank Demand Deposit Ratio CASA**: Automatically aggregates current accounts and non-term deposits in VND and foreign currencies to compute exact quarterly and annual CASA ratios.
- **Five-Tier Credit Quality Breakdown N1 to N5**: Extracts Pass loans Tier 1, Special Mention loans Tier 2, Substandard loans Tier 3, Doubtful loans Tier 4, and Loss loans Tier 5 to compute non-performing loan ratios NPL and loan-loss coverage ratios LLCR.
- **Granular Debt Maturity, Fixed Asset Depreciation, and Factor Input Costs**: Enables Stochastic Frontier Analysis SFA and Total Factor Productivity TFP estimation via Olley-Pakes or Levinsohn-Petrin estimators on [EcoLab](https://ecolab.io.vn).

## Step-by-Step Workflow for Retrieving Equity and Covered Warrant Data

<Steps>

### Step 1: Select the Equity or Covered Warrant Sub-Module

Open the **Stock Market** hub on [Finco](https://www.finco.vn) and select one of six specialized sub-modules: Financial Statements, Financial Notes TM, Valuation and Financial Ratios, Foreign and Insider Trading, Corporate Governance, or Covered Warrants CW.

### Step 2: Filter by Ticker Universe, ICB Industry, and Reporting Frequency

Enter specific tickers such as `VCB`, `FPT`, `HPG`, `SSI` or select an entire ICB Level-2 or Level-3 industry group. Choose Quarterly or Annual reporting frequency across the historical timeline.

### Step 3: Verify Academic Variable Definitions in Variable View

Switch to **Variable View** to inspect exact mathematical definitions for valuation multiples including P/E, P/B, EV/EBITDA, ROE, ROA, NIM, CASA, NPL, or Black-Scholes covered warrant metrics including Implied Volatility, Delta, Effective Gearing, and Break-Even Points.

### Step 4: Export Panel Workbooks or Launch Econometric Estimation

Download the 2-Sheet SPSS Excel workbook to run Fixed Effects, Random Effects, or System GMM panel regressions following the step-by-step methodologies at [VietLOD](https://vietlod.tnsai.vn/sach/kinh-te-luong-ung-dung-toan-dien-tu-a-z).

</Steps>

* Load corporate financial panel exported from Finco
import delimited "finco_corporate_panel.csv", clear encoding("UTF-8")

* Declare panel structure by firm identifier and fiscal year
encode ticker, gen(firm_id)
xtset firm_id fiscal_year

* Estimate two-way Fixed Effects regression with firm-clustered standard errors
xtreg roe leverage firm_size tangibility revenue_growth i.fiscal_year, fe vce(cluster firm_id)

```r
library(readr)
library(plm)
library(lmtest)

# Load listed corporate panel dataset from Finco
df <- read_csv("finco_corporate_panel.csv", show_col_types = FALSE)
pdata <- pdata.frame(df, index = c("ticker", "fiscal_year"))

# Estimate two-way Fixed Effects model with clustered robust standard errors
fe_model <- plm(
  roe ~ leverage + firm_size + tangibility + revenue_growth,
  data = pdata,
  model = "within",
  effect = "twoways"
)
coeftest(fe_model, vcov = vcovHC(fe_model, type = "HC1", cluster = "group"))
```

```python
import pandas as pd
from linearmodels.panel import PanelOLS

# Load panel dataset exported from Finco and set MultiIndex
df = pd.read_csv("finco_corporate_panel.csv")
df = df.set_index(["ticker", "fiscal_year"])

# Estimate Panel OLS with EntityEffects and TimeEffects
mod = PanelOLS.from_formula(
    "roe ~ 1 + leverage + firm_size + tangibility + revenue_growth + EntityEffects + TimeEffects",
    data=df,
)
res = mod.fit(cov_type="clustered", cluster_entity=True)
print(res.summary)
```
