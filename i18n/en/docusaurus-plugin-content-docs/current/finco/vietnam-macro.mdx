# Retrieving Vietnam Macroeconomic, Customs, and 34/63-Province VHLSS Data

The **Vietnam Macro Data** hub on [Finco](https://www.finco.vn) provides authoritative state statistical series fully synchronized with the **34-province merged administrative regime**, the **63-province historical regime**, and the **6 socio-economic regions** harmonization engine of [EcoData](https://ecodata.io.vn).

## Five Authoritative Vietnam Macroeconomic and Provincial Survey Pillars

| Data Sub-System | Primary Official Publisher | Coverage & Temporal Span | 34 & 63 Province Switcher |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :---: |
| **1. NSO / GSO National Statistics** | National Statistics Office of Vietnam | 28 thematic PX-Web tables and statistical appendices `PLV01` through `PLV14` | <Available /> |
| **2. FISCAL State Budget Series** | Ministry of Finance of Vietnam | Continuous `THUCHINS` fiscal revenue and expenditure series from 1995 to 2026 | <Available /> |
| **3. CUSTOMS Merchandise Trade** | Vietnam Customs | 11 official statistical trade forms by HS commodity, partner country, and province | <Available /> |
| **4. SURVEY_INDEX Governance Indices** | VCCI, Ministry of Home Affairs, UNDP | 7 provincial indices: PCI, PAPI, PAR Index, SIPAS, DTI, PII, and PGI | <Available /> |
| **5. VHLSS Living Standards Survey** | National Statistics Office of Vietnam | Household income, expenditure, multidimensional poverty, housing, and demographics | <Available /> |

## Seamless Switching Between 63 Historical Provinces and 34 Harmonized Provinces

Administrative boundary reforms represent a critical challenge in sub-national empirical economics. On [Finco](https://www.finco.vn) and [EcoData](https://ecodata.io.vn), researchers can switch instantaneously across three spatial aggregation regimes:

- **63 Historical Provinces Mode**: Preserves legacy provincial panel identifiers for historical Fixed Effects and Difference-in-Differences policy evaluations.
- **34 Newly Harmonized Provinces Mode**: Automatically crosswalks and weight-aggregates fiscal, demographic, and VHLSS living standard indicators into Vietnam's 34 consolidated provincial jurisdictions.
- **6 Socio-Economic Regions Mode**: Aggregates observations across the Northern Midlands and Mountains, Red River Delta, North Central and Central Coastal, Central Highlands, Southeast, and Mekong River Delta regions.

## Step-by-Step Workflow for Sub-National Panel Extraction

<Steps>

### Step 1: Select the Target Sub-System Across 8 Thematic Tabs

Navigate to **Vietnam Macro Data** on [Finco](https://www.finco.vn) and select NSO PX-Web, Fiscal Budget, Socio-Economic Reports, Customs Trade, Provincial Governance Indices, or VHLSS Living Standards.

### Step 2: Configure the 34-Province, 63-Province, or 6-Region Regime

Use the spatial regime selector to choose either the 34-province consolidated structure, the 63-province historical panel, or the 6 socio-economic regions across your desired sample period.

### Step 3: Export Provincial Panels for Spatial and Policy Econometrics

Download the 2-Sheet SPSS workbook to estimate Spatial Autoregressive SAR, Spatial Error SEM, Spatial Durbin SDM, or Synthetic Control models guided by [VietLOD](https://vietlod.tnsai.vn) and [EcoLab](https://ecolab.io.vn).

</Steps>
