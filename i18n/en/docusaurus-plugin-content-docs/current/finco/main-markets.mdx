# Exploiting 19 Global Main Markets and Vietnam Domestic Benchmarks

The **19 Main Markets** module on [Finco](https://www.finco.vn) bridges the **Vietnam domestic spot market** with **18 of the world's largest trade and financial partner economies**, organized across **8 strategic regional hubs**.

## Step-by-Step Workflow for Cross-Border Spillover and Trade Analysis

<Steps>

### Step 1: Select Regional Hub and Partner Economies

Open the **19 Main Markets** tab on [Finco](https://www.finco.vn) and select the Vietnam domestic benchmark alongside target partner economies such as the United States, China, the European Union, Japan, or South Korea.

### Step 2: Evaluate Domestic Versus International Price Basis

Use the synchronized comparison grid to measure the price basis between Vietnamese provincial spot prices and international exchange benchmarks adjusted for currency movements and freight costs.

### Step 3: Export Bilateral Datasets for Gravity and Spillover Models

Export the combined bilateral dataset across [Finco](https://www.finco.vn) and [EcoData](https://ecodata.io.vn) to estimate Poisson Pseudo-Maximum Likelihood PPML trade gravity models, Global VAR GVAR, or Diebold-Yilmaz connectedness indices guided by [VietLOD](https://vietlod.tnsai.vn).

</Steps>
