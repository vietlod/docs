## Four-Step Workflow for Exploiting Finco Data in Quantitative Research

<Steps>

### Step 1: Authenticate via Single Sign-On

Navigate to [https://www.finco.vn](https://www.finco.vn) and sign in with your unified TNS AI credentials. Single Sign-On synchronizes your session across Finco, [EcoData](https://ecodata.io.vn), [EcoLab](https://ecolab.io.vn), and [VietLOD](https://vietlod.tnsai.vn).

### Step 2: Select Data Pillar and Configure Multi-Dimensional Filters

Choose one of the five primary data hubs on the top navigation bar: Stock Market, Commodity Market, 19 Main Markets, Vietnam Macro Data, or Global Data. Filter by temporal frequency, 34-province or 63-province administrative regime, and indicator group.

### Step 3: Inspect Top 80 Preview and Variable Metadata

Use the instant 80-row preview table and toggle between **Data View** and **Variable View** to verify measurement units, primary publisher citations, temporal coverage, and academic variable naming before downloading.

### Step 4: Export 2-Sheet SPSS Workbooks or Trigger StockMCP

Download the standardized 2-Sheet SPSS Excel workbook or clean CSV for estimation in Stata, R, or Python following [VietLOD](https://vietlod.tnsai.vn) methodologies, or invoke the 138-tool **StockMCP** suite to automate investment backtesting and empirical reporting on [EcoLab](https://ecolab.io.vn).

</Steps>
