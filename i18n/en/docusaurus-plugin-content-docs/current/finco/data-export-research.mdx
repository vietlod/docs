# Academic 2-Sheet SPSS Data Export and Research Pipeline Integration

All five data pillars on [Finco](https://www.finco.vn) feature an interactive data quality inspection grid and a standardized **2-Sheet SPSS Excel export architecture**, eliminating manual data wrangling before running estimations in Stata, R, Python, or SPSS, or launching automated empirical pipelines on [EcoData](https://ecodata.io.vn) and [EcoLab](https://ecolab.io.vn).

## Dual-Mode Data View and Variable View Architecture

Before downloading any dataset, the **Top 80 Observation Preview** on [Finco](https://www.finco.vn) provides two synchronized inspection modes modeled after IBM SPSS Statistics:

| Inspection Mode / Workbook Sheet | Structural Contents | Academic Research Value |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Sheet 1: Data View** | Clean rectangular observation matrix headed by concise academic variable codes capped at 14 characters, with strictly typed numeric and temporal columns | Loads directly into Stata, R, Python, SPSS, or EViews without encoding errors or decimal delimiter conflicts |
| **Sheet 2: Variable View** | Complete metadata dictionary containing `Variable Name`, bilingual `Label`, `Measurement Unit`, `Primary Publisher Source`, `Temporal Frequency`, and methodological notes | Directly populates the **Variable Definitions and Data Sources Table** required in the Methodology section of theses and journal manuscripts |

## End-to-End Research Workflow Linking Finco, EcoData, EcoLab, and VietLOD

<Steps>

### Step 1: Audit Sample Completeness in the Top 80 Preview Grid

After selecting your indicators on [Finco](https://www.finco.vn), review the first 80 observations in **Data View** to verify temporal continuity and cross-sectional balance.

### Step 2: Verify Official Source Citations in Variable View

Switch to **Variable View** to inspect official publisher attributions, measurement units, and variable harmonization rules.

### Step 3: Export the 2-Sheet SPSS Workbook or UTF-8 CSV

Click **Export 2-Sheet SPSS Excel** to generate an `.xlsx` workbook containing both the `Data_View` sheet and the `Variable_View` codebook sheet, or export a clean UTF-8 `.csv` file.

### Step 4: Execute 201 Econometric Models Across VietLOD, EcoData, and EcoLab

Import the exported dataset directly into the 201 step-by-step econometric tutorials on [VietLOD](https://vietlod.tnsai.vn), execute Tri-Engine estimations on [EcoData](https://ecodata.io.vn), or generate publication-ready empirical manuscripts on [EcoLab](https://ecolab.io.vn).

</Steps>
