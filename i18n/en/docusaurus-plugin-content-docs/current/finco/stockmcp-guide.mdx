# Mastering StockMCP Across 138 Analytics Tools and Strategy Backtesting

**StockMCP**, registered under the technical identifier `vietnam-securities`, is a **138-tool Model Context Protocol server** with two-way integration into the [Finco](https://www.finco.vn) database alongside Vietnam's three premier securities data providers: **TCBS**, **SSI**, and **DNSE**. The toolkit empowers quantitative researchers, faculty, and institutional investors to automate macroeconomic synthesis, 360-degree corporate valuation, and multi-horizon strategy backtesting in a single execution workflow.

<VideoTutorial title="Exploiting the 138-Tool StockMCP Suite and Multi-Horizon Strategy Backtesting" src="https://youtu.be/FtiqlHQ7054?list=PLYrLDn3rIFnesSAJW8uy9VAPq-j6IVKO3" opened={false} />

## Architecture of the 8 Core Tool Families Within StockMCP

The 138 tools in **StockMCP** are organized into high-level composite orchestrators and granular market data primitives:

| StockMCP Tool Family | Representative Tool Identifiers | Core Analytical & Research Function | Status |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :---: |
| **1. Session Bootstrap & Routing** | `vietnam_securities_session_bootstrap` | Initializes stateless analytical context and loads unified market metadata in a single step | <Available /> |
| **2. 4-Tier Top-Down Macro Synthesis** | `vietnam_macro_topdown_synthesis` | Synthesizes Global Macro, NSO Statistics, Ministry of Finance Fiscal series, Customs, and Provincial Surveys | <Available /> |
| **3. 360-Degree Equity Intelligence** | `vietnam_securities_stock_360` | Decodes financial statements, Financial Notes TM, P/E, P/B, ROE, CASA, NPL, and insider transactions | <Available /> |
| **4. Market Radar & Capital Flows** | `vietnam_securities_market_radar` | Tracks market breadth, sector leaders, investor classification flows, and foreign institutional net buying | <Available /> |
| **5. Opportunity & Corporate Screeners** | `vietnam_securities_opportunity_screener`, `vietnam_securities_corporate_screener` | Filters equities by value multiples, earnings growth, dividend yield, and balance-sheet resilience | <Available /> |
| **6. Covered Warrant Advisor** | `vietnam_securities_cw_advisor` | Computes Black-Scholes fair value, Delta sensitivity, effective gearing, and break-even thresholds | <Available /> |
| **7. 4-Horizon Strategy Advisor** | `vietnam_securities_strategy_advisor` | Formulates quantitative trading and investment strategies across 3-month, 6-month, 12-month, and 3-year horizons | <Available /> |
| **8. Quantitative Quick Backtest** | `vietnam_securities_quick_backtest` | Executes historical backtests reporting CAGR, Sharpe Ratio, Sortino Ratio, Maximum Drawdown, and Win Rate | <Available /> |

## The Four-Tier Top-Down Investment and Research Framework

When combining **StockMCP** with [Finco](https://www.finco.vn), researchers and analysts execute a disciplined four-tier deductive workflow:

1. **Tier 1 — Global and Domestic Macroeconomic Cycle**: Invoke `global_macro_world_query`, `vietnam_macro_gso_nso_query`, `vietnam_macro_fiscal_mof_query`, and `vietnam_macro_customs_trade_query` to evaluate interest rate regimes, exchange rate dynamics, public investment disbursement, and trade balances.
2. **Tier 2 — Sector Rotation and Institutional Flow Radar**: Invoke `vietnam_securities_market_radar`, `tcbs_getIndustryIndex`, and `tcbs_getMarketForeignVal` to identify industry groups attracting institutional and foreign capital inflows.
3. **Tier 3 — 360-Degree Corporate Fundamentals and Financial Notes TM**: Invoke `vietnam_securities_stock_360` and `vietnam_securities_corporate_screener` to audit earnings quality, commercial bank CASA ratios, Tier 1 to Tier 5 loan quality, and multi-model intrinsic valuation.
4. **Tier 4 — Four-Horizon Strategy Formulation and Empirical Backtesting**: Invoke `vietnam_securities_strategy_advisor` and `vietnam_securities_quick_backtest` to validate portfolio performance across **3-month**, **6-month**, **12-month**, and **3-year** horizons.

## Step-by-Step Workflow for Running StockMCP Analyses

<Steps>

### Step 1: Initialize the Analytical Session via Bootstrap

Start your workflow by calling `vietnam_securities_session_bootstrap` to synchronize the two-way connection between StockMCP and the [Finco](https://www.finco.vn) data warehouse.

### Step 2: Execute Top-Down Macro Synthesis and Corporate Screening

Run `vietnam_macro_topdown_synthesis` alongside `vietnam_securities_corporate_screener` to isolate sector leaders with robust balance sheets.

### Step 3: Run Stock 360 Valuation and Multi-Horizon Backtesting

Call `vietnam_securities_stock_360` on your target ticker, followed by `vietnam_securities_strategy_advisor` and `vietnam_securities_quick_backtest` to generate complete risk-adjusted performance metrics.

### Step 4: Extend into Econometric Modeling on EcoData, EcoLab, and VietLOD

Combine StockMCP outputs with the 52 econometric MCP tools on [EcoData](https://ecodata.io.vn), the automated manuscript pipeline on [EcoLab](https://ecolab.io.vn), and the 201 model guides on [VietLOD](https://vietlod.tnsai.vn) for publication-grade research.

</Steps>
