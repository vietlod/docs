## 14.1. Central Research Question and Core Mathematical Specification

- **Central Research Question**: How to separate stochastic noise from technical inefficiency and consistently estimate Total Factor Productivity under endogenous input selection?
- **Chapter Architecture**: Encompasses `1` specialized methodological parts from `14.1` to `14.1` and `4` empirical model lessons validated on Vietnamese datasets from [EcoData](https://ecodata.io.vn) and [Finco](https://www.finco.vn).

Core structural equation for Chapter 14:

$$
\ln Y_{it} = \beta_0 + \beta_L \ln L_{it} + \beta_K \ln K_{it} + \omega_{it} + \varepsilon_{it}, \quad \text{TE}_i = \text{E}[\exp(-u_i) \mid \varepsilon_i]
$$
