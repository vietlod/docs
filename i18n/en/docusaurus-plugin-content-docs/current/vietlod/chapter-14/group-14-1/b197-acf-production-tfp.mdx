## 1. Methodological Meaning and Scope of Application

### 1.1. Econometric Foundation and Identification Mechanism

Consistently estimates labor and capital elasticities and firm-level Total Factor Productivity omega_it via two-stage intermediate-input proxy inversion and Markov AR — 1 — productivity innovation GMM moments. Across empirical econometrics, `acf_production_tfp` enables researchers to isolate structural parameters while controlling for confounding covariates and verifying identification assumptions via `prodest` in Stata 18 MP, `prodest` in R 4.x, and `statsmodels / scipy` in Python 3.11+.

### 1.2. Scope of Application and Sample Structure Requirements

Firm-level panel data — entity, year — with log output/value-added, free variable input — s — — labor , state capital input, and strictly monotonic intermediate proxy input — materials/energy . Mandatory post-estimation verification includes testing identification restrictions, evaluating goodness-of-fit metrics, and confirming numerical convergence across all three econometric engines.

### 1.3. Structural Equations and Asymptotic Covariance Specification

Core structural equation for `acf_production_tfp`:

$$
y_{it} = \beta_l l_{it} + \beta_k k_{it} + \omega_{it} + \varepsilon_{it}, \quad \omega_{it} = g(\omega_{i,t-1}) + \xi_{it}, \quad \text{E}\left[\xi_{it}(\beta_l, \beta_k) \otimes Z_{it}\right] = 0
$$

Estimator objective function and sample moment conditions:

$$
\ln Y_{it} = \beta_0 + \beta_K \ln K_{it} + \beta_L \ln L_{it} + \omega_{it} - u_{it} + v_{it}, \quad \widehat{\text{TFP}}_{it} = \exp\left(\ln Y_{it} - \hat{\beta}_K \ln K_{it} - \hat{\beta}_L \ln L_{it}\right)
$$

Asymptotic covariance matrix and post-estimation diagnostic metric:

$$
\gamma = \frac{\sigma_u^2}{\sigma_v^2 + \sigma_u^2} \in [0, 1], \quad \text{LR}_{\text{one-sided}} = -2\left(\ell_{\text{OLS}} - \ell_{\text{SFA}}\right) \sim \frac{1}{2}\chi^2_0 + \frac{1}{2}\chi^2_1
$$
