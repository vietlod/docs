## 14.1.3.1. Methodological Meaning and Scope of Application

### 14.1.3.1.1. Econometric Foundation and Identification Mechanism

Computes cross-period radial LP distance functions D^t — x^t,y^t , D^[t+1] — x^[t+1],y^[t+1] , D^t — x^[t+1],y^[t+1] , and D^[t+1] — x^t,y^t — to decompose TFP growth into Technical Catch-Up — EFFCH , Frontier Shift — TECHCH , and Scale Efficiency Change — SECH . Across empirical econometrics, `malmquist_productivity` enables researchers to isolate structural parameters while controlling for confounding covariates and verifying identification assumptions via `malmq2` in Stata 18 MP, `Benchmarking` in R 4.x, and `scipy` in Python 3.11+.

### 14.1.3.1.2. Scope of Application and Sample Structure Requirements

Panel of DMUs — entity, time with T &gt;= 2 periods — with strictly positive outputs — dependent_variables &gt; 0 — and inputs — independent_variables &gt; 0 . Mandatory post-estimation verification includes testing identification restrictions, evaluating goodness-of-fit metrics, and confirming numerical convergence across all three econometric engines.

### 14.1.3.1.3. Structural Equations and Asymptotic Covariance Specification

Core structural equation for `malmquist_productivity`:

$$
M_0(x^t, y^t, x^{t+1}, y^{t+1}) = \frac{D_0^{t+1}(x^{t+1}, y^{t+1})}{D_0^t(x^t, y^t)}\left[\frac{D_0^t(x^{t+1}, y^{t+1})}{D_0^{t+1}(x^{t+1}, y^{t+1})}\cdot\frac{D_0^t(x^t, y^t)}{D_0^{t+1}(x^t, y^t)}\right]^{1/2}
$$

Estimator objective function and sample moment conditions:

$$
\ln Y_{it} = \beta_0 + \beta_K \ln K_{it} + \beta_L \ln L_{it} + \omega_{it} - u_{it} + v_{it}, \quad \widehat{\text{TFP}}_{it} = \exp\left(\ln Y_{it} - \hat{\beta}_K \ln K_{it} - \hat{\beta}_L \ln L_{it}\right)
$$

Asymptotic covariance matrix and post-estimation diagnostic metric:

$$
\gamma = \frac{\sigma_u^2}{\sigma_v^2 + \sigma_u^2} \in [0, 1], \quad \text{LR}_{\text{one-sided}} = -2\left(\ell_{\text{OLS}} - \ell_{\text{SFA}}\right) \sim \frac{1}{2}\chi^2_0 + \frac{1}{2}\chi^2_1
$$
