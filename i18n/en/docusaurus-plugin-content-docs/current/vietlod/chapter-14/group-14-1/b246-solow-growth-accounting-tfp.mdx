## 14.1.6.1. Methodological Meaning and Scope of Application

### 14.1.6.1.1. Econometric Foundation and Identification Mechanism

Decomposes aggregate output growth into factor accumulation contributions and Hicks-neutral Total Factor Productivity (Solow residual), testing constant returns to scale and TFP variance shares.

### 14.1.6.1.2. Scope of Application and Sample Structure Requirements

Macroeconomic, sectoral, or regional growth accounting with strictly positive output and factor inputs measuring technological progress.

- **Mandatory Pre-Estimation Diagnostics**: `input_output_isotonicity_precheck, vif_multicollinearity`
- **Mandatory Post-Estimation Diagnostics**: `solow_crs_wald, durbin_watson_autocorrelation`

### 14.1.6.1.3. Structural Equations and Asymptotic Covariance Specification

Core structural equation for `solow_growth_accounting_tfp`:

$$
\ln Y_{it} = \beta_0 + X_{it}^\top \beta + v_{it} - u_{it}, \quad v_{it} \sim \mathcal{N}(0, \sigma_v^2), \quad u_{it} \ge 0
$$

Estimator objective function and sample moment conditions:

$$
\text{TE}_{it} = \mathbb{E}\left[\exp(-u_{it}) \mid \varepsilon_{it} = v_{it} - u_{it}\right], \quad \omega_{it} = g(\omega_{it-1}) + \xi_{it}
$$

Asymptotic covariance matrix and robust inference metric:

$$
M_0(x^t, y^t, x^{t+1}, y^{t+1}) = \left[\frac{D_0^t(x^{t+1}, y^{t+1})}{D_0^t(x^t, y^t)} \cdot \frac{D_0^{t+1}(x^{t+1}, y^{t+1})}{D_0^{t+1}(x^t, y^t)}\right]^{1/2}
$$
