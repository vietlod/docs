## 14.1.1. Methodological Overview and Structural Equations

- **Methodological Focus**: Provides consistent estimation and robust inference for **Stochastic Frontier Efficiency and Structural Total Factor Productivity** specifications within **Productivity, Frontier Efficiency and Structural Production Functions**.
- **Mandatory Post-Estimation Diagnostics**: Tỷ trọng phương sai phi hiệu quả $\gamma = \sigma_u^2 / (\sigma_v^2 + \sigma_u^2)$ và $\lambda = \sigma_u/\sigma_v$, kiểm định Kodde–Palm mixed-$\chi^2$ LR cho $H_0: \sigma_u^2 = 0$ — có tồn tại phi hiệu quả hay chỉ là OLS, phân phối TE..

Core structural specification for Part 14.1:

$$
\ln Y_{it} = X_{it}^\top \beta + v_{it} - u_{it}, \quad v_{it} \sim \mathcal{N}(0, \sigma_v^2), \quad u_{it} \sim \mathcal{N}^+(\mu_{it}, \sigma_u^2), \quad \text{TE}_{it} = \text{E}[\exp(-u_{it}) \mid \varepsilon_{it}]
$$

Estimator objective and moment conditions:

$$
\ln Y_{it} = \beta_0 + \beta_K \ln K_{it} + \beta_L \ln L_{it} + \omega_{it} - u_{it} + v_{it}, \quad \widehat{\text{TFP}}_{it} = \exp\left(\ln Y_{it} - \hat{\beta}_K \ln K_{it} - \hat{\beta}_L \ln L_{it}\right)
$$
