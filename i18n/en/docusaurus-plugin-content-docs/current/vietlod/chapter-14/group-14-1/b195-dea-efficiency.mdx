## 1. Methodological Meaning and Scope of Application

### 1.1. Econometric Foundation and Identification Mechanism

Solves HiGHS linear programs for every DMU under Constant — CCR — and Variable — BCC — Returns to Scale to measure Technical Efficiency, Scale Efficiency — CRS/VRS , and Simar-Wilson bias-corrected efficiency intervals. Across empirical econometrics, `dea_efficiency` enables researchers to isolate structural parameters while controlling for confounding covariates and verifying identification assumptions via `teradial / simarwilson` in Stata 18 MP, `Benchmarking` in R 4.x, and `scipy` in Python 3.11+.

### 1.2. Scope of Application and Sample Structure Requirements

Multi-output — dependent_variables &gt;= 1, non-negative — and multi-input — independent_variables &gt;= 1, non-negative — DMU benchmarking datasets. Mandatory post-estimation verification includes testing identification restrictions, evaluating goodness-of-fit metrics, and confirming numerical convergence across all three econometric engines.

### 1.3. Structural Equations and Asymptotic Covariance Specification

Core structural equation for `dea_efficiency`:

$$
\hat{\theta}_i = \min\left\{\theta \gt 0 \;\middle|\; \theta x_i \ge \sum_{j=1}^N \lambda_j x_j, \; y_i \le \sum_{j=1}^N \lambda_j y_j, \; \sum_{j=1}^N \lambda_j = 1, \; \lambda_j \ge 0\right\}
$$

Estimator objective function and sample moment conditions:

$$
\ln Y_{it} = \beta_0 + \beta_K \ln K_{it} + \beta_L \ln L_{it} + \omega_{it} - u_{it} + v_{it}, \quad \widehat{\text{TFP}}_{it} = \exp\left(\ln Y_{it} - \hat{\beta}_K \ln K_{it} - \hat{\beta}_L \ln L_{it}\right)
$$

Asymptotic covariance matrix and post-estimation diagnostic metric:

$$
\gamma = \frac{\sigma_u^2}{\sigma_v^2 + \sigma_u^2} \in [0, 1], \quad \text{LR}_{\text{one-sided}} = -2\left(\ell_{\text{OLS}} - \ell_{\text{SFA}}\right) \sim \frac{1}{2}\chi^2_0 + \frac{1}{2}\chi^2_1
$$
