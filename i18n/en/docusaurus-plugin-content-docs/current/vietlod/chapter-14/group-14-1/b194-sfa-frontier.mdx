## 14.1.1.1. Methodological Meaning and Scope of Application

### 14.1.1.1.1. Econometric Foundation and Identification Mechanism

Separates symmetric random noise v_i ~ N — 0, sigma_v^2 — from one-sided technical inefficiency u_i &gt;= 0 via MLE, reporting Jondrow E[exp — -u_i —|e_i] Technical Efficiency scores and Kodde-Palm mixed-chi2 LR test. Across empirical econometrics, `sfa_frontier` enables researchers to isolate structural parameters while controlling for confounding covariates and verifying identification assumptions via `base Stata frontier` in Stata 18 MP, `frontier` in R 4.x, and `scipy / statsmodels` in Python 3.11+.

### 14.1.1.1.2. Scope of Application and Sample Structure Requirements

Firm, bank, hospital, farm, or regional production — y = f — x — + v - u — or cost — c = f — x,w — + v + u — datasets. Mandatory post-estimation verification includes testing identification restrictions, evaluating goodness-of-fit metrics, and confirming numerical convergence across all three econometric engines.

### 14.1.1.1.3. Structural Equations and Asymptotic Covariance Specification

Core structural equation for `sfa_frontier`:

$$
\ln Y_{it} = X_{it}^\top \beta + v_{it} - u_{it}, \quad v_{it} \sim \mathcal{N}(0, \sigma_v^2), \quad u_{it} \sim \mathcal{N}^+(\mu_{it}, \sigma_u^2), \quad \text{TE}_{it} = \text{E}[\exp(-u_{it}) \mid \varepsilon_{it}]
$$

Estimator objective function and sample moment conditions:

$$
\ln Y_{it} = \beta_0 + \beta_K \ln K_{it} + \beta_L \ln L_{it} + \omega_{it} - u_{it} + v_{it}, \quad \widehat{\text{TFP}}_{it} = \exp\left(\ln Y_{it} - \hat{\beta}_K \ln K_{it} - \hat{\beta}_L \ln L_{it}\right)
$$

Asymptotic covariance matrix and post-estimation diagnostic metric:

$$
\gamma = \frac{\sigma_u^2}{\sigma_v^2 + \sigma_u^2} \in [0, 1], \quad \text{LR}_{\text{one-sided}} = -2\left(\ell_{\text{OLS}} - \ell_{\text{SFA}}\right) \sim \frac{1}{2}\chi^2_0 + \frac{1}{2}\chi^2_1
$$
