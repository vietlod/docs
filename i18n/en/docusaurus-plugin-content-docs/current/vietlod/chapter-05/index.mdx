## 5.1. Central Research Question and Core Mathematical Specification

- **Central Research Question**: How to control for unobserved unit heterogeneity, dynamic persistence, Nickell bias, and cross-sectional spillover factors across multi-dimensional panel datasets?
- **Chapter Architecture**: Encompasses `6` specialized methodological parts from `5.1` to `5.6` and `28` empirical model lessons validated on Vietnamese datasets from [EcoData](https://ecodata.io.vn) and [Finco](https://www.finco.vn).

Core structural equation for Chapter 5:

$$
Y_{it} = \rho Y_{i,t-1} + X_{it}^\top \beta + \mu_i + \lambda_t + \varepsilon_{it}
$$
