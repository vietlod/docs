## 1. Central Research Question and Core Mathematical Specification

- **Central Research Question**: How to control for unobserved unit heterogeneity, dynamic persistence, Nickell bias, and cross-sectional spillover factors across multi-dimensional panel datasets?
- **Chapter Architecture**: Encompasses `6` specialized methodological groups and `28` empirical models validated on Vietnamese datasets from [EcoData](https://ecodata.io.vn) and [Finco](https://www.finco.vn).

Core structural equation for Chapter 05:

$$
Y_{it} = \rho Y_{i,t-1} + X_{it}^\top \beta + \mu_i + \lambda_t + \varepsilon_{it}
$$

## 3. Four-Step Workflow for Selecting and Executing Models in Chapter 05

<Steps>

### Step 1. Select the Appropriate Methodological Group Across the 6 Groups

Match your economic hypothesis and sample structure against the identification requirements from Group `5.1` through Group `5.6`.

### Step 2. Prepare Standardized Datasets From EcoData or Finco

Download the standardized `.csv` practice file for your target estimator from `/src/ecoaz/data/` or extract live empirical series from [EcoData](https://ecodata.io.vn) and [Finco](https://www.finco.vn).

### Step 3. Estimate Parameters and Run Diagnostics in Stata 18, R 4.x, and Python 3.11+

Execute the tri-engine scripts provided in each model guide and verify the mandatory post-estimation diagnostics: **Hausman & Mundlak CRE Test, Pesaran CD Test, Pesaran CIPS Unit Root, Westerlund Cointegration, Arellano–Bond AR bậc 2 & Hansen J, Bootstrap Threshold F-test**.

### Step 4. Verify Three-Software Convergence and Export Publication Tables on EcoLab

Confirm numerical convergence across Stata, R, and Python using the three-panel empirical figure and generate journal-grade tables on [EcoLab](https://ecolab.io.vn).

</Steps>
