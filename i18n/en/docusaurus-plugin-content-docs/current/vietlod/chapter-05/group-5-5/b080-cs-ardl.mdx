## 5.5.5.1. Methodological Meaning and Scope of Application

### 5.5.5.1.1. Econometric Foundation and Identification Mechanism

Augments dynamic entity ARDL regressions with contemporaneous and lagged cross-sectional averages to recover consistent long-run effects under dynamics and strong CD. Across empirical econometrics, `cs_ardl` enables researchers to isolate structural parameters while controlling for confounding covariates and verifying identification assumptions via `xtdcce2` in Stata 18 MP, `plm` in R 4.x, and `statsmodels` in Python 3.11+.

### 5.5.5.1.2. Scope of Application and Sample Structure Requirements

Dynamic macro, energy, or financial panels with mixed I — 0 —/I — 1 — variables, heterogeneous dynamics, and unobserved global shocks. Mandatory post-estimation verification includes testing identification restrictions, evaluating goodness-of-fit metrics, and confirming numerical convergence across all three econometric engines.

### 5.5.5.1.3. Structural Equations and Asymptotic Covariance Specification

Core structural equation for `cs_ardl`:

$$
Y_{it} = \alpha_i + \sum_{\ell=1}^p \varphi_{i\ell}Y_{i,t-\ell} + \sum_{\ell=0}^q \beta_{i\ell}^\top X_{i,t-\ell} + \sum_{\ell=0}^{p_{\bar{z}}} \psi_{i\ell}^\top \bar{Z}_{t-\ell} + \varepsilon_{it}, \quad \hat{\theta}_i = \frac{\sum_{\ell=0}^q \hat{\beta}_{i\ell}}{1 - \sum_{\ell=1}^p \hat{\varphi}_{i\ell}}
$$

Estimator objective function and sample moment conditions:

$$
\hat{\beta}_{\text{Panel}} = \left(\sum_{i=1}^N \tilde{X}_i^\top W_i \tilde{X}_i\right)^{-1}\left(\sum_{i=1}^N \tilde{X}_i^\top W_i \tilde{Y}_i\right), \quad \tilde{Y}_{it} = Y_{it} - \theta \bar{Y}_i
$$

Asymptotic covariance matrix and post-estimation diagnostic metric:

$$
\widehat{\text{Var}}_{\text{CR}}(\hat{\beta}) = \frac{N}{N-1}\frac{N_T - 1}{N_T - K}\left(\tilde{X}^\top \tilde{X}\right)^{-1}\left(\sum_{i=1}^N \tilde{X}_i^\top \hat{u}_i \hat{u}_i^\top \tilde{X}_i\right)\left(\tilde{X}^\top \tilde{X}\right)^{-1}
$$
