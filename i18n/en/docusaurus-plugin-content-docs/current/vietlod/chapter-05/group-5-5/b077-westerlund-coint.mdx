## 5.5.2.1. Methodological Meaning and Scope of Application

### 5.5.2.1.1. Econometric Foundation and Identification Mechanism

Tests second-generation panel cointegration via structural error-correction speed without imposing common-factor restrictions. Across empirical econometrics, `westerlund_coint` enables researchers to isolate structural parameters while controlling for confounding covariates and verifying identification assumptions via `xtwest` in Stata 18 MP, `plm` in R 4.x, and `linearmodels` in Python 3.11+.

### 5.5.2.1.2. Scope of Application and Sample Structure Requirements

Nonstationary I — 1 — panel series with sufficient time dimension to estimate entity and pooled conditional ECM statistics Gt, Ga, Pt, and Pa. Mandatory post-estimation verification includes testing identification restrictions, evaluating goodness-of-fit metrics, and confirming numerical convergence across all three econometric engines.

### 5.5.2.1.3. Structural Equations and Asymptotic Covariance Specification

Core structural equation for `westerlund_coint`:

$$
\Delta Y_{it} = \delta_i^\top d_t + \alpha_i Y_{i,t-1} + \lambda_i^\top X_{i,t-1} + \sum_{j=1}^{p_i}\alpha_{ij}\Delta Y_{i,t-j} + \sum_{j=-q_i}^{p_i}\gamma_{ij}^\top \Delta X_{i,t-j} + e_{it}, \quad G_\tau = \frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^N \frac{\hat{\alpha}_i}{\text{SE}(\hat{\alpha}_i)}
$$

Estimator objective function and sample moment conditions:

$$
\hat{\beta}_{\text{Panel}} = \left(\sum_{i=1}^N \tilde{X}_i^\top W_i \tilde{X}_i\right)^{-1}\left(\sum_{i=1}^N \tilde{X}_i^\top W_i \tilde{Y}_i\right), \quad \tilde{Y}_{it} = Y_{it} - \theta \bar{Y}_i
$$

Asymptotic covariance matrix and post-estimation diagnostic metric:

$$
\widehat{\text{Var}}_{\text{CR}}(\hat{\beta}) = \frac{N}{N-1}\frac{N_T - 1}{N_T - K}\left(\tilde{X}^\top \tilde{X}\right)^{-1}\left(\sum_{i=1}^N \tilde{X}_i^\top \hat{u}_i \hat{u}_i^\top \tilde{X}_i\right)\left(\tilde{X}^\top \tilde{X}\right)^{-1}
$$
