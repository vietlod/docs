## 1. Methodological Overview and Structural Equations

- **Methodological Focus**: Provides consistent estimation and robust inference for **Second-Generation Cross-Sectionally Dependent Panels** specifications within **Static, Dynamic and Cross-Sectionally Dependent Panel Econometrics**.
- **Mandatory Post-Estimation Diagnostics**: Thống kê CIPS và CIPS, giá trị tới hạn chính xác theo cặp kích thước mẫu $N$ và $T$ ở mức $1\%, 5\%, 10\%$ — không chặn / có chặn / có xu thế, phân bố $t_i$ CADF..

Core structural specification for Group 5.5:

$$
\Delta Y_{it} = a_i + b_i Y_{i,t-1} + c_i \bar{Y}_{t-1} + \sum_{j=0}^p d_{ij}\Delta \bar{Y}_{t-j} + \sum_{j=1}^p \delta_{ij}\Delta Y_{i,t-j} + e_{it}, \quad \text{CIPS} = \frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^N t_i(N, T)
$$

Estimator objective and moment conditions:

$$
\hat{\beta}_{\text{Panel}} = \left(\sum_{i=1}^N \tilde{X}_i^\top W_i \tilde{X}_i\right)^{-1}\left(\sum_{i=1}^N \tilde{X}_i^\top W_i \tilde{Y}_i\right), \quad \tilde{Y}_{it} = Y_{it} - \theta \bar{Y}_i
$$
