## 1. Methodological Meaning and Scope of Application

### 1.1. Econometric Foundation and Identification Mechanism

Pools homogeneous structural slopes after projecting each entity orthogonal to cross-sectional time averages to absorb heterogeneous loadings on common factors. Across empirical econometrics, `ccep` enables researchers to isolate structural parameters while controlling for confounding covariates and verifying identification assumptions via `xtdcce2` in Stata 18 MP, `plm` in R 4.x, and `linearmodels` in Python 3.11+.

### 1.2. Scope of Application and Sample Structure Requirements

Panels with strong unobserved common shocks where Pesaran-Yamagata tests support homogeneous structural slope coefficients. Mandatory post-estimation verification includes testing identification restrictions, evaluating goodness-of-fit metrics, and confirming numerical convergence across all three econometric engines.

### 1.3. Structural Equations and Asymptotic Covariance Specification

Core structural equation for `ccep`:

$$
\hat{\beta}_{\text{CCEP}} = \left(\sum_{i=1}^N X_i^\top \bar{M}_w X_i\right)^{-1}\left(\sum_{i=1}^N X_i^\top \bar{M}_w Y_i\right), \quad \bar{M}_w = I_T - \bar{H}_w(\bar{H}_w^\top \bar{H}_w)^{-1}\bar{H}_w^\top
$$

Estimator objective function and sample moment conditions:

$$
\hat{\beta}_{\text{Panel}} = \left(\sum_{i=1}^N \tilde{X}_i^\top W_i \tilde{X}_i\right)^{-1}\left(\sum_{i=1}^N \tilde{X}_i^\top W_i \tilde{Y}_i\right), \quad \tilde{Y}_{it} = Y_{it} - \theta \bar{Y}_i
$$

Asymptotic covariance matrix and post-estimation diagnostic metric:

$$
\widehat{\text{Var}}_{\text{CR}}(\hat{\beta}) = \frac{N}{N-1}\frac{N_T - 1}{N_T - K}\left(\tilde{X}^\top \tilde{X}\right)^{-1}\left(\sum_{i=1}^N \tilde{X}_i^\top \hat{u}_i \hat{u}_i^\top \tilde{X}_i\right)\left(\tilde{X}^\top \tilde{X}\right)^{-1}
$$
