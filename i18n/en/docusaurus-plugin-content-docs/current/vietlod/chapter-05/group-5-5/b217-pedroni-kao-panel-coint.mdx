## 5.5.8.1. Methodological Meaning and Scope of Application

### 5.5.8.1.1. Econometric Foundation and Identification Mechanism

Estimates long-run cointegrating vectors in nonstationary I(1) panels and evaluates Kao homogeneous ADF and Pedroni heterogeneous group-ADF residual stationarity statistics.

### 5.5.8.1.2. Scope of Application and Sample Structure Requirements

Nonstationary macroeconomic or cross-country panels where variables are integrated I(1) and long-run equilibrium cointegration must be verified.

- **Mandatory Pre-Estimation Diagnostics**: `pesaran_cd_cross_sectional_dependence, panel_unit_root_ips`
- **Mandatory Post-Estimation Diagnostics**: `kao_panel_coint_adf, pedroni_group_adf_stat`

### 5.5.8.1.3. Structural Equations and Asymptotic Covariance Specification

Core structural equation for `pedroni_kao_panel_coint`:

$$
Y_{it} = \alpha_i + \lambda_t + X_{it}^\top \beta + \gamma^\top \bar{F}_t + \varepsilon_{it}, \quad i=1,\dots,N,\; t=1,\dots,T
$$

Estimator objective function and sample moment conditions:

$$
\mathbb{E}\left[Z_{i}^\top \Delta \varepsilon_i\right] = 0, \quad \hat{\beta}_{\text{GMM}} = \arg\min_{\beta} \left(\frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^N Z_i^\top \Delta \varepsilon_i(\beta)\right)^\top W_N \left(\frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^N Z_i^\top \Delta \varepsilon_i(\beta)\right)
$$

Asymptotic covariance matrix and robust inference metric:

$$
\widehat{\text{Var}}_{\text{CR}}(\hat{\beta}) = (X^\top M X)^{-1} \left(\sum_{i=1}^N X_i^\top M_i \hat{u}_i \hat{u}_i^\top M_i X_i\right) (X^\top M X)^{-1}
$$

import pandas as pd
# df: a pandas DataFrame holding your selected variables
import statsmodels.api as sm
d = df.sort_values(["entity", "year"]).copy()
dy = d["y"] - d.groupby("entity")["y"].transform("mean")
dX = d[['x1', 'x2']] - d.groupby("entity")[['x1', 'x2']].transform("mean")
lr = sm.OLS(dy, dX).fit()
d["e"] = lr.resid; d["de"] = d.groupby("entity")["e"].diff(); d["el1"] = d.groupby("entity")["e"].shift(1)
sub = d.dropna(subset=["de", "el1"])
print("Kao ADF t-stat:", sm.OLS(sub["de"], sub[["el1"]]).fit().tvalues.iloc[0])
