## 5.2.6.1. Methodological Meaning and Scope of Application

### 5.2.6.1.1. Econometric Foundation and Identification Mechanism

Maximizes the first-differenced Gaussian quasi-likelihood jointly modeling initial observations Delta y_i1 and MA(1) tri-diagonal error covariance, avoiding Arellano-Bond instrument proliferation and Nickell bias.

### 5.2.6.1.2. Scope of Application and Sample Structure Requirements

Short-T, large-N dynamic panels where GMM suffers weak or too-many-instrument distortions and likelihood-based fixed-effects dynamic estimation is preferred.

- **Mandatory Pre-Estimation Diagnostics**: `hausman_fe_vs_re, vif_multicollinearity`
- **Mandatory Post-Estimation Diagnostics**: `arellano_bond_ar2, wooldridge_panel_autocorr`

### 5.2.6.1.3. Structural Equations and Asymptotic Covariance Specification

Core structural equation for `quasi_ml_dynamic_panel_hsiao`:

$$
Y_{it} = \alpha_i + \lambda_t + X_{it}^\top \beta + \gamma^\top \bar{F}_t + \varepsilon_{it}, \quad i=1,\dots,N,\; t=1,\dots,T
$$

Estimator objective function and sample moment conditions:

$$
\mathbb{E}\left[Z_{i}^\top \Delta \varepsilon_i\right] = 0, \quad \hat{\beta}_{\text{GMM}} = \arg\min_{\beta} \left(\frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^N Z_i^\top \Delta \varepsilon_i(\beta)\right)^\top W_N \left(\frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^N Z_i^\top \Delta \varepsilon_i(\beta)\right)
$$

Asymptotic covariance matrix and robust inference metric:

$$
\widehat{\text{Var}}_{\text{CR}}(\hat{\beta}) = (X^\top M X)^{-1} \left(\sum_{i=1}^N X_i^\top M_i \hat{u}_i \hat{u}_i^\top M_i X_i\right) (X^\top M X)^{-1}
$$
