## 1. Methodological Meaning and Scope of Application

### 1.1. Econometric Foundation and Identification Mechanism

Estimates time-series cross-section panels with Prais-Winsten AR1 serial correlation and contemporaneous cross-unit sandwich covariance. Across empirical econometrics, `pcse_panel` enables researchers to isolate structural parameters while controlling for confounding covariates and verifying identification assumptions via `base Stata xtpcse` in Stata 18 MP, `pcse` in R 4.x, and `linearmodels` in Python 3.11+.

### 1.2. Scope of Application and Sample Structure Requirements

Macro-political or fiscal panels with moderate N and comparable or longer T exhibiting groupwise heteroskedasticity and cross-unit correlation. Mandatory post-estimation verification includes testing identification restrictions, evaluating goodness-of-fit metrics, and confirming numerical convergence across all three econometric engines.

### 1.3. Structural Equations and Asymptotic Covariance Specification

Core structural equation for `pcse_panel`:

$$
\widehat{\text{Var}}_{\text{PCSE}}(\hat{\beta}) = (X^\top X)^{-1} X^\top (\hat{\Sigma}_N \otimes I_T) X (X^\top X)^{-1}, \quad \hat{\Sigma}_{ij} = \frac{1}{T}\sum_{t=1}^T \hat{\varepsilon}_{it}\hat{\varepsilon}_{jt}
$$

Estimator objective function and sample moment conditions:

$$
\hat{\beta}_{\text{Panel}} = \left(\sum_{i=1}^N \tilde{X}_i^\top W_i \tilde{X}_i\right)^{-1}\left(\sum_{i=1}^N \tilde{X}_i^\top W_i \tilde{Y}_i\right), \quad \tilde{Y}_{it} = Y_{it} - \theta \bar{Y}_i
$$

Asymptotic covariance matrix and post-estimation diagnostic metric:

$$
\widehat{\text{Var}}_{\text{CR}}(\hat{\beta}) = \frac{N}{N-1}\frac{N_T - 1}{N_T - K}\left(\tilde{X}^\top \tilde{X}\right)^{-1}\left(\sum_{i=1}^N \tilde{X}_i^\top \hat{u}_i \hat{u}_i^\top \tilde{X}_i\right)\left(\tilde{X}^\top \tilde{X}\right)^{-1}
$$
