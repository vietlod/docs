## 5.1.1. Methodological Overview and Structural Equations

- **Methodological Focus**: Provides consistent estimation and robust inference for **Static Panel Data Models** specifications within **Static, Dynamic and Cross-Sectionally Dependent Panel Econometrics**.
- **Mandatory Post-Estimation Diagnostics**: Kiểm định nhân tử Lagrange Breusch–Pagan cho hiệu ứng ngẫu nhiên (LM), kiểm định $F$ cho hiệu ứng cố định và sai số chuẩn phân cụm theo đơn vị.

Core structural specification for Part 5.1:

$$
Y_{it} = \beta_0 + X_{it}^\top \beta + u_{it}, \quad \text{Cov}(u_{it}, u_{is}) \neq 0
$$

Estimator objective and moment conditions:

$$
\hat{\beta}_{\text{Panel}} = \left(\sum_{i=1}^N \tilde{X}_i^\top W_i \tilde{X}_i\right)^{-1}\left(\sum_{i=1}^N \tilde{X}_i^\top W_i \tilde{Y}_i\right), \quad \tilde{Y}_{it} = Y_{it} - \theta \bar{Y}_i
$$
