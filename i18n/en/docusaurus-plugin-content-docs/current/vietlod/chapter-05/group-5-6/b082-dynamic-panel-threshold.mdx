## 5.6.2.1. Methodological Meaning and Scope of Application

### 5.6.2.1.1. Econometric Foundation and Identification Mechanism

Estimates regime-switching dynamic panels via two-step first-differenced GMM where lagged outcomes, regressors, and the threshold variable itself may be endogenous. Across empirical econometrics, `dynamic_panel_threshold` enables researchers to isolate structural parameters while controlling for confounding covariates and verifying identification assumptions via `xthenreg` in Stata 18 MP, `gmm` in R 4.x, and `linearmodels` in Python 3.11+.

### 5.6.2.1.2. Scope of Application and Sample Structure Requirements

Dynamic firm, bank, or macro panels with lagged dependent variable L1.y and potentially endogenous threshold switching. Mandatory post-estimation verification includes testing identification restrictions, evaluating goodness-of-fit metrics, and confirming numerical convergence across all three econometric engines.

### 5.6.2.1.3. Structural Equations and Asymptotic Covariance Specification

Core structural equation for `dynamic_panel_threshold`:

$$
\Delta Y_{it} = \rho \Delta Y_{i,t-1} + \Delta X_{it}^\top \beta_1 + \left(\mathbf{1}\{q_{it}\gt\gamma\}X_{it} - \mathbf{1}\{q_{i,t-1}\gt\gamma\}X_{i,t-1}\right)^\top \delta + \Delta\varepsilon_{it}
$$

Estimator objective function and sample moment conditions:

$$
\hat{\beta}_{\text{Panel}} = \left(\sum_{i=1}^N \tilde{X}_i^\top W_i \tilde{X}_i\right)^{-1}\left(\sum_{i=1}^N \tilde{X}_i^\top W_i \tilde{Y}_i\right), \quad \tilde{Y}_{it} = Y_{it} - \theta \bar{Y}_i
$$

Asymptotic covariance matrix and post-estimation diagnostic metric:

$$
\widehat{\text{Var}}_{\text{CR}}(\hat{\beta}) = \frac{N}{N-1}\frac{N_T - 1}{N_T - K}\left(\tilde{X}^\top \tilde{X}\right)^{-1}\left(\sum_{i=1}^N \tilde{X}_i^\top \hat{u}_i \hat{u}_i^\top \tilde{X}_i\right)\left(\tilde{X}^\top \tilde{X}\right)^{-1}
$$
