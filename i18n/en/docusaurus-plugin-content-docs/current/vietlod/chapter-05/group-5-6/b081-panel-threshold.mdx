## 1. Methodological Meaning and Scope of Application

### 1.1. Econometric Foundation and Identification Mechanism

Endogenously estimates regime-switching threshold gamma and regime-specific slopes in fixed-effects panels with likelihood-ratio confidence intervals. Across empirical econometrics, `panel_threshold` enables researchers to isolate structural parameters while controlling for confounding covariates and verifying identification assumptions via `xthreg` in Stata 18 MP, `pdR` in R 4.x, and `linearmodels` in Python 3.11+.

### 1.2. Scope of Application and Sample Structure Requirements

Panels testing nonlinear regime shifts by debt, inflation, institutional quality, or financial depth. Mandatory post-estimation verification includes testing identification restrictions, evaluating goodness-of-fit metrics, and confirming numerical convergence across all three econometric engines.

### 1.3. Structural Equations and Asymptotic Covariance Specification

Core structural equation for `panel_threshold`:

$$
Y_{it} = \mu_i + X_{it}^\top \beta_1 \mathbb{I}(q_{it} \le \gamma) + X_{it}^\top \beta_2 \mathbb{I}(q_{it} \gt \gamma) + \varepsilon_{it}, \quad \hat{\gamma} = \arg\min_{\gamma} S_1(\gamma)
$$

Estimator objective function and sample moment conditions:

$$
\hat{\beta}_{\text{Panel}} = \left(\sum_{i=1}^N \tilde{X}_i^\top W_i \tilde{X}_i\right)^{-1}\left(\sum_{i=1}^N \tilde{X}_i^\top W_i \tilde{Y}_i\right), \quad \tilde{Y}_{it} = Y_{it} - \theta \bar{Y}_i
$$

Asymptotic covariance matrix and post-estimation diagnostic metric:

$$
\widehat{\text{Var}}_{\text{CR}}(\hat{\beta}) = \frac{N}{N-1}\frac{N_T - 1}{N_T - K}\left(\tilde{X}^\top \tilde{X}\right)^{-1}\left(\sum_{i=1}^N \tilde{X}_i^\top \hat{u}_i \hat{u}_i^\top \tilde{X}_i\right)\left(\tilde{X}^\top \tilde{X}\right)^{-1}
$$
