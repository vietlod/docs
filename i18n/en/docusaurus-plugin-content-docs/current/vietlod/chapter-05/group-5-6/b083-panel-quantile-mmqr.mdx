## 5.6.3.1. Methodological Meaning and Scope of Application

### 5.6.3.1.1. Econometric Foundation and Identification Mechanism

Estimates non-crossing conditional quantiles with unit fixed effects through a two-step location-scale panel transformation. Across empirical econometrics, `panel_quantile_mmqr` enables researchers to isolate structural parameters while controlling for confounding covariates and verifying identification assumptions via `xtqreg` in Stata 18 MP, `quantreg` in R 4.x, and `statsmodels` in Python 3.11+.

### 5.6.3.1.2. Scope of Application and Sample Structure Requirements

Short-T or moderate-T panels where covariate impacts vary asymmetrically across the conditional outcome distribution. Mandatory post-estimation verification includes testing identification restrictions, evaluating goodness-of-fit metrics, and confirming numerical convergence across all three econometric engines.

### 5.6.3.1.3. Structural Equations and Asymptotic Covariance Specification

Core structural equation for `panel_quantile_mmqr`:

$$
Q_Y(\tau \mid X_{it}) = \left(\alpha_i + \delta_i q(\tau)\right) + X_{it}^\top\left(\beta + \gamma q(\tau)\right), \quad \text{E}[R_{it}] = 0, \quad \text{E}[|R_{it}|] = 1
$$

Estimator objective function and sample moment conditions:

$$
\hat{\beta}_{\text{Panel}} = \left(\sum_{i=1}^N \tilde{X}_i^\top W_i \tilde{X}_i\right)^{-1}\left(\sum_{i=1}^N \tilde{X}_i^\top W_i \tilde{Y}_i\right), \quad \tilde{Y}_{it} = Y_{it} - \theta \bar{Y}_i
$$

Asymptotic covariance matrix and post-estimation diagnostic metric:

$$
\widehat{\text{Var}}_{\text{CR}}(\hat{\beta}) = \frac{N}{N-1}\frac{N_T - 1}{N_T - K}\left(\tilde{X}^\top \tilde{X}\right)^{-1}\left(\sum_{i=1}^N \tilde{X}_i^\top \hat{u}_i \hat{u}_i^\top \tilde{X}_i\right)\left(\tilde{X}^\top \tilde{X}\right)^{-1}
$$
