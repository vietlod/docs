## 5.6.1. Methodological Overview and Structural Equations

- **Methodological Focus**: Provides consistent estimation and robust inference for **Nonlinear Threshold and Quantile Panel Models** specifications within **Static, Dynamic and Cross-Sectionally Dependent Panel Econometrics**.
- **Mandatory Post-Estimation Diagnostics**: Kiểm định Bootstrap $F_1$ tồn tại hiệu ứng ngưỡng, vì $\gamma$ không định danh dưới $H_0$, khoảng tin cậy tỷ số hợp lý $LR_1(\gamma) \le c(\alpha)$ cho điểm ngưỡng $\hat{\gamma}$..

Core structural specification for Part 5.6:

$$
Y_{it} = \mu_i + X_{it}^\top \beta_1 \mathbb{I}(q_{it} \le \gamma) + X_{it}^\top \beta_2 \mathbb{I}(q_{it} \gt \gamma) + \varepsilon_{it}, \quad \hat{\gamma} = \arg\min_{\gamma} S_1(\gamma)
$$

Estimator objective and moment conditions:

$$
\hat{\beta}_{\text{Panel}} = \left(\sum_{i=1}^N \tilde{X}_i^\top W_i \tilde{X}_i\right)^{-1}\left(\sum_{i=1}^N \tilde{X}_i^\top W_i \tilde{Y}_i\right), \quad \tilde{Y}_{it} = Y_{it} - \theta \bar{Y}_i
$$
