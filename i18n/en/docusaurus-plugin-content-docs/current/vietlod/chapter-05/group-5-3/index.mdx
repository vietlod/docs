## 5.3.1. Methodological Overview and Structural Equations

- **Methodological Focus**: Provides consistent estimation and robust inference for **Panel Instrumental Variables** specifications within **Static, Dynamic and Cross-Sectionally Dependent Panel Econometrics**.
- **Mandatory Post-Estimation Diagnostics**: Thống kê $F$ giai đoạn 1 Kleibergen–Paap rk Wald $F$ trên dữ liệu đã khử trung bình cá thể, kiểm định nội sinh Davidson–MacKinnon / Hausman và kiểm định Hansen $J$.

Core structural specification for Part 5.3:

$$
\tilde{Y}_{it} = \tilde{X}_{it}^\top \beta + \tilde{D}_{it}^\top \gamma + \tilde{\varepsilon}_{it}, \quad \text{E}[\tilde{Z}_{it}\tilde{\varepsilon}_{it}] = 0
$$

Estimator objective and moment conditions:

$$
\hat{\beta}_{\text{Panel}} = \left(\sum_{i=1}^N \tilde{X}_i^\top W_i \tilde{X}_i\right)^{-1}\left(\sum_{i=1}^N \tilde{X}_i^\top W_i \tilde{Y}_i\right), \quad \tilde{Y}_{it} = Y_{it} - \theta \bar{Y}_i
$$
