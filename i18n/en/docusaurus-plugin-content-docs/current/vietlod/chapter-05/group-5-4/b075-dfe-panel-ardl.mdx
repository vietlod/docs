## 5.4.5.1. Methodological Meaning and Scope of Application

### 5.4.5.1.1. Econometric Foundation and Identification Mechanism

Pools both long-run equilibrium coefficients and short-run error-correction dynamics across entities while absorbing unit fixed effects. Across empirical econometrics, `dfe_panel_ardl` enables researchers to isolate structural parameters while controlling for confounding covariates and verifying identification assumptions via `xtpmg` in Stata 18 MP, `fixest` in R 4.x, and `linearmodels` in Python 3.11+.

### 5.4.5.1.2. Scope of Application and Sample Structure Requirements

Institutionally homogeneous panels with cointegrated series serving as the benchmark counterpart to PMG and MG. Mandatory post-estimation verification includes testing identification restrictions, evaluating goodness-of-fit metrics, and confirming numerical convergence across all three econometric engines.

### 5.4.5.1.3. Structural Equations and Asymptotic Covariance Specification

Core structural equation for `dfe_panel_ardl`:

$$
\Delta Y_{it} = \phi\left(Y_{i,t-1} - \theta^\top X_{it}\right) + \sum_{j=1}^{p-1}\lambda_j \Delta Y_{i,t-j} + \sum_{j=0}^{q-1}\delta_j^\top \Delta X_{i,t-j} + \mu_i + \varepsilon_{it}
$$

Estimator objective function and sample moment conditions:

$$
\hat{\beta}_{\text{Panel}} = \left(\sum_{i=1}^N \tilde{X}_i^\top W_i \tilde{X}_i\right)^{-1}\left(\sum_{i=1}^N \tilde{X}_i^\top W_i \tilde{Y}_i\right), \quad \tilde{Y}_{it} = Y_{it} - \theta \bar{Y}_i
$$

Asymptotic covariance matrix and post-estimation diagnostic metric:

$$
\widehat{\text{Var}}_{\text{CR}}(\hat{\beta}) = \frac{N}{N-1}\frac{N_T - 1}{N_T - K}\left(\tilde{X}^\top \tilde{X}\right)^{-1}\left(\sum_{i=1}^N \tilde{X}_i^\top \hat{u}_i \hat{u}_i^\top \tilde{X}_i\right)\left(\tilde{X}^\top \tilde{X}\right)^{-1}
$$
