## 1. Methodological Overview and Structural Equations

- **Methodological Focus**: Provides consistent estimation and robust inference for **Long-Run Panel Cointegration and ARDL** specifications within **Static, Dynamic and Cross-Sectionally Dependent Panel Econometrics**.
- **Mandatory Post-Estimation Diagnostics**: Kiểm định đồng tích hợp bảng Pedroni / Kao trước ước lượng, kiểm tra độ nhạy đối với cửa sổ hạt nhân Bartlett/Parzen và so sánh hệ số Pooled vs Group-Mean (FMOLS).

Core structural specification for Group 5.4:

$$
\hat{\beta}_{\text{GFMOLS}} = \frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^N \left[\left(\sum_{t=1}^T \tilde{X}_{it}\tilde{X}_{it}^\top\right)^{-1}\left(\sum_{t=1}^T \tilde{X}_{it} Y_{it}^+ - T\hat{\Delta}_{\varepsilon u, i}^+\right)\right]
$$

Estimator objective and moment conditions:

$$
\hat{\beta}_{\text{Panel}} = \left(\sum_{i=1}^N \tilde{X}_i^\top W_i \tilde{X}_i\right)^{-1}\left(\sum_{i=1}^N \tilde{X}_i^\top W_i \tilde{Y}_i\right), \quad \tilde{Y}_{it} = Y_{it} - \theta \bar{Y}_i
$$
