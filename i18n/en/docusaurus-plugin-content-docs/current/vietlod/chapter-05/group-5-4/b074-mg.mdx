## 5.4.4.1. Methodological Meaning and Scope of Application

### 5.4.4.1.1. Econometric Foundation and Identification Mechanism

Estimates separate dynamic regressions per entity and averages long-run and short-run parameters nonparametrically to avoid heterogeneous slope aggregation bias. Across empirical econometrics, `mg` enables researchers to isolate structural parameters while controlling for confounding covariates and verifying identification assumptions via `xtmg` in Stata 18 MP, `plm` in R 4.x, and `statsmodels` in Python 3.11+.

### 5.4.4.1.2. Scope of Application and Sample Structure Requirements

Panels with sufficient periods per entity where structural elasticities and adjustment speeds vary genuinely across units. Mandatory post-estimation verification includes testing identification restrictions, evaluating goodness-of-fit metrics, and confirming numerical convergence across all three econometric engines.

### 5.4.4.1.3. Structural Equations and Asymptotic Covariance Specification

Core structural equation for `mg`:

$$
Y_{it} = \alpha_i + X_{it}^\top \beta_i + \varepsilon_{it}, \quad \hat{\beta}_{\text{MG}} = \frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^N \hat{\beta}_i, \quad \widehat{\text{Var}}(\hat{\beta}_{\text{MG}}) = \frac{1}{N(N-1)}\sum_{i=1}^N (\hat{\beta}_i - \hat{\beta}_{\text{MG}})(\hat{\beta}_i - \hat{\beta}_{\text{MG}})^\top
$$

Estimator objective function and sample moment conditions:

$$
\hat{\beta}_{\text{Panel}} = \left(\sum_{i=1}^N \tilde{X}_i^\top W_i \tilde{X}_i\right)^{-1}\left(\sum_{i=1}^N \tilde{X}_i^\top W_i \tilde{Y}_i\right), \quad \tilde{Y}_{it} = Y_{it} - \theta \bar{Y}_i
$$

Asymptotic covariance matrix and post-estimation diagnostic metric:

$$
\widehat{\text{Var}}_{\text{CR}}(\hat{\beta}) = \frac{N}{N-1}\frac{N_T - 1}{N_T - K}\left(\tilde{X}^\top \tilde{X}\right)^{-1}\left(\sum_{i=1}^N \tilde{X}_i^\top \hat{u}_i \hat{u}_i^\top \tilde{X}_i\right)\left(\tilde{X}^\top \tilde{X}\right)^{-1}
$$
