## 1. Methodological Meaning and Scope of Application

### 1.1. Econometric Foundation and Identification Mechanism

Jointly estimates N portfolio excess-return regressions on K pricing factors and tests exact finite-sample mean-variance spanning H0: alpha_1 =... = alpha_N = 0. Across empirical econometrics, `grs_factor_pricing` enables researchers to isolate structural parameters while controlling for confounding covariates and verifying identification assumptions via `grstest / sureg` in Stata 18 MP, `systemfit` in R 4.x, and `linearmodels` in Python 3.11+.

### 1.2. Scope of Application and Sample Structure Requirements

Empirical asset pricing evaluating CAPM or Fama-French multi-factor models across N &gt;= 2 test portfolios and K &gt;= 1 factor return series with T &gt; N + K + 2. Mandatory post-estimation verification includes testing identification restrictions, evaluating goodness-of-fit metrics, and confirming numerical convergence across all three econometric engines.

### 1.3. Structural Equations and Asymptotic Covariance Specification

Core structural equation for `grs_factor_pricing`:

$$
R_{it} - R_{ft} = \alpha_i + \beta_i^\top f_t + \varepsilon_{it}, \quad F_{\text{GRS}} = \frac{T - N - K}{N}\left(1 + \bar{f}^\top \hat{\Omega}_f^{-1}\bar{f}\right)^{-1}\hat{\alpha}^\top \hat{\Sigma}_\varepsilon^{-1}\hat{\alpha} \sim F_{N, T-N-K}
$$

Estimator objective function and sample moment conditions:

$$
\ell_T(\theta) = -\frac{1}{2}\sum_{t=1}^T \left[\ln(2\pi) + \ln(\sigma_t^2(\theta)) + \frac{\varepsilon_t^2}{\sigma_t^2(\theta)}\right], \quad z_t = \frac{\varepsilon_t}{\sigma_t} \sim \text{i.i.d. }(0, 1)
$$

Asymptotic covariance matrix and post-estimation diagnostic metric:

$$
\text{ARCH-LM}(q) = T \cdot R^2_{\hat{z}_t^2 \sim \hat{z}_{t-1}^2,\dots,\hat{z}_{t-q}^2} \xrightarrow{d} \chi^2_q, \quad \text{VaR}_\alpha(t+1 \mid t) = \hat{\mu}_{t+1\mid t} + q_\alpha(z)\hat{\sigma}_{t+1\mid t}
$$
