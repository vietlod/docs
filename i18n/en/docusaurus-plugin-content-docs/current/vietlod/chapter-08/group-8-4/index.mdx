## 1. Methodological Overview and Structural Equations

- **Methodological Focus**: Provides consistent estimation and robust inference for **Multi-Factor Asset Pricing Models** specifications within **Financial Volatility, Asset Pricing and Systemic Risk**.
- **Mandatory Post-Estimation Diagnostics**: Thống kê chính xác $F_{\text{GRS}} \sim F(N, T-N-K)$ & $p$-value, thống kê Wald GMM bền vững phương sai thay đổi, trung bình tuyệt đối định giá sai $\overline{|\hat{\alpha}|}$, tỷ số Sharpe cực đại nhân tố vs tiếp tuyến toàn thể..

Core structural specification for Group 8.4:

$$
R_{it} - R_{ft} = \alpha_i + \beta_i^\top f_t + \varepsilon_{it}, \quad F_{\text{GRS}} = \frac{T - N - K}{N}\left(1 + \bar{f}^\top \hat{\Omega}_f^{-1}\bar{f}\right)^{-1}\hat{\alpha}^\top \hat{\Sigma}_\varepsilon^{-1}\hat{\alpha} \sim F_{N, T-N-K}
$$

Estimator objective and moment conditions:

$$
\ell_T(\theta) = -\frac{1}{2}\sum_{t=1}^T \left[\ln(2\pi) + \ln(\sigma_t^2(\theta)) + \frac{\varepsilon_t^2}{\sigma_t^2(\theta)}\right], \quad z_t = \frac{\varepsilon_t}{\sigma_t} \sim \text{i.i.d. }(0, 1)
$$
