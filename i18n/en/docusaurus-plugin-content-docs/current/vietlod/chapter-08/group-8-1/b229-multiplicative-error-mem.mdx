## 8.1.11.1. Methodological Meaning and Scope of Application

### 8.1.11.1.1. Econometric Foundation and Identification Mechanism

Models positive financial activity processes (realized range, trading volume, Amihud illiquidity, spreads) as the product of dynamic Gamma/GARCH-type conditional scale mu_t and unit-mean positive innovations.

### 8.1.11.1.2. Scope of Application and Sample Structure Requirements

Non-negative financial market microstructure indicators exhibiting strong persistence and clustering without requiring logarithmic zero-truncation.

- **Mandatory Pre-Estimation Diagnostics**: `arch_lm_heteroskedasticity, adf_unit_root`
- **Mandatory Post-Estimation Diagnostics**: `engle_gallo_mem_persistence, ljung_box_autocorrelation`

### 8.1.11.1.3. Structural Equations and Asymptotic Covariance Specification

Core structural equation for `multiplicative_error_mem`:

$$
r_t = \mu_t + \varepsilon_t, \quad \varepsilon_t = \sigma_t z_t, \quad \sigma_t^2 = \omega + \sum_{i=1}^q \alpha_i \varepsilon_{t-i}^2 + \sum_{j=1}^p \beta_j \sigma_{t-j}^2
$$

Estimator objective function and sample moment conditions:

$$
\mathbb{E}_t\left[M_{t+1} R_{i,t+1}^e\right] = 0, \quad \mathbb{E}[R_i^e] = \beta_i^\top \lambda
$$

Asymptotic covariance matrix and robust inference metric:

$$
\ell_{\text{QMLE}}(\theta) = -\frac{1}{2}\sum_{t=1}^T \left(\ln \sigma_t^2(\theta) + \frac{\varepsilon_t^2(\theta)}{\sigma_t^2(\theta)}\right)
$$
