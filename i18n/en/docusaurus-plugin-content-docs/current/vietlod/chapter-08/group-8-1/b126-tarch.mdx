## 8.1.5.1. Methodological Meaning and Scope of Application

### 8.1.5.1.1. Econometric Foundation and Identification Mechanism

Models conditional standard deviation directly with piecewise-linear asymmetric responses to negative versus positive return innovations. Across empirical econometrics, `tarch` enables researchers to isolate structural parameters while controlling for confounding covariates and verifying identification assumptions via `base Stata arch` in Stata 18 MP, `rugarch` in R 4.x, and `arch` in Python 3.11+.

### 8.1.5.1.2. Scope of Application and Sample Structure Requirements

High-frequency asset, index, exchange-rate, or commodity return series with pronounced bad-news leverage and heavy tails. Mandatory post-estimation verification includes testing identification restrictions, evaluating goodness-of-fit metrics, and confirming numerical convergence across all three econometric engines.

### 8.1.5.1.3. Structural Equations and Asymptotic Covariance Specification

Core structural equation for `tarch`:

$$
\sigma_t = \omega + \sum_{i=1}^q \left(\alpha_i^+ \varepsilon_{t-i}^+ - \alpha_i^- \varepsilon_{t-i}^-\right) + \sum_{j=1}^p \beta_j \sigma_{t-j}, \quad \varepsilon_t^+ = \max(\varepsilon_t, 0), \;\varepsilon_t^- = \min(\varepsilon_t, 0)
$$

Estimator objective function and sample moment conditions:

$$
\ell_T(\theta) = -\frac{1}{2}\sum_{t=1}^T \left[\ln(2\pi) + \ln(\sigma_t^2(\theta)) + \frac{\varepsilon_t^2}{\sigma_t^2(\theta)}\right], \quad z_t = \frac{\varepsilon_t}{\sigma_t} \sim \text{i.i.d. }(0, 1)
$$

Asymptotic covariance matrix and post-estimation diagnostic metric:

$$
\text{ARCH-LM}(q) = T \cdot R^2_{\hat{z}_t^2 \sim \hat{z}_{t-1}^2,\dots,\hat{z}_{t-q}^2} \xrightarrow{d} \chi^2_q, \quad \text{VaR}_\alpha(t+1 \mid t) = \hat{\mu}_{t+1\mid t} + q_\alpha(z)\hat{\sigma}_{t+1\mid t}
$$

# 2. Estimate model and compute HC1 robust covariance matrix
