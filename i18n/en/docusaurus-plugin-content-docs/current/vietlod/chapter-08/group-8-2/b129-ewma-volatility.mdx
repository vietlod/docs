## 8.2.1.1. Methodological Meaning and Scope of Application

### 8.2.1.1.1. Econometric Foundation and Identification Mechanism

Estimates the RiskMetrics Exponentially Weighted Volatility — EWMA specification within the Realized Volatility, Mixed-Frequency and Tail Risk Models framework by optimizing the structural objective function and computing heteroskedasticity- and cluster-robust asymptotic standard errors. Across empirical econometrics, `ewma_volatility` enables researchers to isolate structural parameters while controlling for confounding covariates and verifying identification assumptions via `base Stata` in Stata 18 MP, `stats` in R 4.x, and `pandas / numpy` in Python 3.11+.

### 8.2.1.1.2. Scope of Application and Sample Structure Requirements

Chronologically ordered macro-financial time series requiring stationarity, lag-order selection, and serial-correlation diagnostics. Mandatory post-estimation verification includes testing identification restrictions, evaluating goodness-of-fit metrics, and confirming numerical convergence across all three econometric engines.

### 8.2.1.1.3. Structural Equations and Asymptotic Covariance Specification

Core structural equation for `ewma_volatility`:

$$
\sigma_t^2 = \lambda \sigma_{t-1}^2 + (1 - \lambda) r_{t-1}^2, \quad \lambda = 0.94
$$

Estimator objective function and sample moment conditions:

$$
\ell_T(\theta) = -\frac{1}{2}\sum_{t=1}^T \left[\ln(2\pi) + \ln(\sigma_t^2(\theta)) + \frac{\varepsilon_t^2}{\sigma_t^2(\theta)}\right], \quad z_t = \frac{\varepsilon_t}{\sigma_t} \sim \text{i.i.d. }(0, 1)
$$

Asymptotic covariance matrix and post-estimation diagnostic metric:

$$
\text{ARCH-LM}(q) = T \cdot R^2_{\hat{z}_t^2 \sim \hat{z}_{t-1}^2,\dots,\hat{z}_{t-q}^2} \xrightarrow{d} \chi^2_q, \quad \text{VaR}_\alpha(t+1 \mid t) = \hat{\mu}_{t+1\mid t} + q_\alpha(z)\hat{\sigma}_{t+1\mid t}
$$
