## 8.2.6.1. Methodological Meaning and Scope of Application

### 8.2.6.1.1. Econometric Foundation and Identification Mechanism

Couples return conditional variance with high-frequency realized volatility via a log-linear measurement equation featuring asymmetric leverage polynomials tau(z_t) for rapid volatility adaptation.

### 8.2.6.1.2. Scope of Application and Sample Structure Requirements

Equity, index, FX, and commodity series accompanied by intraday realized variance, bipower variation, or high-low range volatility proxies.

- **Mandatory Pre-Estimation Diagnostics**: `arch_lm_precheck, return_stationarity_adf`
- **Mandatory Post-Estimation Diagnostics**: `ljung_box_squared_std_resid, volatility_persistence_half_life`

### 8.2.6.1.3. Structural Equations and Asymptotic Covariance Specification

Core structural equation for `realized_garch_hansen_huang`:

$$
r_t = \mu_t + \varepsilon_t, \quad \varepsilon_t = \sigma_t z_t, \quad \sigma_t^2 = \omega + \sum_{i=1}^q \alpha_i \varepsilon_{t-i}^2 + \sum_{j=1}^p \beta_j \sigma_{t-j}^2
$$

Estimator objective function and sample moment conditions:

$$
\mathbb{E}_t\left[M_{t+1} R_{i,t+1}^e\right] = 0, \quad \mathbb{E}[R_i^e] = \beta_i^\top \lambda
$$

Asymptotic covariance matrix and robust inference metric:

$$
\ell_{\text{QMLE}}(\theta) = -\frac{1}{2}\sum_{t=1}^T \left(\ln \sigma_t^2(\theta) + \frac{\varepsilon_t^2(\theta)}{\sigma_t^2(\theta)}\right)
$$
