## 1. Methodological Meaning and Scope of Application

### 1.1. Econometric Foundation and Identification Mechanism

Decomposes conditional return variance into a short-run mean-reverting GARCH component multiplied by a secular Beta-MIDAS macroeconomic trend. Across empirical econometrics, `garch_midas` enables researchers to isolate structural parameters while controlling for confounding covariates and verifying identification assumptions via `garchmidas` in Stata 18 MP, `mfGARCH` in R 4.x, and `arch / scipy` in Python 3.11+.

### 1.2. Scope of Application and Sample Structure Requirements

Financial return series paired with macroeconomic or policy-uncertainty covariates driving low-frequency secular volatility. Mandatory post-estimation verification includes testing identification restrictions, evaluating goodness-of-fit metrics, and confirming numerical convergence across all three econometric engines.

### 1.3. Structural Equations and Asymptotic Covariance Specification

Core structural equation for `garch_midas`:

$$
r_{i,t} = \mu + \sqrt{\tau_t \cdot g_{i,t}}\,\varepsilon_{i,t}, \quad g_{i,t} = (1-\alpha-\beta) + \alpha\frac{(r_{i-1,t}-\mu)^2}{\tau_t} + \beta g_{i-1,t}, \quad \ln\tau_t = m + \theta\sum_{k=1}^K \varphi_k(\omega_1,\omega_2)X_{t-k}
$$

Estimator objective function and sample moment conditions:

$$
\ell_T(\theta) = -\frac{1}{2}\sum_{t=1}^T \left[\ln(2\pi) + \ln(\sigma_t^2(\theta)) + \frac{\varepsilon_t^2}{\sigma_t^2(\theta)}\right], \quad z_t = \frac{\varepsilon_t}{\sigma_t} \sim \text{i.i.d. }(0, 1)
$$

Asymptotic covariance matrix and post-estimation diagnostic metric:

$$
\text{ARCH-LM}(q) = T \cdot R^2_{\hat{z}_t^2 \sim \hat{z}_{t-1}^2,\dots,\hat{z}_{t-q}^2} \xrightarrow{d} \chi^2_q, \quad \text{VaR}_\alpha(t+1 \mid t) = \hat{\mu}_{t+1\mid t} + q_\alpha(z)\hat{\sigma}_{t+1\mid t}
$$
