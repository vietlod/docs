## 1. Methodological Meaning and Scope of Application

### 1.1. Econometric Foundation and Identification Mechanism

Quantifies an institution or sector marginal contribution to system-wide tail risk as the shift in system quantile VaR when the institution moves from median to distress. Across empirical econometrics, `covar_systemic_risk` enables researchers to isolate structural parameters while controlling for confounding covariates and verifying identification assumptions via `Stata qreg / sqreg` in Stata 18 MP, `quantreg` in R 4.x, and `statsmodels` in Python 3.11+.

### 1.2. Scope of Application and Sample Structure Requirements

Paired financial system and institution/sector return series with optional lagged macro-financial state variables. Mandatory post-estimation verification includes testing identification restrictions, evaluating goodness-of-fit metrics, and confirming numerical convergence across all three econometric engines.

### 1.3. Structural Equations and Asymptotic Covariance Specification

Core structural equation for `covar_systemic_risk`:

$$
\text{P}\left(R_t^{\text{sys}} \le \text{CoVaR}_q^{\text{sys}\mid i} \;\middle|\; R_t^i = \text{VaR}_q^i\right) = q, \quad \Delta\text{CoVaR}_{q,t}^i = \hat{\beta}_q^{\text{sys}\mid i}\left(\text{VaR}_{q,t}^i - \text{VaR}_{0.50,t}^i\right)
$$

Estimator objective function and sample moment conditions:

$$
\ell_T(\theta) = -\frac{1}{2}\sum_{t=1}^T \left[\ln(2\pi) + \ln(\sigma_t^2(\theta)) + \frac{\varepsilon_t^2}{\sigma_t^2(\theta)}\right], \quad z_t = \frac{\varepsilon_t}{\sigma_t} \sim \text{i.i.d. }(0, 1)
$$

Asymptotic covariance matrix and post-estimation diagnostic metric:

$$
\text{ARCH-LM}(q) = T \cdot R^2_{\hat{z}_t^2 \sim \hat{z}_{t-1}^2,\dots,\hat{z}_{t-q}^2} \xrightarrow{d} \chi^2_q, \quad \text{VaR}_\alpha(t+1 \mid t) = \hat{\mu}_{t+1\mid t} + q_\alpha(z)\hat{\sigma}_{t+1\mid t}
$$
