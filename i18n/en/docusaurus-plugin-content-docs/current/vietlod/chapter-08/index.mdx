## 8.1. Central Research Question and Core Mathematical Specification

- **Central Research Question**: How to model time-varying volatility clustering, leverage asymmetries, dynamic conditional correlations, tail risk, and multi-factor asset pricing anomalies?
- **Chapter Architecture**: Encompasses `4` specialized methodological parts from `8.1` to `8.4` and `14` empirical model lessons validated on Vietnamese datasets from [EcoData](https://ecodata.io.vn) and [Finco](https://www.finco.vn).

Core structural equation for Chapter 8:

$$
\sigma_t^2 = \omega + \alpha \varepsilon_{t-1}^2 + \gamma \varepsilon_{t-1}^2 \mathbb{I}(\varepsilon_{t-1} \lt 0) + \beta \sigma_{t-1}^2
$$
