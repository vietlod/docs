## 8.3.1.1. Methodological Meaning and Scope of Application

### 8.3.1.1.1. Econometric Foundation and Identification Mechanism

Models multivariate asset covariance via univariate GARCH marginal volatilities coupled with a positive-definite constant conditional correlation matrix. Across empirical econometrics, `ccc_garch` enables researchers to isolate structural parameters while controlling for confounding covariates and verifying identification assumptions via `base Stata mgarch` in Stata 18 MP, `rmgarch` in R 4.x, and `arch` in Python 3.11+.

### 8.3.1.1.2. Scope of Application and Sample Structure Requirements

Multi-asset portfolio risk, hedging ratios, and baseline testing of correlation constancy against DCC-GARCH. Mandatory post-estimation verification includes testing identification restrictions, evaluating goodness-of-fit metrics, and confirming numerical convergence across all three econometric engines.

### 8.3.1.1.3. Structural Equations and Asymptotic Covariance Specification

Core structural equation for `ccc_garch`:

$$
H_t = D_t R D_t, \quad D_t = \text{diag}(\sigma_{1,t}, \dots, \sigma_{K,t}), \quad R = [\rho_{ij}]_{K\times K}, \quad \sigma_{k,t}^2 = \omega_k + \alpha_k \varepsilon_{k,t-1}^2 + \beta_k \sigma_{k,t-1}^2
$$

Estimator objective function and sample moment conditions:

$$
\ell_T(\theta) = -\frac{1}{2}\sum_{t=1}^T \left[\ln(2\pi) + \ln(\sigma_t^2(\theta)) + \frac{\varepsilon_t^2}{\sigma_t^2(\theta)}\right], \quad z_t = \frac{\varepsilon_t}{\sigma_t} \sim \text{i.i.d. }(0, 1)
$$

Asymptotic covariance matrix and post-estimation diagnostic metric:

$$
\text{ARCH-LM}(q) = T \cdot R^2_{\hat{z}_t^2 \sim \hat{z}_{t-1}^2,\dots,\hat{z}_{t-q}^2} \xrightarrow{d} \chi^2_q, \quad \text{VaR}_\alpha(t+1 \mid t) = \hat{\mu}_{t+1\mid t} + q_\alpha(z)\hat{\sigma}_{t+1\mid t}
$$
