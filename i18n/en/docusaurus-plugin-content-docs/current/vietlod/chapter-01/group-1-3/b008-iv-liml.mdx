## 1.3.2.1. Methodological Meaning and Scope of Application

### 1.3.2.1.1. Econometric Foundation and Identification Mechanism

Estimates the Limited-Information Maximum Likelihood — LIML specification within the Classical Instrumental Variables framework by optimizing the structural objective function and computing heteroskedasticity- and cluster-robust asymptotic standard errors. Across empirical econometrics, `iv_liml` enables researchers to isolate structural parameters while controlling for confounding covariates and verifying identification assumptions via `base Stata` in Stata 18 MP, `AER / ivmodel` in R 4.x, and `linearmodels` in Python 3.11+.

### 1.3.2.1.2. Scope of Application and Sample Structure Requirements

Cross-sectional, pooled, or tabular microeconometric datasets requiring robust covariance estimation and specification testing. Mandatory post-estimation verification includes testing identification restrictions, evaluating goodness-of-fit metrics, and confirming numerical convergence across all three econometric engines.

### 1.3.2.1.3. Structural Equations and Asymptotic Covariance Specification

Core structural equation for `iv_liml`:

$$
\hat{\beta}_{\text{LIML}} = \arg\min_{\beta} \frac{(Y - Y_{\text{end}}\beta)^\top P_Z (Y - Y_{\text{end}}\beta)}{(Y - Y_{\text{end}}\beta)^\top M_Z (Y - Y_{\text{end}}\beta)}
$$

Estimator objective function and sample moment conditions:

$$
\hat{\beta}_{\text{IV}} = \left(X^\top P_Z X\right)^{-1} X^\top P_Z Y, \quad P_Z = Z\left(Z^\top Z\right)^{-1}Z^\top, \quad \text{E}\left[Z_i \varepsilon_i\right] = 0
$$

Asymptotic covariance matrix and post-estimation diagnostic metric:

$$
F_{\text{KP}} = \frac{\hat{\pi}^\top \widehat{\text{Var}}_{\text{rob}}(\hat{\pi})^{-1}\hat{\pi}}{L_1} \gt 10, \quad J_{\text{Hansen}} = N \cdot \bar{g}_N(\hat{\beta})^\top \hat{\Omega}_N^{-1}\bar{g}_N(\hat{\beta}) \xrightarrow{d} \chi^2_{L - K}
$$
