## 1.3.1. Methodological Overview and Structural Equations

- **Methodological Focus**: Provides consistent estimation and robust inference for **Classical Instrumental Variables** specifications within **Classical Linear and Instrumental Variables Regression**.
- **Mandatory Post-Estimation Diagnostics**: Kiểm định nội sinh Durbin–Wu–Hausman, thống kê $F$ giai đoạn 1 Kleibergen–Paap rk Wald $F$ vượt ngưỡng $10$ và kiểm định nhận dạng quá mức Sargan / Hansen $J$.

Core structural specification for Part 1.3:

$$
\hat{\beta}_{\text{2SLS}} = (X^\top P_Z X)^{-1} X^\top P_Z Y, \quad P_Z = Z(Z^\top Z)^{-1}Z^\top
$$

Estimator objective and moment conditions:

$$
\hat{\beta}_{\text{IV}} = \left(X^\top P_Z X\right)^{-1} X^\top P_Z Y, \quad P_Z = Z\left(Z^\top Z\right)^{-1}Z^\top, \quad \text{E}\left[Z_i \varepsilon_i\right] = 0
$$
