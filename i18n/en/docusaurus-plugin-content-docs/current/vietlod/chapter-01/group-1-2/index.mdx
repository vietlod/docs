## 1. Methodological Overview and Structural Equations

- **Methodological Focus**: Provides consistent estimation and robust inference for **Robust and Quantile Regression** specifications within **Classical Linear and Instrumental Variables Regression**.
- **Mandatory Post-Estimation Diagnostics**: Phân tích đòn bẩy $h_{ii}$, khoảng cách Cook's $D$, thống kê DFFITS, DFBETAS và kiểm định tính ổn định trọng số hội tụ IRLS.

Core structural specification for Group 1.2:

$$
\hat{\beta}_{\text{RLM}} = \arg\min_{\beta} \sum_{i=1}^N \rho\left(\frac{Y_i - X_i^\top \beta}{\hat{\sigma}}\right)
$$

Estimator objective and moment conditions:

$$
\hat{\beta}(\tau) = \arg\min_{\beta \in \mathbb{R}^K} \sum_{i=1}^N \rho_\tau\left(Y_i - X_i^\top \beta\right), \quad \rho_\tau(u) = u\left(\tau - \mathbb{I}(u \lt 0)\right)
$$
