## 1.1. Central Research Question and Core Mathematical Specification

- **Central Research Question**: How to estimate consistent linear causal effects under heteroskedasticity, outliers, multi-way high-dimensional fixed effects, and endogenous regressors?
- **Chapter Architecture**: Encompasses `4` specialized methodological parts from `1.1` to `1.4` and `14` empirical model lessons validated on Vietnamese datasets from [EcoData](https://ecodata.io.vn) and [Finco](https://www.finco.vn).

Core structural equation for Chapter 1:

$$
Y_i = X_i^\top \beta + \varepsilon_i, \quad \text{E}[Z_i \varepsilon_i] = 0
$$
