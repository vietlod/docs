## 1. Central Research Question and Core Mathematical Specification

- **Central Research Question**: How to estimate consistent linear causal effects under heteroskedasticity, outliers, multi-way high-dimensional fixed effects, and endogenous regressors?
- **Chapter Architecture**: Encompasses `4` specialized methodological groups and `14` empirical models validated on Vietnamese datasets from [EcoData](https://ecodata.io.vn) and [Finco](https://www.finco.vn).

Core structural equation for Chapter 01:

$$
Y_i = X_i^\top \beta + \varepsilon_i, \quad \text{E}[Z_i \varepsilon_i] = 0
$$

## 3. Four-Step Workflow for Selecting and Executing Models in Chapter 01

<Steps>

### Step 1. Select the Appropriate Methodological Group Across the 4 Groups

Match your economic hypothesis and sample structure against the identification requirements from Group `1.1` through Group `1.4`.

### Step 2. Prepare Standardized Datasets From EcoData or Finco

Download the standardized `.csv` practice file for your target estimator from `/src/ecoaz/data/` or extract live empirical series from [EcoData](https://ecodata.io.vn) and [Finco](https://www.finco.vn).

### Step 3. Estimate Parameters and Run Diagnostics in Stata 18, R 4.x, and Python 3.11+

Execute the tri-engine scripts provided in each model guide and verify the mandatory post-estimation diagnostics: **Breusch–Pagan, White, VIF, Ramsey RESET, Kleibergen–Paap rk Wald F, Montiel Olea–Pflueger Effective F, Hansen J**.

### Step 4. Verify Three-Software Convergence and Export Publication Tables on EcoLab

Confirm numerical convergence across Stata, R, and Python using the three-panel empirical figure and generate journal-grade tables on [EcoLab](https://ecolab.io.vn).

</Steps>
