## 1.4.3.1. Methodological Meaning and Scope of Application

### 1.4.3.1.1. Econometric Foundation and Identification Mechanism

Identifies causal effects from regional exposure shares combined with aggregate sectoral shocks. Across empirical econometrics, `bartik_shift_share_iv` enables researchers to isolate structural parameters while controlling for confounding covariates and verifying identification assumptions via `ssaggregate / ivreg2` in Stata 18 MP, `ShiftShareSE` in R 4.x, and `linearmodels` in Python 3.11+.

### 1.4.3.1.2. Scope of Application and Sample Structure Requirements

Exogenous initial exposure shares or conditionally exogenous aggregate shocks across sectors and regions. Mandatory post-estimation verification includes testing identification restrictions, evaluating goodness-of-fit metrics, and confirming numerical convergence across all three econometric engines.

### 1.4.3.1.3. Structural Equations and Asymptotic Covariance Specification

Core structural equation for `bartik_shift_share_iv`:

$$
Z_i^{\text{Bartik}} = \sum_{k=1}^K s_{ik,0} g_{kt}, \quad Y_{it} = \beta D_{it} + X_{it}^\top \gamma + \varepsilon_{it}, \quad \text{E}\left[\sum_{i=1}^N s_{ik,0}\varepsilon_{it} \;\middle|\; g_k\right] = 0
$$

Estimator objective function and sample moment conditions:

$$
\hat{\beta}_N = \arg\min_{\beta \in \mathbb{R}^K} \sum_{i=1}^N w_i \left(Y_i - X_i^\top \beta\right)^2, \quad \text{E}\left[X_i \varepsilon_i\right] = 0
$$

Asymptotic covariance matrix and post-estimation diagnostic metric:

$$
\widehat{\text{Var}}_{\text{HC1}}(\hat{\beta}_N) = \frac{N}{N-K}\left(X^\top X\right)^{-1}\left(\sum_{i=1}^N \hat{\varepsilon}_i^2 X_i X_i^\top\right)\left(X^\top X\right)^{-1}, \quad \text{VIF}_k = \frac{1}{1 - R_k^2}
$$
