## 1. Methodological Overview and Structural Equations

- **Methodological Focus**: Provides consistent estimation and robust inference for **High-Dimensional Fixed Effects and Modern Instrumental Variables** specifications within **Classical Linear and Instrumental Variables Regression**.
- **Mandatory Post-Estimation Diagnostics**: Kiểm định $F$ đồng thời các biến giải thích — within-$F$, kiểm cáo loại singleton, $R^2$ within/projected, VIF sau khử FE..

Core structural specification for Group 1.4:

$$
Y_{ijt} = X_{ijt}^\top \beta + \alpha_i + \gamma_j + \lambda_t + \varepsilon_{ijt}, \quad \tilde{Y} = M_D Y, \quad \hat{\beta}_{\text{HDFE}} = (\tilde{X}^\top \tilde{X})^{-1}\tilde{X}^\top \tilde{Y}
$$

Estimator objective and moment conditions:

$$
\hat{\beta}_N = \arg\min_{\beta \in \mathbb{R}^K} \sum_{i=1}^N w_i \left(Y_i - X_i^\top \beta\right)^2, \quad \text{E}\left[X_i \varepsilon_i\right] = 0
$$
