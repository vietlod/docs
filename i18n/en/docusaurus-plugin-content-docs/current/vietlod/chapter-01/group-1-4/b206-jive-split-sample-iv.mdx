## 1.4.9.1. Methodological Meaning and Scope of Application

### 1.4.9.1.1. Econometric Foundation and Identification Mechanism

Constructs leave-one-out predicted endogenous regressors using the Sherman-Morrison hat-matrix diagonal to purge finite-sample correlation between first-stage fitted noise and structural disturbances.

### 1.4.9.1.2. Scope of Application and Sample Structure Requirements

Instrumental variable designs with many weak instruments such as judge/examiner leniency or quarter-of-birth interacts where 2SLS suffers severe bias toward OLS.

- **Mandatory Pre-Estimation Diagnostics**: `first_stage_f_weak_iv, vif_multicollinearity`
- **Mandatory Post-Estimation Diagnostics**: `jive_leave_one_out_f, hansen_j_overidentification`

### 1.4.9.1.3. Structural Equations and Asymptotic Covariance Specification

Core structural equation for `jive_split_sample_iv`:

$$
Y_i = X_i^\top \beta + \varepsilon_i, \quad \mathbb{E}[Z_i \varepsilon_i] = 0
$$

Estimator objective function and sample moment conditions:

$$
\hat{\beta}_N = \arg\min_{\beta \in \mathbb{R}^K} \frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^N \rho\left(Y_i - X_i^\top \beta\right)
$$

Asymptotic covariance matrix and robust inference metric:

$$
\widehat{\text{Var}}(\hat{\beta}) = \left(X^\top P_Z X\right)^{-1} \left(\sum_{i=1}^N \hat{\varepsilon}_i^2 P_{Z,i} X_i X_i^\top P_{Z,i}^\top\right) \left(X^\top P_Z X\right)^{-1}
$$

# ECODATA replication & postestimation script - Jackknife and Split Sample Instrumental Variables (numpy::JackknifeIV)
# Family: classical_regression / instrumental_system | Key: jive_split_sample_iv
import pandas as pd
# df: a pandas DataFrame holding your selected variables
import numpy as np
Z = np.column_stack([np.ones(len(df)), df[['z1']].values])
X = np.column_stack([np.ones(len(df)), df[['x1', 'x2']].values])
P_Z = Z @ np.linalg.pinv(Z.T @ Z) @ Z.T
h = np.clip(np.diag(P_Z), 0.0, 0.95)
X_jive = ((P_Z @ X) - h[:, None] * X) / (1.0 - h[:, None])
b_jive = np.linalg.pinv(X_jive.T @ X) @ (X_jive.T @ df["y"].values)
print("JIVE1:", b_jive)
