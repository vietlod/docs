## 1. Methodological Meaning and Scope of Application

### 1.1. Econometric Foundation and Identification Mechanism

Estimates causal instrumental-variable regressions while partialling out multi-way high-dimensional fixed effects. Across empirical econometrics, `hdfe_iv` enables researchers to isolate structural parameters while controlling for confounding covariates and verifying identification assumptions via `ivreghdfe` in Stata 18 MP, `fixest` in R 4.x, and `pyfixest` in Python 3.11+.

### 1.2. Scope of Application and Sample Structure Requirements

At least one endogenous regressor and valid excluded instruments orthogonal to absorbed fixed effects. Mandatory post-estimation verification includes testing identification restrictions, evaluating goodness-of-fit metrics, and confirming numerical convergence across all three econometric engines.

### 1.3. Structural Equations and Asymptotic Covariance Specification

Core structural equation for `hdfe_iv`:

$$
Y_{ijt} = D_{ijt}^\top \delta + X_{ijt}^\top \beta + \sum_{g=1}^G \alpha_g + \varepsilon_{ijt}, \quad \hat{\delta}_{\text{HDFE-IV}} = (\tilde{D}^\top P_{\tilde{Z}}\tilde{D})^{-1}\tilde{D}^\top P_{\tilde{Z}}\tilde{Y}
$$

Estimator objective function and sample moment conditions:

$$
\hat{\beta}_N = \arg\min_{\beta \in \mathbb{R}^K} \sum_{i=1}^N w_i \left(Y_i - X_i^\top \beta\right)^2, \quad \text{E}\left[X_i \varepsilon_i\right] = 0
$$

Asymptotic covariance matrix and post-estimation diagnostic metric:

$$
\widehat{\text{Var}}_{\text{HC1}}(\hat{\beta}_N) = \frac{N}{N-K}\left(X^\top X\right)^{-1}\left(\sum_{i=1}^N \hat{\varepsilon}_i^2 X_i X_i^\top\right)\left(X^\top X\right)^{-1}, \quad \text{VIF}_k = \frac{1}{1 - R_k^2}
$$
