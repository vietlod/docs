## 1.4.10.1. Methodological Meaning and Scope of Application

### 1.4.10.1.1. Econometric Foundation and Identification Mechanism

Estimates linear systems under low-rank singular value restrictions on the coefficient matrix via canonical correlation projection, nesting cointegration rank and factor-pricing restrictions.

### 1.4.10.1.2. Scope of Application and Sample Structure Requirements

Multivariate macro-finance or demand systems where common latent factors restrict cross-equation response matrices to reduced rank.

- **Mandatory Pre-Estimation Diagnostics**: `vif_multicollinearity, predictor_scale_collinearity_precheck`
- **Mandatory Post-Estimation Diagnostics**: `anderson_canonical_rank_test, breusch_pagan_heteroskedasticity`

### 1.4.10.1.3. Structural Equations and Asymptotic Covariance Specification

Core structural equation for `reduced_rank_regression`:

$$
Y_i = X_i^\top \beta + \varepsilon_i, \quad \mathbb{E}[Z_i \varepsilon_i] = 0
$$

Estimator objective function and sample moment conditions:

$$
\hat{\beta}_N = \arg\min_{\beta \in \mathbb{R}^K} \frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^N \rho\left(Y_i - X_i^\top \beta\right)
$$

Asymptotic covariance matrix and robust inference metric:

$$
\widehat{\text{Var}}(\hat{\beta}) = \left(X^\top P_Z X\right)^{-1} \left(\sum_{i=1}^N \hat{\varepsilon}_i^2 P_{Z,i} X_i X_i^\top P_{Z,i}^\top\right) \left(X^\top P_Z X\right)^{-1}
$$
