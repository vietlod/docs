## 1.1.5.1. Methodological Meaning and Scope of Application

### 1.1.5.1.1. Econometric Foundation and Identification Mechanism

Estimates linear regression under exact linear restrictions R*beta = r via Lagrangian projection or convex box/inequality constraints via quadratic programming, testing homogeneity and adding-up restrictions.

### 1.1.5.1.2. Scope of Application and Sample Structure Requirements

Continuous dependent variable with linear equality restrictions such as constant returns to scale or Slutsky symmetry, or theoretical sign/bound constraints on structural elasticities.

- **Mandatory Pre-Estimation Diagnostics**: `vif_multicollinearity, design_matrix_rank_check`
- **Mandatory Post-Estimation Diagnostics**: `wald_linear_restriction, breusch_pagan_heteroskedasticity`

### 1.1.5.1.3. Structural Equations and Asymptotic Covariance Specification

Core structural equation for `constrained_least_squares`:

$$
Y_i = X_i^\top \beta + \varepsilon_i, \quad \mathbb{E}[Z_i \varepsilon_i] = 0
$$

Estimator objective function and sample moment conditions:

$$
\hat{\beta}_N = \arg\min_{\beta \in \mathbb{R}^K} \frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^N \rho\left(Y_i - X_i^\top \beta\right)
$$

Asymptotic covariance matrix and robust inference metric:

$$
\widehat{\text{Var}}(\hat{\beta}) = \left(X^\top P_Z X\right)^{-1} \left(\sum_{i=1}^N \hat{\varepsilon}_i^2 P_{Z,i} X_i X_i^\top P_{Z,i}^\top\right) \left(X^\top P_Z X\right)^{-1}
$$

# df: a pandas DataFrame holding your selected variables
import numpy as np, statsmodels.api as sm
X = sm.add_constant(df[['x1', 'x2']])
ols = sm.OLS(df["y"], X).fit(cov_type="HC1")
R = np.zeros((1, X.shape[1])); R[0, 1:] = 1.0
XtXi = np.linalg.pinv(X.T @ X)
b_cls = ols.params.values - XtXi @ R.T @ np.linalg.pinv(R @ XtXi @ R.T) @ (R @ ols.params.values - np.array([1.0]))
print("CLS:", dict(zip(X.columns, b_cls)))
