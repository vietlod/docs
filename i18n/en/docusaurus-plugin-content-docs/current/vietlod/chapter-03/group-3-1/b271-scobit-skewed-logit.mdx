## 3.1.7.1. Methodological Meaning and Scope of Application

### 3.1.7.1.1. Econometric Foundation and Identification Mechanism

Relaxes standard Logit's fixed inflection point at P = 0.5 via the Burr-II skewed logistic CDF P(y=1|X) = 1 - (1 + exp(X*beta))^(-alpha), testing symmetry H0: alpha = 1 by Likelihood Ratio.

### 3.1.7.1.2. Scope of Application and Sample Structure Requirements

Binary choice applications such as voter turnout, credit default, or technology adoption where maximal marginal sensitivity occurs away from probability 0.5.

- **Mandatory Pre-Estimation Diagnostics**: `outcome_balance_separation_precheck, vif_multicollinearity`
- **Mandatory Post-Estimation Diagnostics**: `hosmer_lemeshow_gof, lr_joint_significance`

### 3.1.7.1.3. Structural Equations and Asymptotic Covariance Specification

Core structural equation for `scobit_skewed_logit`:

$$
U_{ij} = V_{ij}(X_{ij}, \beta_i) + \varepsilon_{ij}, \quad P_{ij} = \mathbb{P}\left(U_{ij} \ge U_{ik}, \forall k \in \mathcal{J}\right)
$$

Estimator objective function and sample moment conditions:

$$
\hat{\theta}_{\text{MLE}} = \arg\max_{\theta} \sum_{i=1}^N \sum_{j \in \mathcal{J}} d_{ij} \ln P_{ij}(X_i; \theta)
$$

Asymptotic covariance matrix and robust inference metric:

$$
\widehat{\text{Var}}(\hat{\theta}) = \hat{H}(\hat{\theta})^{-1} \left(\sum_{i=1}^N \hat{s}_i(\hat{\theta})\hat{s}_i(\hat{\theta})^\top\right) \hat{H}(\hat{\theta})^{-1}
$$
