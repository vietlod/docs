## 3.1.4.1. Methodological Meaning and Scope of Application

### 3.1.4.1.1. Econometric Foundation and Identification Mechanism

Separates structural mean effects from group-varying error variance exp — Z*gamma — in binary choice models. Across empirical econometrics, `heteroskedastic_probit` enables researchers to isolate structural parameters while controlling for confounding covariates and verifying identification assumptions via `base Stata hetprobit` in Stata 18 MP, `glmx` in R 4.x, and `statsmodels` in Python 3.11+.

### 3.1.4.1.2. Scope of Application and Sample Structure Requirements

Binary [0, 1] outcome where unobserved error dispersion varies systematically across covariates. Mandatory post-estimation verification includes testing identification restrictions, evaluating goodness-of-fit metrics, and confirming numerical convergence across all three econometric engines.

### 3.1.4.1.3. Structural Equations and Asymptotic Covariance Specification

Core structural equation for `heteroskedastic_probit`:

$$
Y_i^\ast = X_i^\top \beta + \varepsilon_i, \quad \varepsilon_i \sim \mathcal{N}(0, \sigma_i^2), \quad \sigma_i = \exp(Z_i^\top \gamma), \quad \text{P}(Y_i = 1 \mid X_i, Z_i) = \Phi\left(\frac{X_i^\top \beta}{\exp(Z_i^\top \gamma)}\right)
$$

Estimator objective function and sample moment conditions:

$$
\hat{\theta}_{\text{MLE}} = \arg\max_{\theta \in \Theta} \sum_{i=1}^N \sum_{j=1}^J y_{ij}\ln P_{ij}(X_i; \theta), \quad \frac{\partial \ell_N(\hat{\theta})}{\partial \theta} = \sum_{i=1}^N s_i(Y_i, X_i; \hat{\theta}) = 0
$$

Asymptotic covariance matrix and post-estimation diagnostic metric:

$$
\text{AME}_k = \frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^N \frac{\partial \text{P}(Y_i = 1 \mid X_i; \hat{\theta})}{\partial X_{ik}}, \quad \text{LR} = 2\left(\ell_N(\hat{\theta}) - \ell_N(\tilde{\theta}_0)\right) \xrightarrow{d} \chi^2_{\text{df}}
$$
