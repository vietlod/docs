## 1. Methodological Overview and Structural Equations

- **Methodological Focus**: Provides consistent estimation and robust inference for **Ordered Choice Models** specifications within **Discrete Choice and Structural Demand Models**.
- **Mandatory Post-Estimation Diagnostics**: Kiểm định giả định đường song song của Brant và Wolfe–Gould, kiểm định tách biệt ngưỡng cắt $\kappa_j$ và (AME) cho từng mức.

Core structural specification for Group 3.3:

$$
P(Y_i \le j \mid X_i) = \Lambda(\kappa_j - X_i^\top \beta), \quad j = 1, \dots, J-1
$$

Estimator objective and moment conditions:

$$
\hat{\theta}_{\text{MLE}} = \arg\max_{\theta \in \Theta} \sum_{i=1}^N \sum_{j=1}^J y_{ij}\ln P_{ij}(X_i; \theta), \quad \frac{\partial \ell_N(\hat{\theta})}{\partial \theta} = \sum_{i=1}^N s_i(Y_i, X_i; \hat{\theta}) = 0
$$
