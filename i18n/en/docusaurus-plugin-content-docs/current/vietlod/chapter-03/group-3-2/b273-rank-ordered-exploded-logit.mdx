## 3.2.5.1. Methodological Meaning and Scope of Application

### 3.2.5.1.1. Econometric Foundation and Identification Mechanism

Decomposes complete or partial preference rankings into sequential conditional logit choices under Luce's choice axiom, maximizing the Plackett-Luce exploded random-utility partial log-likelihood.

### 3.2.5.1.2. Scope of Application and Sample Structure Requirements

Stated-preference conjoint surveys, school/university preference rankings, and multi-alternative ranked discrete choice experiments.

- **Mandatory Pre-Estimation Diagnostics**: `outcome_balance_separation_precheck, vif_multicollinearity`
- **Mandatory Post-Estimation Diagnostics**: `lr_joint_significance, aic_bic_information_criteria`

### 3.2.5.1.3. Structural Equations and Asymptotic Covariance Specification

Core structural equation for `rank_ordered_exploded_logit`:

$$
U_{ij} = V_{ij}(X_{ij}, \beta_i) + \varepsilon_{ij}, \quad P_{ij} = \mathbb{P}\left(U_{ij} \ge U_{ik}, \forall k \in \mathcal{J}\right)
$$

Estimator objective function and sample moment conditions:

$$
\hat{\theta}_{\text{MLE}} = \arg\max_{\theta} \sum_{i=1}^N \sum_{j \in \mathcal{J}} d_{ij} \ln P_{ij}(X_i; \theta)
$$

Asymptotic covariance matrix and robust inference metric:

$$
\widehat{\text{Var}}(\hat{\theta}) = \hat{H}(\hat{\theta})^{-1} \left(\sum_{i=1}^N \hat{s}_i(\hat{\theta})\hat{s}_i(\hat{\theta})^\top\right) \hat{H}(\hat{\theta})^{-1}
$$
