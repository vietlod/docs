## 1. Central Research Question and Core Mathematical Specification

- **Central Research Question**: How to model discrete economic decisions across binary, multinomial, ordered, random-parameter, and structural differentiated-product demand settings?
- **Chapter Architecture**: Encompasses `5` specialized methodological groups and `14` empirical models validated on Vietnamese datasets from [EcoData](https://ecodata.io.vn) and [Finco](https://www.finco.vn).

Core structural equation for Chapter 03:

$$
P(Y_i = j \mid X_i) = \frac{\exp(X_{ij}^\top \beta)}{\sum_{m=1}^J \exp(X_{im}^\top \beta)}
$$

## 3. Four-Step Workflow for Selecting and Executing Models in Chapter 03

<Steps>

### Step 1. Select the Appropriate Methodological Group Across the 5 Groups

Match your economic hypothesis and sample structure against the identification requirements from Group `3.1` through Group `3.5`.

### Step 2. Prepare Standardized Datasets From EcoData or Finco

Download the standardized `.csv` practice file for your target estimator from `/src/ecoaz/data/` or extract live empirical series from [EcoData](https://ecodata.io.vn) and [Finco](https://www.finco.vn).

### Step 3. Estimate Parameters and Run Diagnostics in Stata 18, R 4.x, and Python 3.11+

Execute the tri-engine scripts provided in each model guide and verify the mandatory post-estimation diagnostics: **Likelihood Ratio Chi2, Hosmer–Lemeshow, Hausman–McFadden IIA Test, Brant Parallel Odds Test, Average Marginal Effects (AME)**.

### Step 4. Verify Three-Software Convergence and Export Publication Tables on EcoLab

Confirm numerical convergence across Stata, R, and Python using the three-panel empirical figure and generate journal-grade tables on [EcoLab](https://ecolab.io.vn).

</Steps>
