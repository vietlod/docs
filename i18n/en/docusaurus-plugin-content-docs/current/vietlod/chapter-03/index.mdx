## 3.1. Central Research Question and Core Mathematical Specification

- **Central Research Question**: How to model discrete economic decisions across binary, multinomial, ordered, random-parameter, and structural differentiated-product demand settings?
- **Chapter Architecture**: Encompasses `5` specialized methodological parts from `3.1` to `3.5` and `14` empirical model lessons validated on Vietnamese datasets from [EcoData](https://ecodata.io.vn) and [Finco](https://www.finco.vn).

Core structural equation for Chapter 3:

$$
P(Y_i = j \mid X_i) = \frac{\exp(X_{ij}^\top \beta)}{\sum_{m=1}^J \exp(X_{im}^\top \beta)}
$$
