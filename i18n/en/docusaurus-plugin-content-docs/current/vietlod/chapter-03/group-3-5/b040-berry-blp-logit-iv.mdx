## 1. Methodological Meaning and Scope of Application

### 1.1. Econometric Foundation and Identification Mechanism

Inverts aggregate market shares ln — s_jt — - ln — s_0t — with BLP or cost-shifter instruments to recover structural demand elasticities and markups. Across empirical econometrics, `berry_blp_logit_iv` enables researchers to isolate structural parameters while controlling for confounding covariates and verifying identification assumptions via `blp / ivreghdfe` in Stata 18 MP, `fixest / BLPestimatoR` in R 4.x, and `pyblp / linearmodels` in Python 3.11+.

### 1.2. Scope of Application and Sample Structure Requirements

Product market shares strictly in — 0, 1 — with positive outside-good share s_0t &gt; 0, endogenous price, and valid BLP/cost instruments. Mandatory post-estimation verification includes testing identification restrictions, evaluating goodness-of-fit metrics, and confirming numerical convergence across all three econometric engines.

### 1.3. Structural Equations and Asymptotic Covariance Specification

Core structural equation for `berry_blp_logit_iv`:

$$
\ln(s_{jt}) - \ln(s_{0t}) = X_{jt}^\top \beta - \alpha p_{jt} + \sigma \ln(s_{j\mid g,t}) + \xi_{jt}, \quad \delta_{jt}^{h+1} = \delta_{jt}^h + \ln s_{jt} - \ln \hat{s}_{jt}(\delta_t^h, \theta_2)
$$

Estimator objective function and sample moment conditions:

$$
\hat{\theta}_{\text{MLE}} = \arg\max_{\theta \in \Theta} \sum_{i=1}^N \sum_{j=1}^J y_{ij}\ln P_{ij}(X_i; \theta), \quad \frac{\partial \ell_N(\hat{\theta})}{\partial \theta} = \sum_{i=1}^N s_i(Y_i, X_i; \hat{\theta}) = 0
$$

Asymptotic covariance matrix and post-estimation diagnostic metric:

$$
\text{AME}_k = \frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^N \frac{\partial \text{P}(Y_i = 1 \mid X_i; \hat{\theta})}{\partial X_{ik}}, \quad \text{LR} = 2\left(\ell_N(\hat{\theta}) - \ell_N(\tilde{\theta}_0)\right) \xrightarrow{d} \chi^2_{\text{df}}
$$
