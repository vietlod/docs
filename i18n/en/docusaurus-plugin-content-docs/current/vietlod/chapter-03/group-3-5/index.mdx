## 3.5.1. Methodological Overview and Structural Equations

- **Methodological Focus**: Provides consistent estimation and robust inference for **Panel Discrete Choice and Structural Demand** specifications within **Discrete Choice and Structural Demand Models**.
- **Mandatory Post-Estimation Diagnostics**: Kiểm định Hausman Panel FE Logit vs RE Logit, số đơn vị chuyển trạng thái thực chất — switchers count — cảnh báo loại các đơn vị không đổi $Y_{it} \equiv 0/1$, Average Partial Effects đã hiệu chỉnh chệch..

Core structural specification for Part 3.5:

$$
\ell_{\text{cond}}(\beta) = \sum_{i=1}^N \ln \frac{\exp\left(\sum_{t=1}^T Y_{it} X_{it}^\top \beta\right)}{\sum_{d_i \in \mathcal{B}_i} \exp\left(\sum_{t=1}^T d_{it} X_{it}^\top \beta\right)}, \quad \mathcal{B}_i = \left\{d_i \in \{0,1\}^T : \sum_{t=1}^T d_{it} = \sum_{t=1}^T Y_{it}\right\}
$$

Estimator objective and moment conditions:

$$
\hat{\theta}_{\text{MLE}} = \arg\max_{\theta \in \Theta} \sum_{i=1}^N \sum_{j=1}^J y_{ij}\ln P_{ij}(X_i; \theta), \quad \frac{\partial \ell_N(\hat{\theta})}{\partial \theta} = \sum_{i=1}^N s_i(Y_i, X_i; \hat{\theta}) = 0
$$
