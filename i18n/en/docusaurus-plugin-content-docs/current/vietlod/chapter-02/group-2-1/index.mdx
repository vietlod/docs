## 1. Methodological Overview and Structural Equations

- **Methodological Focus**: Provides consistent estimation and robust inference for **Penalized Shrinkage Regression** specifications within **Regularized, High-Dimensional Inference and Dimension Reduction Models**.
- **Mandatory Post-Estimation Diagnostics**: Đường cong kiểm định chéo K-Fold (MSE) theo tham số phạt $\lambda$, quỹ đạo nghiệm Ridge trace và bậc tự do hiệu dụng $\text{df}(\lambda) = \text{tr}[X(X^\top X + \lambda I)^{-1}X^\top]$.

Core structural specification for Group 2.1:

$$
\hat{\beta}_{\text{Ridge}} = (X^\top X + \lambda I_p)^{-1} X^\top Y
$$

Estimator objective and moment conditions:

$$
\hat{\beta}(\lambda, \alpha) = \arg\min_{\beta \in \mathbb{R}^p} \left\{\frac{1}{2N}\sum_{i=1}^N \left(Y_i - X_i^\top \beta\right)^2 + \lambda\left(\alpha \|\beta\|_1 + \frac{1-\alpha}{2}\|\beta\|_2^2\right)\right\}
$$
