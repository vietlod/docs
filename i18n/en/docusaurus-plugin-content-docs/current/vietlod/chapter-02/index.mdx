## 2.1. Central Research Question and Core Mathematical Specification

- **Central Research Question**: How to select variables, prevent overfitting, extract latent indices, and conduct valid post-selection causal inference when covariates are highly collinear or high-dimensional?
- **Chapter Architecture**: Encompasses `4` specialized methodological parts from `2.1` to `2.4` and `12` empirical model lessons validated on Vietnamese datasets from [EcoData](https://ecodata.io.vn) and [Finco](https://www.finco.vn).

Core structural equation for Chapter 2:

$$
\hat{\beta} = \arg\min_{\beta} \frac{1}{2N}\|Y - X\beta\|_2^2 + \lambda \left(\alpha \|\beta\|_1 + \frac{1-\alpha}{2}\|\beta\|_2^2\right)
$$
