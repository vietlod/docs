## 1. Central Research Question and Core Mathematical Specification

- **Central Research Question**: How to select variables, prevent overfitting, extract latent indices, and conduct valid post-selection causal inference when covariates are highly collinear or high-dimensional?
- **Chapter Architecture**: Encompasses `4` specialized methodological groups and `12` empirical models validated on Vietnamese datasets from [EcoData](https://ecodata.io.vn) and [Finco](https://www.finco.vn).

Core structural equation for Chapter 02:

$$
\hat{\beta} = \arg\min_{\beta} \frac{1}{2N}\|Y - X\beta\|_2^2 + \lambda \left(\alpha \|\beta\|_1 + \frac{1-\alpha}{2}\|\beta\|_2^2\right)
$$

## 3. Four-Step Workflow for Selecting and Executing Models in Chapter 02

<Steps>

### Step 1. Select the Appropriate Methodological Group Across the 4 Groups

Match your economic hypothesis and sample structure against the identification requirements from Group `2.1` through Group `2.4`.

### Step 2. Prepare Standardized Datasets From EcoData or Finco

Download the standardized `.csv` practice file for your target estimator from `/src/ecoaz/data/` or extract live empirical series from [EcoData](https://ecodata.io.vn) and [Finco](https://www.finco.vn).

### Step 3. Estimate Parameters and Run Diagnostics in Stata 18, R 4.x, and Python 3.11+

Execute the tri-engine scripts provided in each model guide and verify the mandatory post-estimation diagnostics: **10-Fold Cross-Validation MSE, Sparsity Ratio, KMO & Bartlett Test, Bai–Ng ICp2, Silhouette Score**.

### Step 4. Verify Three-Software Convergence and Export Publication Tables on EcoLab

Confirm numerical convergence across Stata, R, and Python using the three-panel empirical figure and generate journal-grade tables on [EcoLab](https://ecolab.io.vn).

</Steps>
