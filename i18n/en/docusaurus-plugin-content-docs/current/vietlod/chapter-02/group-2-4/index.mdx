## 1. Methodological Overview and Structural Equations

- **Methodological Focus**: Provides consistent estimation and robust inference for **Economic Regime Clustering** specifications within **Regularized, High-Dimensional Inference and Dimension Reduction Models**.
- **Mandatory Post-Estimation Diagnostics**: Hệ số Silhouette trung bình, chỉ số Calinski–Harabasz — pseudo-$F$, chỉ số Davies–Bouldin, kiểm định ANOVA/$F$ phân biệt tâm cụm..

Core structural specification for Group 2.4:

$$
\min_{\{C_k, \mu_k\}_{k=1}^K} \sum_{k=1}^K \sum_{i \in C_k} \|X_i - \mu_k\|_2^2, \quad s(i) = \frac{b(i) - a(i)}{\max\{a(i), b(i)\}}
$$

Estimator objective and moment conditions:

$$
\hat{\beta}(\lambda, \alpha) = \arg\min_{\beta \in \mathbb{R}^p} \left\{\frac{1}{2N}\sum_{i=1}^N \left(Y_i - X_i^\top \beta\right)^2 + \lambda\left(\alpha \|\beta\|_1 + \frac{1-\alpha}{2}\|\beta\|_2^2\right)\right\}
$$
