## 1. Methodological Meaning and Scope of Application

### 1.1. Econometric Foundation and Identification Mechanism

Partitions provinces, firms, or countries into homogeneous structural regimes on standardized multi-dimensional indicators. Across empirical econometrics, `kmeans_clustering` enables researchers to isolate structural parameters while controlling for confounding covariates and verifying identification assumptions via `base Stata cluster` in Stata 18 MP, `stats + cluster` in R 4.x, and `scikit-learn` in Python 3.11+.

### 1.2. Scope of Application and Sample Structure Requirements

At least two continuous economic indicators requiring unsupervised typology classification. Mandatory post-estimation verification includes testing identification restrictions, evaluating goodness-of-fit metrics, and confirming numerical convergence across all three econometric engines.

### 1.3. Structural Equations and Asymptotic Covariance Specification

Core structural equation for `kmeans_clustering`:

$$
\min_{\{C_k, \mu_k\}_{k=1}^K} \sum_{k=1}^K \sum_{i \in C_k} \|X_i - \mu_k\|_2^2, \quad s(i) = \frac{b(i) - a(i)}{\max\{a(i), b(i)\}}
$$

Estimator objective function and sample moment conditions:

$$
\hat{\beta}(\lambda, \alpha) = \arg\min_{\beta \in \mathbb{R}^p} \left\{\frac{1}{2N}\sum_{i=1}^N \left(Y_i - X_i^\top \beta\right)^2 + \lambda\left(\alpha \|\beta\|_1 + \frac{1-\alpha}{2}\|\beta\|_2^2\right)\right\}
$$

Asymptotic covariance matrix and post-estimation diagnostic metric:

$$
\text{CV}_K(\lambda) = \frac{1}{K}\sum_{k=1}^K \frac{1}{|I_k|}\sum_{i \in I_k}\left(Y_i - X_i^\top \hat{\beta}^{(-k)}(\lambda)\right)^2, \quad \lambda_{\text{opt}} = \arg\min_{\lambda}\text{CV}_K(\lambda)
$$
