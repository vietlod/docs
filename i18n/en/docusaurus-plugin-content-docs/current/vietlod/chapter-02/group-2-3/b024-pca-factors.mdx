## 1. Methodological Meaning and Scope of Application

### 1.1. Econometric Foundation and Identification Mechanism

Extracts orthogonal latent principal components and builds variance-weighted composite indices with KMO and Bai-Ng diagnostics. Across empirical econometrics, `pca_factors` enables researchers to isolate structural parameters while controlling for confounding covariates and verifying identification assumptions via `base Stata pca` in Stata 18 MP, `stats + psych` in R 4.x, and `scikit-learn` in Python 3.11+.

### 1.2. Scope of Application and Sample Structure Requirements

At least two correlated continuous indicators suitable for dimension reduction or index aggregation. Mandatory post-estimation verification includes testing identification restrictions, evaluating goodness-of-fit metrics, and confirming numerical convergence across all three econometric engines.

### 1.3. Structural Equations and Asymptotic Covariance Specification

Core structural equation for `pca_factors`:

$$
\Sigma_X = V \Lambda V^\top, \quad F_m = X_{\text{std}} v_m, \quad \text{KMO} = \frac{\sum_{j\neq k} r_{jk}^2}{\sum_{j\neq k} r_{jk}^2 + \sum_{j\neq k} p_{jk}^2}
$$

Estimator objective function and sample moment conditions:

$$
\hat{\beta}(\lambda, \alpha) = \arg\min_{\beta \in \mathbb{R}^p} \left\{\frac{1}{2N}\sum_{i=1}^N \left(Y_i - X_i^\top \beta\right)^2 + \lambda\left(\alpha \|\beta\|_1 + \frac{1-\alpha}{2}\|\beta\|_2^2\right)\right\}
$$

Asymptotic covariance matrix and post-estimation diagnostic metric:

$$
\text{CV}_K(\lambda) = \frac{1}{K}\sum_{k=1}^K \frac{1}{|I_k|}\sum_{i \in I_k}\left(Y_i - X_i^\top \hat{\beta}^{(-k)}(\lambda)\right)^2, \quad \lambda_{\text{opt}} = \arg\min_{\lambda}\text{CV}_K(\lambda)
$$
