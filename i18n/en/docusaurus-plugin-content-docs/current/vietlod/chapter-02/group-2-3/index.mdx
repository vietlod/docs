## 2.3.1. Methodological Overview and Structural Equations

- **Methodological Focus**: Provides consistent estimation and robust inference for **Dimension Reduction and Latent Factors** specifications within **Regularized, High-Dimensional Inference and Dimension Reduction Models**.
- **Mandatory Post-Estimation Diagnostics**: Tỷ lệ phương sai giải thích tích lũy đạt từ $80\%$ trở lên, biểu đồ Scree plot trị riêng và K-Fold (CV) (MSE) để chọn số thành phần $M$.

Core structural specification for Part 2.3:

$$
Z_m = X V_m, \quad \hat{\beta}_{\text{PCR}} = V_m (Z_m^\top Z_m)^{-1} Z_m^\top Y
$$

Estimator objective and moment conditions:

$$
\hat{\beta}(\lambda, \alpha) = \arg\min_{\beta \in \mathbb{R}^p} \left\{\frac{1}{2N}\sum_{i=1}^N \left(Y_i - X_i^\top \beta\right)^2 + \lambda\left(\alpha \|\beta\|_1 + \frac{1-\alpha}{2}\|\beta\|_2^2\right)\right\}
$$
