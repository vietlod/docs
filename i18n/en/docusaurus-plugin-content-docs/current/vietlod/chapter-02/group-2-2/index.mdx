## 1. Methodological Overview and Structural Equations

- **Methodological Focus**: Provides consistent estimation and robust inference for **High-Dimensional Model Selection and Causal Inference** specifications within **Regularized, High-Dimensional Inference and Dimension Reduction Models**.
- **Mandatory Post-Estimation Diagnostics**: Tiêu chuẩn thông tin Mallows $C_p$ dọc theo đường dẫn (LARS), K-Fold Cross-Validation (MSE) và đồ thị tiến hóa hệ số chuẩn hóa.

Core structural specification for Group 2.2:

$$
\hat{\beta}(\lambda) \text{ tiến hóa dọc theo đường đẳng góc } |x_j^\top r(\lambda)| = \lambda
$$

Estimator objective and moment conditions:

$$
\hat{\beta}(\lambda, \alpha) = \arg\min_{\beta \in \mathbb{R}^p} \left\{\frac{1}{2N}\sum_{i=1}^N \left(Y_i - X_i^\top \beta\right)^2 + \lambda\left(\alpha \|\beta\|_1 + \frac{1-\alpha}{2}\|\beta\|_2^2\right)\right\}
$$
