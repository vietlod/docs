## 2.2.4.1. Methodological Meaning and Scope of Application

### 2.2.4.1.1. Econometric Foundation and Identification Mechanism

Selects controls predictive of either outcome Y or focal regressor D via two Lasso steps before valid post-selection OLS inference. Across empirical econometrics, `post_lasso_ols` enables researchers to isolate structural parameters while controlling for confounding covariates and verifying identification assumptions via `Stata 18 dsregress` in Stata 18 MP, `hdm` in R 4.x, and `doubleml` in Python 3.11+.

### 2.2.4.1.2. Scope of Application and Sample Structure Requirements

Causal inference with many candidate confounding controls relative to sample size. Mandatory post-estimation verification includes testing identification restrictions, evaluating goodness-of-fit metrics, and confirming numerical convergence across all three econometric engines.

### 2.2.4.1.3. Structural Equations and Asymptotic Covariance Specification

Core structural equation for `post_lasso_ols`:

$$
Y_i = \alpha_0 D_i + g_0(X_i) + \varepsilon_i, \quad D_i = m_0(X_i) + v_i, \quad \hat{I} = \hat{I}_Y \cup \hat{I}_D \implies \text{OLS}(Y \sim D + X_{\hat{I}})
$$

Estimator objective function and sample moment conditions:

$$
\hat{\beta}(\lambda, \alpha) = \arg\min_{\beta \in \mathbb{R}^p} \left\{\frac{1}{2N}\sum_{i=1}^N \left(Y_i - X_i^\top \beta\right)^2 + \lambda\left(\alpha \|\beta\|_1 + \frac{1-\alpha}{2}\|\beta\|_2^2\right)\right\}
$$

Asymptotic covariance matrix and post-estimation diagnostic metric:

$$
\text{CV}_K(\lambda) = \frac{1}{K}\sum_{k=1}^K \frac{1}{|I_k|}\sum_{i \in I_k}\left(Y_i - X_i^\top \hat{\beta}^{(-k)}(\lambda)\right)^2, \quad \lambda_{\text{opt}} = \arg\min_{\lambda}\text{CV}_K(\lambda)
$$
