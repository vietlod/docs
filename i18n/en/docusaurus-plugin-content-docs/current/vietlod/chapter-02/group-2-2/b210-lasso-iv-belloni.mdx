## 2.2.7.1. Methodological Meaning and Scope of Application

### 2.2.7.1.1. Econometric Foundation and Identification Mechanism

Selects optimal sparse instruments from high-dimensional dictionary expansions via cross-validated L1 penalization, then computes post-Lasso 2SLS for root-N asymptotically normal structural inference.

### 2.2.7.1.2. Scope of Application and Sample Structure Requirements

Endogenous regressors accompanied by high-dimensional candidate instruments, technical interactions, or spline bases where unpenalized 2SLS overfits.

- **Mandatory Pre-Estimation Diagnostics**: `predictor_scale_collinearity_precheck, first_stage_f_weak_iv`
- **Mandatory Post-Estimation Diagnostics**: `belloni_post_lasso_support, hansen_j_overidentification`

### 2.2.7.1.3. Structural Equations and Asymptotic Covariance Specification

Core structural equation for `lasso_iv_belloni`:

$$
\hat{\beta}(\lambda, \alpha) = \arg\min_{\beta \in \mathbb{R}^p} \left\{\frac{1}{2N}\|Y - X\beta\|_2^2 + \lambda \left(\alpha \|\beta\|_1 + \frac{1-\alpha}{2}\|\beta\|_2^2\right)\right\}
$$

Estimator objective function and sample moment conditions:

$$
\psi(W_i; \theta, \eta) = (Y_i - \ell_0(Z_i) - \theta(D_i - m_0(Z_i)))(D_i - m_0(Z_i)), \quad \mathbb{E}[\psi(W_i; \theta_0, \eta_0)] = 0
$$

Asymptotic covariance matrix and robust inference metric:

$$
\sqrt{N}(\hat{\theta} - \theta_0) \xrightarrow{d} \mathcal{N}\left(0, J_0^{-2}\mathbb{E}[\psi^2(W_i; \theta_0, \eta_0)]\right)
$$
