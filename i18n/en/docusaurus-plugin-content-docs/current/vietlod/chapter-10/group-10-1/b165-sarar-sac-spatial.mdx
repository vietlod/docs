## 10.1.5.1. Methodological Meaning and Scope of Application

### 10.1.5.1.1. Econometric Foundation and Identification Mechanism

Jointly estimates endogenous outcome spillovers rho*W*Y and spatially autocorrelated unobserved shocks u = lambda*W*u + e via Kelejian-Prucha heteroskedastic GS2SLS with LeSage-Pace trace-adjusted impacts. Across empirical econometrics, `sarar_sac_spatial` enables researchers to isolate structural parameters while controlling for confounding covariates and verifying identification assumptions via `base Stata sp` in Stata 18 MP, `spatialreg` in R 4.x, and `spreg` in Python 3.11+.

### 10.1.5.1.2. Scope of Application and Sample Structure Requirements

Regional growth, real-estate, environmental, or agricultural datasets where both Robust LM-Lag and Robust LM-Error diagnostics are active. Mandatory post-estimation verification includes testing identification restrictions, evaluating goodness-of-fit metrics, and confirming numerical convergence across all three econometric engines.

### 10.1.5.1.3. Structural Equations and Asymptotic Covariance Specification

Core structural equation for `sarar_sac_spatial`:

$$
Y = \rho W_1 Y + X\beta + u, \quad u = \lambda W_2 u + \varepsilon, \quad \varepsilon \sim \mathcal{N}(0, \sigma^2 I_N)
$$

Estimator objective function and sample moment conditions:

$$
\ell_N(\beta, \rho, \sigma^2) = -\frac{N}{2}\ln(2\pi\sigma^2) + \ln\det\left(I_N - \rho W\right) - \frac{1}{2\sigma^2}\| (I_N - \rho W)Y - X\beta - WX\theta \|_2^2
$$

Asymptotic covariance matrix and post-estimation diagnostic metric:

$$
S_k(W) = \left(I_N - \rho W\right)^{-1}\left(I_N \beta_k + W \theta_k\right), \quad \bar{M}_{\text{direct}} = \frac{1}{N}\text{tr}(S_k(W)), \quad \bar{M}_{\text{indirect}} = \frac{1}{N}\mathbf{1}_N^\top S_k(W)\mathbf{1}_N - \bar{M}_{\text{direct}}
$$
