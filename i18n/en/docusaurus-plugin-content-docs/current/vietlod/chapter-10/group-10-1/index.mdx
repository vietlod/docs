## 10.1.1. Methodological Overview and Structural Equations

- **Methodological Focus**: Provides consistent estimation and robust inference for **Cross-Sectional Spatial Regression** specifications within **Spatial Econometrics, Conley Standard Errors and Dyadic Networks**.
- **Mandatory Post-Estimation Diagnostics**: Bảng phân rã Direct, Indirect — Spillover $\theta$ — và Total Effects kèm $p$-value, kiểm định Wald đồng thời $H_0: \theta = 0$, kiểm định Moran's $I$ & LM-Error trên phần dư..

Core structural specification for Part 10.1:

$$
Y = X\beta + W X \theta + \varepsilon, \quad \frac{\partial \text{E}[Y]}{\partial X_k^\top} = I_N \beta_k + W \theta_k
$$

Estimator objective and moment conditions:

$$
\ell_N(\beta, \rho, \sigma^2) = -\frac{N}{2}\ln(2\pi\sigma^2) + \ln\det\left(I_N - \rho W\right) - \frac{1}{2\sigma^2}\| (I_N - \rho W)Y - X\beta - WX\theta \|_2^2
$$
