## 1. Central Research Question and Core Mathematical Specification

- **Central Research Question**: How to estimate direct and indirect spatial spillover effects, multiscale geographic heterogeneity, spatial HAC standard errors, and peer network interactions?
- **Chapter Architecture**: Encompasses `4` specialized methodological groups and `10` empirical models validated on Vietnamese datasets from [EcoData](https://ecodata.io.vn) and [Finco](https://www.finco.vn).

Core structural equation for Chapter 10:

$$
Y = \rho W Y + X\beta + W X \theta + u, \quad u = \lambda W u + \varepsilon
$$

## 3. Four-Step Workflow for Selecting and Executing Models in Chapter 10

<Steps>

### Step 1. Select the Appropriate Methodological Group Across the 4 Groups

Match your economic hypothesis and sample structure against the identification requirements from Group `10.1` through Group `10.4`.

### Step 2. Prepare Standardized Datasets From EcoData or Finco

Download the standardized `.csv` practice file for your target estimator from `/src/ecoaz/data/` or extract live empirical series from [EcoData](https://ecodata.io.vn) and [Finco](https://www.finco.vn).

### Step 3. Estimate Parameters and Run Diagnostics in Stata 18, R 4.x, and Python 3.11+

Execute the tri-engine scripts provided in each model guide and verify the mandatory post-estimation diagnostics: **Global Moran's I & Local LISA, Anselin LM-Lag & Robust LM-Error, LeSage–Pace Direct/Indirect/Total Impacts, AICc Bandwidth Selection, Conley Distance Cutoff Sensitivity**.

### Step 4. Verify Three-Software Convergence and Export Publication Tables on EcoLab

Confirm numerical convergence across Stata, R, and Python using the three-panel empirical figure and generate journal-grade tables on [EcoLab](https://ecolab.io.vn).

</Steps>
