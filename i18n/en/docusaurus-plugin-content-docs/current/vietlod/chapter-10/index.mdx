## 10.1. Central Research Question and Core Mathematical Specification

- **Central Research Question**: How to estimate direct and indirect spatial spillover effects, multiscale geographic heterogeneity, spatial HAC standard errors, and peer network interactions?
- **Chapter Architecture**: Encompasses `4` specialized methodological parts from `10.1` to `10.4` and `10` empirical model lessons validated on Vietnamese datasets from [EcoData](https://ecodata.io.vn) and [Finco](https://www.finco.vn).

Core structural equation for Chapter 10:

$$
Y = \rho W Y + X\beta + W X \theta + u, \quad u = \lambda W u + \varepsilon
$$
