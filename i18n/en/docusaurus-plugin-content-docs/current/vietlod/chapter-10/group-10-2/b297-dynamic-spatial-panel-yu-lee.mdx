## 10.2.3.1. Methodological Meaning and Scope of Application

### 10.2.3.1.1. Econometric Foundation and Identification Mechanism

Estimates space-time dynamic panel regressions y_nt = tau*y_\{n,t-1\} + rho*W*y_nt + X_nt*beta + c_n + a_t + v_nt via concentrated log-determinant QMLE with analytical O(1/T) Nickell-spatial bias correction.

### 10.2.3.1.2. Scope of Application and Sample Structure Requirements

Regional growth, housing prices, fiscal competition, and environmental spillover panels exhibiting both state persistence and contemporaneous spatial autocorrelation.

- **Mandatory Pre-Estimation Diagnostics**: `morans_i_spatial_autocorr, lm_sar_vs_sem_robust`
- **Mandatory Post-Estimation Diagnostics**: `spatial_residual_morans_i, direct_indirect_spillover_wald`

### 10.2.3.1.3. Structural Equations and Asymptotic Covariance Specification

Core structural equation for `dynamic_spatial_panel_yu_lee`:

$$
Y = \rho W Y + X\beta + W X \theta + u, \quad u = \lambda M u + \varepsilon
$$

Estimator objective function and sample moment conditions:

$$
\frac{\partial \mathbb{E}[Y]}{\partial X_k^\top} = (I_N - \rho W)^{-1}\left(I_N \beta_k + W \theta_k\right)
$$

Asymptotic covariance matrix and robust inference metric:

$$
\widehat{\text{Var}}_{\text{Conley}}(\hat{\beta}) = (X^\top X)^{-1}\left(\sum_{i=1}^N \sum_{j=1}^N K(d_{ij}/\bar{d}) X_i \hat{u}_i \hat{u}_j X_j^\top\right)(X^\top X)^{-1}
$$
