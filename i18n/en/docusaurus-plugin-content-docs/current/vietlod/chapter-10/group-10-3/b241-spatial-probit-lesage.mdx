## 10.3.4.1. Methodological Meaning and Scope of Application

### 10.3.4.1.1. Econometric Foundation and Identification Mechanism

Models interdependent binary decisions y_i = 1(rho*W*y* + X*beta + e &gt; 0) across neighboring units with spatial lag feedback and heteroskedasticity-consistent latent spatial testing.

### 10.3.4.1.2. Scope of Application and Sample Structure Requirements

Binary adoption, market entry, sovereign default, or municipal tax competition where peer/neighbor choices create spatial strategic interdependence.

- **Mandatory Pre-Estimation Diagnostics**: `outcome_separation_balance_precheck, moran_i_spatial_autocorrelation`
- **Mandatory Post-Estimation Diagnostics**: `lesage_spatial_lag_wald, hosmer_lemeshow_goodness_of_fit`

### 10.3.4.1.3. Structural Equations and Asymptotic Covariance Specification

Core structural equation for `spatial_probit_lesage`:

$$
Y = \rho W Y + X\beta + W X \theta + u, \quad u = \lambda M u + \varepsilon
$$

Estimator objective function and sample moment conditions:

$$
\frac{\partial \mathbb{E}[Y]}{\partial X_k^\top} = (I_N - \rho W)^{-1}\left(I_N \beta_k + W \theta_k\right)
$$

Asymptotic covariance matrix and robust inference metric:

$$
\widehat{\text{Var}}_{\text{Conley}}(\hat{\beta}) = (X^\top X)^{-1}\left(\sum_{i=1}^N \sum_{j=1}^N K(d_{ij}/\bar{d}) X_i \hat{u}_i \hat{u}_j X_j^\top\right)(X^\top X)^{-1}
$$
