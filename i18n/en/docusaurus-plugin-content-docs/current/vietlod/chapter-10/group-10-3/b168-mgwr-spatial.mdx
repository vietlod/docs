## 1. Methodological Meaning and Scope of Application

### 1.1. Econometric Foundation and Identification Mechanism

Allows regression parameters beta_k — u_i, v_i — to vary continuously over geographic coordinates with covariate-specific optimal AICc spatial bandwidths via backfitting. Across empirical econometrics, `mgwr_spatial` enables researchers to isolate structural parameters while controlling for confounding covariates and verifying identification assumptions via `gwr` in Stata 18 MP, `GWmodel` in R 4.x, and `mgwr` in Python 3.11+.

### 1.2. Scope of Application and Sample Structure Requirements

Georeferenced cross-sectional datasets with latitude and longitude coordinates exhibiting spatial parameter non-stationarity. Mandatory post-estimation verification includes testing identification restrictions, evaluating goodness-of-fit metrics, and confirming numerical convergence across all three econometric engines.

### 1.3. Structural Equations and Asymptotic Covariance Specification

Core structural equation for `mgwr_spatial`:

$$
Y_i = \beta_{b_0}(u_i, v_i) + \sum_{k=1}^K \beta_{b_k}(u_i, v_i) X_{ik} + \varepsilon_i, \quad \hat{\beta}(u_i, v_i) = \left(X^\top W(u_i, v_i) X\right)^{-1}X^\top W(u_i, v_i)Y
$$

Estimator objective function and sample moment conditions:

$$
\ell_N(\beta, \rho, \sigma^2) = -\frac{N}{2}\ln(2\pi\sigma^2) + \ln\det\left(I_N - \rho W\right) - \frac{1}{2\sigma^2}\| (I_N - \rho W)Y - X\beta - WX\theta \|_2^2
$$

Asymptotic covariance matrix and post-estimation diagnostic metric:

$$
S_k(W) = \left(I_N - \rho W\right)^{-1}\left(I_N \beta_k + W \theta_k\right), \quad \bar{M}_{\text{direct}} = \frac{1}{N}\text{tr}(S_k(W)), \quad \bar{M}_{\text{indirect}} = \frac{1}{N}\mathbf{1}_N^\top S_k(W)\mathbf{1}_N - \bar{M}_{\text{direct}}
$$
