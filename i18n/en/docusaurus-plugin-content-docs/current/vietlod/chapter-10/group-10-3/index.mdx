## 1. Methodological Overview and Structural Equations

- **Methodological Focus**: Provides consistent estimation and robust inference for **Multiscale Geographically Weighted Regression and Conley HAC** specifications within **Spatial Econometrics, Conley Standard Errors and Dyadic Networks**.
- **Mandatory Post-Estimation Diagnostics**: Băng thông tối ưu riêng $bw_k$ cho từng biến, $\text{AIC}_c$ so sánh MGWR vs GWR vs Global OLS, kiểm định Monte-Carlo / Leung $F_3$ về sự biến thiên không gian thực sự của từng $\beta_k(u_i, v_i)$..

Core structural specification for Group 10.3:

$$
Y_i = \beta_{b_0}(u_i, v_i) + \sum_{k=1}^K \beta_{b_k}(u_i, v_i) X_{ik} + \varepsilon_i, \quad \hat{\beta}(u_i, v_i) = \left(X^\top W(u_i, v_i) X\right)^{-1}X^\top W(u_i, v_i)Y
$$

Estimator objective and moment conditions:

$$
\ell_N(\beta, \rho, \sigma^2) = -\frac{N}{2}\ln(2\pi\sigma^2) + \ln\det\left(I_N - \rho W\right) - \frac{1}{2\sigma^2}\| (I_N - \rho W)Y - X\beta - WX\theta \|_2^2
$$
