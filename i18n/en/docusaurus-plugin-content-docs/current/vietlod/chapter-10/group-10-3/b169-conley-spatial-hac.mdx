## 1. Methodological Meaning and Scope of Application

### 1.1. Econometric Foundation and Identification Mechanism

Nonparametric covariance estimator allowing arbitrary error correlation within Haversine distance cutoff d_km — plus Newey-West temporal lags — or across overlapping nodes in bilateral dyadic networks. Across empirical econometrics, `conley_spatial_hac` enables researchers to isolate structural parameters while controlling for confounding covariates and verifying identification assumptions via `acreg` in Stata 18 MP, `fixest` in R 4.x, and `statsmodels` in Python 3.11+.

### 1.2. Scope of Application and Sample Structure Requirements

Georeferenced GPS micro/regional datasets — latitude, longitude — without an ad-hoc W matrix, or bilateral origin-destination dyadic trade, migration, and interbank datasets. Mandatory post-estimation verification includes testing identification restrictions, evaluating goodness-of-fit metrics, and confirming numerical convergence across all three econometric engines.

### 1.3. Structural Equations and Asymptotic Covariance Specification

Core structural equation for `conley_spatial_hac`:

$$
\widehat{\text{Var}}_{\text{Conley}}(\hat{\beta}) = (X^\top X)^{-1}\left(\sum_{i=1}^N \sum_{j=1}^N K\left(\frac{d_{ij}}{d_{\max}}\right) X_i \hat{\varepsilon}_i \hat{\varepsilon}_j X_j^\top\right)(X^\top X)^{-1}
$$

Estimator objective function and sample moment conditions:

$$
\ell_N(\beta, \rho, \sigma^2) = -\frac{N}{2}\ln(2\pi\sigma^2) + \ln\det\left(I_N - \rho W\right) - \frac{1}{2\sigma^2}\| (I_N - \rho W)Y - X\beta - WX\theta \|_2^2
$$

Asymptotic covariance matrix and post-estimation diagnostic metric:

$$
S_k(W) = \left(I_N - \rho W\right)^{-1}\left(I_N \beta_k + W \theta_k\right), \quad \bar{M}_{\text{direct}} = \frac{1}{N}\text{tr}(S_k(W)), \quad \bar{M}_{\text{indirect}} = \frac{1}{N}\mathbf{1}_N^\top S_k(W)\mathbf{1}_N - \bar{M}_{\text{direct}}
$$
