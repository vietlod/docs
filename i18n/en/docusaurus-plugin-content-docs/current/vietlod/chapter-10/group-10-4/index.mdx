- **Methodological Focus**: Provides consistent estimation and robust inference for **Network Econometrics and Dyadic Regression** specifications within **Spatial Econometrics, Conley Standard Errors and Dyadic Networks**.

Core structural specification for Part 10.4:

$$
Y = \alpha \iota + \beta G Y + X\gamma + G X \delta + \varepsilon
$$

Estimator objective and moment conditions:

$$
\ell_N(\beta, \rho, \sigma^2) = -\frac{N}{2}\ln(2\pi\sigma^2) + \ln\det\left(I_N - \rho W\right) - \frac{1}{2\sigma^2}\| (I_N - \rho W)Y - X\beta - WX\theta \|_2^2
$$
