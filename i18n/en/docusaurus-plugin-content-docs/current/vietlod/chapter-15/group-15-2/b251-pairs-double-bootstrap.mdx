## 15.2.4.1. Methodological Meaning and Scope of Application

### 15.2.4.1.1. Econometric Foundation and Identification Mechanism

Resamples full observation pairs (y_i, x_i) with replacement alongside HC1 studentized pivotal t-statistics to achieve Edgeworth asymptotic refinement under arbitrary heteroskedasticity.

### 15.2.4.1.2. Scope of Application and Sample Structure Requirements

Cross-sectional regressions with skewed covariates, small samples, or unknown conditional heteroskedasticity where asymptotic normal critical values over-reject.

- **Mandatory Pre-Estimation Diagnostics**: `curse_of_dimensionality_density_precheck, vif_multicollinearity`
- **Mandatory Post-Estimation Diagnostics**: `pairs_studentized_refinement, white_heteroskedasticity`

### 15.2.4.1.3. Structural Equations and Asymptotic Covariance Specification

Core structural equation for `pairs_double_bootstrap`:

$$
Y_i = m(X_i) + \varepsilon_i, \quad \min_{\alpha, \beta} \sum_{i=1}^N \left(Y_i - \alpha - (X_i - x)^\top \beta\right)^2 K_H(X_i - x)
$$

Estimator objective function and sample moment conditions:

$$
p_{\text{boot}} = \frac{1}{B}\sum_{b=1}^B \mathbb{I}\left(|t^{\ast(b)}| \ge |t_{\text{obs}}|\right), \quad y_g^{\ast(b)} = X_g \tilde{\beta} + v_g^{(b)}\tilde{u}_g
$$

Asymptotic covariance matrix and robust inference metric:

$$
h_{\text{opt}} = \arg\min_{h > 0} \frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^N \left(Y_i - \hat{m}_{-i, h}(X_i)\right)^2
$$
