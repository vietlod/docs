## 1. Methodological Overview and Structural Equations

- **Methodological Focus**: Provides consistent estimation and robust inference for **Few-Cluster Resampling and Randomization Inference** specifications within **Nonparametric Econometrics and Few-Cluster Resampling Inference**.
- **Mandatory Post-Estimation Diagnostics**: $p$-value Wild Cluster Restricted (WCR), khoảng tin cậy đảo ngược thống kê $t^*$ — bootstrap-$t$ inversion CI — không phụ thuộc đối xứng, bậc tự do hiệu dụng Pustejovsky–Tipton CR2, đối chiếu với CRV1 $p$-value thường..

Core structural specification for Group 15.2:

$$
\tilde{Y}_{ig}^\ast = X_{ig}^\top \tilde{\beta}_0 + \tilde{u}_{ig}\cdot v_g^\ast, \quad w_{\text{Boot}} = \frac{\hat{\beta}_1^\ast - \beta_1^0}{\text{SE}_{\text{CR}}(\hat{\beta}_1^\ast)}, \quad p_{\text{WCB}} = \frac{1}{B}\sum_{b=1}^B \mathbb{I}(|w_{\text{Boot}}^{(b)}| \ge |w_{\text{obs}}|)
$$

Estimator objective and moment conditions:

$$
\hat{m}_h(x) = e_1^\top \left(\mathcal{X}_x^\top \mathcal{W}_h(x)\mathcal{X}_x\right)^{-1}\mathcal{X}_x^\top \mathcal{W}_h(x) Y, \quad h_{\text{opt}} = \arg\min_{h \gt 0}\text{CV}(h)
$$
