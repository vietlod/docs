## 15.2.1.1. Methodological Meaning and Scope of Application

### 15.2.1.1.1. Econometric Foundation and Identification Mechanism

Delivers finite-cluster size-accurate p-values and test-inversion confidence intervals under few or unbalanced clusters — G &lt; 30 , automatically selecting Webb 6-point weights for G &lt; 12 and Rademacher weights for G &gt;= 12. Across empirical econometrics, `wild_cluster_bootstrap` enables researchers to isolate structural parameters while controlling for confounding covariates and verifying identification assumptions via `boottest` in Stata 18 MP, `sandwich + clubSandwich` in R 4.x, and `wildboottest` in Python 3.11+.

### 15.2.1.1.2. Scope of Application and Sample Structure Requirements

Clustered cross-sectional, panel FE, or DID regressions with a cluster/entity grouping variable — especially 5 &lt;= G &lt;= 50 regions, industries, or countries . Mandatory post-estimation verification includes testing identification restrictions, evaluating goodness-of-fit metrics, and confirming numerical convergence across all three econometric engines.

### 15.2.1.1.3. Structural Equations and Asymptotic Covariance Specification

Core structural equation for `wild_cluster_bootstrap`:

$$
\tilde{Y}_{ig}^\ast = X_{ig}^\top \tilde{\beta}_0 + \tilde{u}_{ig}\cdot v_g^\ast, \quad w_{\text{Boot}} = \frac{\hat{\beta}_1^\ast - \beta_1^0}{\text{SE}_{\text{CR}}(\hat{\beta}_1^\ast)}, \quad p_{\text{WCB}} = \frac{1}{B}\sum_{b=1}^B \mathbb{I}(|w_{\text{Boot}}^{(b)}| \ge |w_{\text{obs}}|)
$$

Estimator objective function and sample moment conditions:

$$
\hat{m}_h(x) = e_1^\top \left(\mathcal{X}_x^\top \mathcal{W}_h(x)\mathcal{X}_x\right)^{-1}\mathcal{X}_x^\top \mathcal{W}_h(x) Y, \quad h_{\text{opt}} = \arg\min_{h \gt 0}\text{CV}(h)
$$

Asymptotic covariance matrix and post-estimation diagnostic metric:

$$
T_N^\ast = \frac{\hat{\beta}_1^\ast - \hat{\beta}_1}{\text{SE}_{\text{CR}}(\hat{\beta}_1^\ast)}, \quad \text{CI}_{1-\alpha}^{\text{Boot}} = \left\{b : p_{\text{Boot}}(b) \ge \alpha\right\}
$$
