## 1. Methodological Meaning and Scope of Application

### 1.1. Econometric Foundation and Identification Mechanism

Computes finite-sample design-based permutation p-values under Fisher's sharp null Y_i — 1 — = Y_i — 0 — respecting stratified and cluster-assigned randomization, immune to high-leverage outlier distortion. Across empirical econometrics, `randomization_inference` enables researchers to isolate structural parameters while controlling for confounding covariates and verifying identification assumptions via `ritest` in Stata 18 MP, `ri2` in R 4.x, and `scipy / statsmodels` in Python 3.11+.

### 1.2. Scope of Application and Sample Structure Requirements

Randomized controlled trials — RCTs , natural experiments, or few-cluster policy assignments with a treatment variable and optional strata/cluster columns. Mandatory post-estimation verification includes testing identification restrictions, evaluating goodness-of-fit metrics, and confirming numerical convergence across all three econometric engines.

### 1.3. Structural Equations and Asymptotic Covariance Specification

Core structural equation for `randomization_inference`:

$$
p_{\text{Fisher}} = \frac{1}{|\Omega_D|}\sum_{d \in \Omega_D} \mathbb{I}\left(|T(d, Y_{\text{obs}})| \ge |T(D_{\text{obs}}, Y_{\text{obs}})|\right), \quad H_0^{\text{sharp}}: Y_i(1) = Y_i(0) \;\forall i
$$

Estimator objective function and sample moment conditions:

$$
\hat{m}_h(x) = e_1^\top \left(\mathcal{X}_x^\top \mathcal{W}_h(x)\mathcal{X}_x\right)^{-1}\mathcal{X}_x^\top \mathcal{W}_h(x) Y, \quad h_{\text{opt}} = \arg\min_{h \gt 0}\text{CV}(h)
$$

Asymptotic covariance matrix and post-estimation diagnostic metric:

$$
T_N^\ast = \frac{\hat{\beta}_1^\ast - \hat{\beta}_1}{\text{SE}_{\text{CR}}(\hat{\beta}_1^\ast)}, \quad \text{CI}_{1-\alpha}^{\text{Boot}} = \left\{b : p_{\text{Boot}}(b) \ge \alpha\right\}
$$
