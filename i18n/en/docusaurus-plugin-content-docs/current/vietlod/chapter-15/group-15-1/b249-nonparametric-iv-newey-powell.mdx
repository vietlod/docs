## 15.1.5.1. Methodological Meaning and Scope of Application

### 15.1.5.1.1. Econometric Foundation and Identification Mechanism

Solves the ill-posed inverse problem E[Y - g(X) | Z] = 0 via Tikhonov-regularized sieve two-stage projection onto nonlinear instrument dictionaries with robust sandwich standard errors.

### 15.1.5.1.2. Scope of Application and Sample Structure Requirements

Structural Engel curves, demand functions, or production relationships with endogenous continuous regressors where linear functional form is misspecified.

- **Mandatory Pre-Estimation Diagnostics**: `curse_of_dimensionality_density_precheck, first_stage_f_weak_iv`
- **Mandatory Post-Estimation Diagnostics**: `newey_powell_tikhonov_sieve, hansen_j_overidentification`

### 15.1.5.1.3. Structural Equations and Asymptotic Covariance Specification

Core structural equation for `nonparametric_iv_newey_powell`:

$$
Y_i = m(X_i) + \varepsilon_i, \quad \min_{\alpha, \beta} \sum_{i=1}^N \left(Y_i - \alpha - (X_i - x)^\top \beta\right)^2 K_H(X_i - x)
$$

Estimator objective function and sample moment conditions:

$$
p_{\text{boot}} = \frac{1}{B}\sum_{b=1}^B \mathbb{I}\left(|t^{\ast(b)}| \ge |t_{\text{obs}}|\right), \quad y_g^{\ast(b)} = X_g \tilde{\beta} + v_g^{(b)}\tilde{u}_g
$$

Asymptotic covariance matrix and robust inference metric:

$$
h_{\text{opt}} = \arg\min_{h > 0} \frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^N \left(Y_i - \hat{m}_{-i, h}(X_i)\right)^2
$$

Q = np.column_stack([np.ones(len(y)), z, z**2, z**3])
