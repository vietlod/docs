## 15.1.3.1. Methodological Meaning and Scope of Application

### 15.1.3.1.1. Econometric Foundation and Identification Mechanism

Partials out smooth unknown nonlinear nuisance functions g(Z) via leave-one-out Nadaraya-Watson kernel regression on both Y and X, achieving root-N asymptotically normal estimation of parametric slopes beta.

### 15.1.3.1.2. Scope of Application and Sample Structure Requirements

Semiparametric models Y = X'beta + g(Z) + u where primary policy/economic regressors enter linearly while nuisance controls Z enter with arbitrary smooth nonlinearity.

- **Mandatory Pre-Estimation Diagnostics**: `curse_of_dimensionality_density_precheck, vif_multicollinearity`
- **Mandatory Post-Estimation Diagnostics**: `robinson_double_residual_bw, breusch_pagan_heteroskedasticity`

### 15.1.3.1.3. Structural Equations and Asymptotic Covariance Specification

Core structural equation for `partially_linear_robinson`:

$$
Y_i = m(X_i) + \varepsilon_i, \quad \min_{\alpha, \beta} \sum_{i=1}^N \left(Y_i - \alpha - (X_i - x)^\top \beta\right)^2 K_H(X_i - x)
$$

Estimator objective function and sample moment conditions:

$$
p_{\text{boot}} = \frac{1}{B}\sum_{b=1}^B \mathbb{I}\left(|t^{\ast(b)}| \ge |t_{\text{obs}}|\right), \quad y_g^{\ast(b)} = X_g \tilde{\beta} + v_g^{(b)}\tilde{u}_g
$$

Asymptotic covariance matrix and robust inference metric:

$$
h_{\text{opt}} = \arg\min_{h > 0} \frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^N \left(Y_i - \hat{m}_{-i, h}(X_i)\right)^2
$$

z = df["x1"].values; y = df["y"].values; h = 1.06 * z.std() * len(y)**(-0.2)
K = np.exp(-0.5 * ((z[:, None] - z[None, :]) / max(h, 1e-4))**2); np.fill_diagonal(K, 0)
W = K / np.maximum(K.sum(1, keepdims=True), 1e-12)
X = df[['x1', 'x2']].values
print(sm.OLS(y - W @ y, X - W @ X).fit(cov_type="HC1").summary())
