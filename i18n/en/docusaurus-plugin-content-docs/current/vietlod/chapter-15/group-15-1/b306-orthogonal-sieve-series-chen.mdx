## 15.1.6.1. Methodological Meaning and Scope of Application

### 15.1.6.1.1. Econometric Foundation and Identification Mechanism

Approximates smooth unknown conditional expectations over orthonormal Legendre polynomial dictionaries on [-1, 1], eliminating Vandermonde ill-conditioning and delivering root-N average marginal derivative inference.

### 15.1.6.1.2. Scope of Application and Sample Structure Requirements

Semiparametric Engel curves, asset pricing kernels, and nonlinear production surfaces where raw power polynomials suffer severe collinearity.

- **Mandatory Pre-Estimation Diagnostics**: `curse_of_dimensionality_density_precheck, predictor_scale_collinearity_precheck`
- **Mandatory Post-Estimation Diagnostics**: `nonparametric_r2_improvement, aic_bic_information_criteria`

### 15.1.6.1.3. Structural Equations and Asymptotic Covariance Specification

Core structural equation for `orthogonal_sieve_series_chen`:

$$
Y_i = m(X_i) + \varepsilon_i, \quad \min_{\alpha, \beta} \sum_{i=1}^N \left(Y_i - \alpha - (X_i - x)^\top \beta\right)^2 K_H(X_i - x)
$$

Estimator objective function and sample moment conditions:

$$
p_{\text{boot}} = \frac{1}{B}\sum_{b=1}^B \mathbb{I}\left(|t^{\ast(b)}| \ge |t_{\text{obs}}|\right), \quad y_g^{\ast(b)} = X_g \tilde{\beta} + v_g^{(b)}\tilde{u}_g
$$

Asymptotic covariance matrix and robust inference metric:

$$
h_{\text{opt}} = \arg\min_{h > 0} \frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^N \left(Y_i - \hat{m}_{-i, h}(X_i)\right)^2
$$
