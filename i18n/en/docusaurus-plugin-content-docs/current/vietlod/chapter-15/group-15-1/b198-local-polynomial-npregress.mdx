## 1. Methodological Meaning and Scope of Application

### 1.1. Econometric Foundation and Identification Mechanism

Nonparametrically estimates both the conditional mean surface m — x — = E[Y|X=x] and observation-level partial derivatives nabla m — x — via cross-validated local-linear kernel smoothing without functional-form assumptions. Across empirical econometrics, `local_polynomial_npregress` enables researchers to isolate structural parameters while controlling for confounding covariates and verifying identification assumptions via `Stata 18 npregress` in Stata 18 MP, `np` in R 4.x, and `statsmodels` in Python 3.11+.

### 1.2. Scope of Application and Sample Structure Requirements

Continuous outcome Y with 1 to 5 continuous covariates X exploring nonlinear curves — Environmental Kuznets Curve, Engel curves, age-earnings profiles . Mandatory post-estimation verification includes testing identification restrictions, evaluating goodness-of-fit metrics, and confirming numerical convergence across all three econometric engines.

### 1.3. Structural Equations and Asymptotic Covariance Specification

Core structural equation for `local_polynomial_npregress`:

$$
\min_{\alpha, \beta} \sum_{i=1}^N \left(Y_i - \alpha - (X_i - x)^\top \beta\right)^2 K_H(X_i - x), \quad \hat{m}(x) = \hat{\alpha}(x), \quad \widehat{\nabla m}(x) = \hat{\beta}(x)
$$

Estimator objective function and sample moment conditions:

$$
\hat{m}_h(x) = e_1^\top \left(\mathcal{X}_x^\top \mathcal{W}_h(x)\mathcal{X}_x\right)^{-1}\mathcal{X}_x^\top \mathcal{W}_h(x) Y, \quad h_{\text{opt}} = \arg\min_{h \gt 0}\text{CV}(h)
$$

Asymptotic covariance matrix and post-estimation diagnostic metric:

$$
T_N^\ast = \frac{\hat{\beta}_1^\ast - \hat{\beta}_1}{\text{SE}_{\text{CR}}(\hat{\beta}_1^\ast)}, \quad \text{CI}_{1-\alpha}^{\text{Boot}} = \left\{b : p_{\text{Boot}}(b) \ge \alpha\right\}
$$
