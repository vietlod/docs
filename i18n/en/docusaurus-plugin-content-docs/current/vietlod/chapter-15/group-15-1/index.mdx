## 1. Methodological Overview and Structural Equations

- **Methodological Focus**: Provides consistent estimation and robust inference for **Nonparametric and Semiparametric Smooth Regression** specifications within **Nonparametric Econometrics and Few-Cluster Resampling Inference**.
- **Mandatory Post-Estimation Diagnostics**: Băng thông tối ưu theo từng biến $h_1\dots h_k$, Average Marginal Effects $\overline{\partial \hat{m}/\partial x_j}$ kèm bootstrap SE, kiểm định đặc tả Hsiao–Li–Racine so với mô hình tuyến tính..

Core structural specification for Group 15.1:

$$
\min_{\alpha, \beta} \sum_{i=1}^N \left(Y_i - \alpha - (X_i - x)^\top \beta\right)^2 K_H(X_i - x), \quad \hat{m}(x) = \hat{\alpha}(x), \quad \widehat{\nabla m}(x) = \hat{\beta}(x)
$$

Estimator objective and moment conditions:

$$
\hat{m}_h(x) = e_1^\top \left(\mathcal{X}_x^\top \mathcal{W}_h(x)\mathcal{X}_x\right)^{-1}\mathcal{X}_x^\top \mathcal{W}_h(x) Y, \quad h_{\text{opt}} = \arg\min_{h \gt 0}\text{CV}(h)
$$
