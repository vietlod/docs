## 1. Central Research Question and Core Mathematical Specification

- **Central Research Question**: How to estimate flexible functional forms without parametric restrictions and conduct exact hypothesis testing when the number of clusters is small?
- **Chapter Architecture**: Encompasses `2` specialized methodological groups and `4` empirical models validated on Vietnamese datasets from [EcoData](https://ecodata.io.vn) and [Finco](https://www.finco.vn).

Core structural equation for Chapter 15:

$$
Y_i = m(X_i) + \varepsilon_i, \quad p_{\text{boot}} = \frac{1}{B}\sum_{b=1}^B \mathbb{I}\left(|t^{\ast(b)}| \ge |t_{\text{obs}}|\right)
$$
