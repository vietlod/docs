## 7.1. Central Research Question and Core Mathematical Specification

- **Central Research Question**: How to identify structural macroeconomic shocks, trace dynamic impulse response functions, and quantify directional connectedness across multivariate systems?
- **Chapter Architecture**: Encompasses `5` specialized methodological parts from `7.1` to `7.5` and `15` empirical model lessons validated on Vietnamese datasets from [EcoData](https://ecodata.io.vn) and [Finco](https://www.finco.vn).

Core structural equation for Chapter 7:

$$
Y_{t+h} = \alpha_h + \beta_h \text{Shock}_t + \sum_{\ell=1}^p \Gamma_{h,\ell} Y_{t-\ell} + u_{t+h}, \quad h = 0, 1, \dots, H
$$
