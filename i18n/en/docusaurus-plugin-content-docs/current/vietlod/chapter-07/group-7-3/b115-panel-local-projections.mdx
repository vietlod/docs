## 1. Methodological Meaning and Scope of Application

### 1.1. Econometric Foundation and Identification Mechanism

Estimates medium-run cumulative panel impulse responses absorbing unit and time fixed effects with cross-sectionally and serially robust inference. Across empirical econometrics, `panel_local_projections` enables researchers to isolate structural parameters while controlling for confounding covariates and verifying identification assumptions via `reghdfe / xtscc` in Stata 18 MP, `lpirfs / fixest` in R 4.x, and `pyfixest` in Python 3.11+.

### 1.2. Scope of Application and Sample Structure Requirements

Multi-entity macro, regional, or firm panels evaluating dynamic horizon responses and pre-shock placebo pre-trends. Mandatory post-estimation verification includes testing identification restrictions, evaluating goodness-of-fit metrics, and confirming numerical convergence across all three econometric engines.

### 1.3. Structural Equations and Asymptotic Covariance Specification

Core structural equation for `panel_local_projections`:

$$
Y_{i,t+h} - Y_{i,t-1} = \mu_i^h + \lambda_t^h + \beta_h D_{it} + \sum_{\ell=1}^p \gamma_{h,\ell}^\top X_{i,t-\ell} + \varepsilon_{i,t+h}
$$

Estimator objective function and sample moment conditions:

$$
Y_t = c + \sum_{\ell=1}^p A_\ell Y_{t-\ell} + B^{-1}\Sigma_u \varepsilon_t, \quad \Psi_h = \frac{\partial Y_{t+h}}{\partial \varepsilon_t^\top} = \Phi_h B^{-1}\Sigma_u
$$

Asymptotic covariance matrix and post-estimation diagnostic metric:

$$
\text{FEVD}_{j \leftarrow k}(H) = \frac{\sum_{h=0}^{H-1}\left(e_j^\top \Psi_h e_k\right)^2}{\sum_{h=0}^{H-1} e_j^\top \Psi_h \Psi_h^\top e_j}, \quad \det\left(I_K - \sum_{\ell=1}^p A_\ell z^\ell\right) \neq 0 \;\forall |z| \le 1
$$
