## 1. Methodological Overview and Structural Equations

- **Methodological Focus**: Provides consistent estimation and robust inference for **Local Projections Suite** specifications within **Multivariate Time Series, Local Projections and Spillover Networks**.
- **Mandatory Post-Estimation Diagnostics**: Dải tin cậy đồng thời/điểm $68\%$ & $95\%$ Newey–West HAC theo chân trời $h$, kiểm định Wald tích lũy, kiểm định phi tuyến trạng thái..

Core structural specification for Group 7.3:

$$
Y_{t+h} = \alpha_h + \beta_h \text{shock}_t + \sum_{\ell=1}^p \Gamma_{h,\ell}^\top W_{t-\ell} + u_{t+h}, \quad \widehat{\text{IRF}}(h) = \hat{\beta}_h, \quad h = 0, 1, \dots, H
$$

Estimator objective and moment conditions:

$$
Y_t = c + \sum_{\ell=1}^p A_\ell Y_{t-\ell} + B^{-1}\Sigma_u \varepsilon_t, \quad \Psi_h = \frac{\partial Y_{t+h}}{\partial \varepsilon_t^\top} = \Phi_h B^{-1}\Sigma_u
$$
