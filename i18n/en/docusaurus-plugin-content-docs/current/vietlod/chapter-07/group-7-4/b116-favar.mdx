## 1. Methodological Meaning and Scope of Application

### 1.1. Econometric Foundation and Identification Mechanism

Augments observed policy variables with latent macroeconomic factors extracted from broad indicator sets to eliminate omitted-information puzzles. Across empirical econometrics, `favar` enables researchers to isolate structural parameters while controlling for confounding covariates and verifying identification assumptions via `pca / svar` in Stata 18 MP, `FAVAR / vars` in R 4.x, and `statsmodels` in Python 3.11+.

### 1.2. Scope of Application and Sample Structure Requirements

Monetary or macro analysis combining policy instruments with multiple co-moving economic indicators. Mandatory post-estimation verification includes testing identification restrictions, evaluating goodness-of-fit metrics, and confirming numerical convergence across all three econometric engines.

### 1.3. Structural Equations and Asymptotic Covariance Specification

Core structural equation for `favar`:

$$
\begin{bmatrix} F_t \\ Y_t \end{bmatrix} = \Phi(L)\begin{bmatrix} F_{t-1} \\ Y_{t-1} \end{bmatrix} + v_t, \quad X_t = \Lambda^f F_t + \Lambda^y Y_t + e_t
$$

Estimator objective function and sample moment conditions:

$$
Y_t = c + \sum_{\ell=1}^p A_\ell Y_{t-\ell} + B^{-1}\Sigma_u \varepsilon_t, \quad \Psi_h = \frac{\partial Y_{t+h}}{\partial \varepsilon_t^\top} = \Phi_h B^{-1}\Sigma_u
$$

Asymptotic covariance matrix and post-estimation diagnostic metric:

$$
\text{FEVD}_{j \leftarrow k}(H) = \frac{\sum_{h=0}^{H-1}\left(e_j^\top \Psi_h e_k\right)^2}{\sum_{h=0}^{H-1} e_j^\top \Psi_h \Psi_h^\top e_j}, \quad \det\left(I_K - \sum_{\ell=1}^p A_\ell z^\ell\right) \neq 0 \;\forall |z| \le 1
$$
