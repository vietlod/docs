## 7.4.3.1. Methodological Meaning and Scope of Application

### 7.4.3.1.1. Econometric Foundation and Identification Mechanism

Decomposes generalized forecast-error variance shares into directional TO, FROM, NET, pairwise NPDC, and total system connectedness TCI invariant to variable ordering. Across empirical econometrics, `spillover_connectedness` enables researchers to isolate structural parameters while controlling for confounding covariates and verifying identification assumptions via `dyconnectedness / var` in Stata 18 MP, `ConnectednessApproach` in R 4.x, and `statsmodels` in Python 3.11+.

### 7.4.3.1.2. Scope of Application and Sample Structure Requirements

Cross-market equity, bond, FX, commodity, crypto, or regional inflation return/volatility transmission studies. Mandatory post-estimation verification includes testing identification restrictions, evaluating goodness-of-fit metrics, and confirming numerical convergence across all three econometric engines.

### 7.4.3.1.3. Structural Equations and Asymptotic Covariance Specification

Core structural equation for `spillover_connectedness`:

$$
\tilde{\theta}_{ij}^g(H) = \frac{\theta_{ij}^g(H)}{\sum_{k=1}^N \theta_{ik}^g(H)}, \quad \text{TCI}(H) = \frac{\sum_{i\neq j=1}^N \tilde{\theta}_{ij}^g(H)}{N} \times 100
$$

Estimator objective function and sample moment conditions:

$$
Y_t = c + \sum_{\ell=1}^p A_\ell Y_{t-\ell} + B^{-1}\Sigma_u \varepsilon_t, \quad \Psi_h = \frac{\partial Y_{t+h}}{\partial \varepsilon_t^\top} = \Phi_h B^{-1}\Sigma_u
$$

Asymptotic covariance matrix and post-estimation diagnostic metric:

$$
\text{FEVD}_{j \leftarrow k}(H) = \frac{\sum_{h=0}^{H-1}\left(e_j^\top \Psi_h e_k\right)^2}{\sum_{h=0}^{H-1} e_j^\top \Psi_h \Psi_h^\top e_j}, \quad \det\left(I_K - \sum_{\ell=1}^p A_\ell z^\ell\right) \neq 0 \;\forall |z| \le 1
$$
