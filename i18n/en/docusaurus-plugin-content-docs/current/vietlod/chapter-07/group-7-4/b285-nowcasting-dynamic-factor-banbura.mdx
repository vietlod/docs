## 7.4.4.1. Methodological Meaning and Scope of Application

### 7.4.4.1.1. Econometric Foundation and Identification Mechanism

Extracts latent common activity factors across asynchronous macro releases with ragged-edge missingness using EM-Kalman state-space filtering and factor-augmented bridge equations.

### 7.4.4.1.2. Scope of Application and Sample Structure Requirements

Real-time GDP, inflation, or trade nowcasting combining high-frequency monthly indicators with delayed quarterly national accounts.

- **Mandatory Pre-Estimation Diagnostics**: `lag_order_selection, granger_causality`
- **Mandatory Post-Estimation Diagnostics**: `var_residual_autocorr, var_stability_eigenvalues`

### 7.4.4.1.3. Structural Equations and Asymptotic Covariance Specification

Core structural equation for `nowcasting_dynamic_factor_banbura`:

$$
A_0 Y_t = \nu + \sum_{\ell=1}^p A_\ell Y_{t-\ell} + B \varepsilon_t, \quad \mathbb{E}[\varepsilon_t \varepsilon_t^\top] = I_K
$$

Estimator objective function and sample moment conditions:

$$
Y_{t+h} - Y_{t-1} = \alpha_h + \beta_h \text{Shock}_t + \Gamma_h^\top W_t + u_{t+h}, \quad h = 0, 1, \dots, H
$$

Asymptotic covariance matrix and robust inference metric:

$$
\text{IRF}(h) = \frac{\partial \mathbb{E}_t[Y_{t+h}]}{\partial \varepsilon_t^\top} = \Psi_h A_0^{-1} B
$$
