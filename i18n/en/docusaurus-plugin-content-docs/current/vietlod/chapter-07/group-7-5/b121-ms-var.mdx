## 1. Methodological Meaning and Scope of Application

### 1.1. Econometric Foundation and Identification Mechanism

Jointly models regime-switching intercepts, autoregressive dynamics, and shock covariances governed by a hidden Markov transition matrix. Across empirical econometrics, `ms_var` enables researchers to isolate structural parameters while controlling for confounding covariates and verifying identification assumptions via `Stata mswitch` in Stata 18 MP, `MSwM / vars` in R 4.x, and `statsmodels` in Python 3.11+.

### 1.2. Scope of Application and Sample Structure Requirements

Multivariate macro-financial systems experiencing discrete expansion-recession or calm-crisis regime shifts. Mandatory post-estimation verification includes testing identification restrictions, evaluating goodness-of-fit metrics, and confirming numerical convergence across all three econometric engines.

### 1.3. Structural Equations and Asymptotic Covariance Specification

Core structural equation for `ms_var`:

$$
Y_t = \nu(S_t) + \sum_{\ell=1}^p A_\ell(S_t) Y_{t-\ell} + \Sigma(S_t)^{1/2}\varepsilon_t, \quad \text{P}(S_t = j \mid S_{t-1} = i) = p_{ij}
$$

Estimator objective function and sample moment conditions:

$$
Y_t = c + \sum_{\ell=1}^p A_\ell Y_{t-\ell} + B^{-1}\Sigma_u \varepsilon_t, \quad \Psi_h = \frac{\partial Y_{t+h}}{\partial \varepsilon_t^\top} = \Phi_h B^{-1}\Sigma_u
$$

Asymptotic covariance matrix and post-estimation diagnostic metric:

$$
\text{FEVD}_{j \leftarrow k}(H) = \frac{\sum_{h=0}^{H-1}\left(e_j^\top \Psi_h e_k\right)^2}{\sum_{h=0}^{H-1} e_j^\top \Psi_h \Psi_h^\top e_j}, \quad \det\left(I_K - \sum_{\ell=1}^p A_\ell z^\ell\right) \neq 0 \;\forall |z| \le 1
$$
