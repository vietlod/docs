## 6.1. Central Research Question and Core Mathematical Specification

- **Central Research Question**: How to test for stationarity, long-run cointegration, asymmetric short-run adjustments, structural breaks, and explosive speculative bubbles in univariate macro-financial series?
- **Chapter Architecture**: Encompasses `5` specialized methodological parts from `6.1` to `6.5` and `23` empirical model lessons validated on Vietnamese datasets from [EcoData](https://ecodata.io.vn) and [Finco](https://www.finco.vn).

Core structural equation for Chapter 6:

$$
\Delta Y_t = \alpha_0 + \rho Y_{t-1} + \theta^+ X_{t-1}^+ + \theta^- X_{t-1}^- + \sum_{j=1}^{p-1} \gamma_j \Delta Y_{t-j} + \varepsilon_t
$$
