## 6.2.5.1. Methodological Meaning and Scope of Application

### 6.2.5.1.1. Econometric Foundation and Identification Mechanism

Estimates the Seasonal ARIMA Time Series specification within the Univariate Time Series Forecasting framework by optimizing the structural objective function and computing heteroskedasticity- and cluster-robust asymptotic standard errors. Across empirical econometrics, `sarima` enables researchers to isolate structural parameters while controlling for confounding covariates and verifying identification assumptions via `base Stata` in Stata 18 MP, `stats / forecast` in R 4.x, and `statsmodels` in Python 3.11+.

### 6.2.5.1.2. Scope of Application and Sample Structure Requirements

Chronologically ordered macro-financial time series requiring stationarity, lag-order selection, and serial-correlation diagnostics. Mandatory post-estimation verification includes testing identification restrictions, evaluating goodness-of-fit metrics, and confirming numerical convergence across all three econometric engines.

### 6.2.5.1.3. Structural Equations and Asymptotic Covariance Specification

Core structural equation for `sarima`:

$$
\Phi_p(L)\tilde{\Phi}_P(L^s)(1 - L)^d(1 - L^s)^D Y_t = \Theta_q(L)\tilde{\Theta}_Q(L^s)\varepsilon_t
$$

Estimator objective function and sample moment conditions:

$$
\hat{\theta}_T = \arg\max_{\theta \in \Theta} \ell_T\left(Y_1, \dots, Y_T; \theta\right), \quad \Phi(L)(1-L)^d Y_t = \mu + \Theta(L)\varepsilon_t
$$

Asymptotic covariance matrix and post-estimation diagnostic metric:

$$
\text{AICc} = -2\ell_T(\hat{\theta}) + 2k + \frac{2k(k+1)}{T-k-1}, \quad Q_{\text{LB}}(m) = T(T+2)\sum_{\ell=1}^m \frac{\hat{\rho}_\ell^2}{T-\ell} \xrightarrow{d} \chi^2_{m-p-q}
$$

coeftest(fit_sarima, vcov = vcovHC(fit_sarima, type = "HC1"))
