## 6.5.1. Methodological Overview and Structural Equations

- **Methodological Focus**: Provides consistent estimation and robust inference for **Nonlinear Time Series, Structural Breaks and Bubbles** specifications within **Univariate, Nonlinear, Structural Break and Bubble Time Series**.
- **Mandatory Post-Estimation Diagnostics**: Thống kê Sup-Wald / Sup-LM bootstrap $p$-value kiểm định tồn tại ngưỡng, khoảng tin cậy $95\%$ cho điểm ngưỡng $\hat{\gamma}$, kiểm định phương sai thay đổi từng chế độ..

Core structural specification for Part 6.5:

$$
Y_t = X_t^\top \beta_1 \mathbb{I}(q_t \le \gamma) + X_t^\top \beta_2 \mathbb{I}(q_t \gt \gamma) + \varepsilon_t, \quad \text{LR}_T(\gamma) = T\frac{\text{SSR}(\gamma) - \text{SSR}(\hat{\gamma})}{\text{SSR}(\hat{\gamma})}
$$

Estimator objective and moment conditions:

$$
\hat{\theta}_T = \arg\max_{\theta \in \Theta} \ell_T\left(Y_1, \dots, Y_T; \theta\right), \quad \Phi(L)(1-L)^d Y_t = \mu + \Theta(L)\varepsilon_t
$$
