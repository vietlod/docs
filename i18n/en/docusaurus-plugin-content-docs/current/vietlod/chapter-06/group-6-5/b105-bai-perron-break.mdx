## 6.5.4.1. Methodological Meaning and Scope of Application

### 6.5.4.1.1. Econometric Foundation and Identification Mechanism

Globally partitions time-series regressions into m+1 regimes at unknown break dates via dynamic programming with sup-F, UDmax, and LWZ selection. Across empirical econometrics, `bai_perron_break` enables researchers to isolate structural parameters while controlling for confounding covariates and verifying identification assumptions via `Stata estat sbsingle` in Stata 18 MP, `strucchange` in R 4.x, and `statsmodels` in Python 3.11+.

### 6.5.4.1.2. Scope of Application and Sample Structure Requirements

Time series with at least 25 periods potentially subject to one or more unknown regime shifts in mean or regression slopes. Mandatory post-estimation verification includes testing identification restrictions, evaluating goodness-of-fit metrics, and confirming numerical convergence across all three econometric engines.

### 6.5.4.1.3. Structural Equations and Asymptotic Covariance Specification

Core structural equation for `bai_perron_break`:

$$
Y_t = X_t^\top \beta + Z_t^\top \delta_j + u_t, \quad t = T_{j-1}+1, \dots, T_j \;(j=1,\dots,m+1), \quad \{\hat{T}_j\} = \arg\min_{(T_1,\dots,T_m)} \text{SSR}_T(T_1,\dots,T_m)
$$

Estimator objective function and sample moment conditions:

$$
\hat{\theta}_T = \arg\max_{\theta \in \Theta} \ell_T\left(Y_1, \dots, Y_T; \theta\right), \quad \Phi(L)(1-L)^d Y_t = \mu + \Theta(L)\varepsilon_t
$$

Asymptotic covariance matrix and post-estimation diagnostic metric:

$$
\text{AICc} = -2\ell_T(\hat{\theta}) + 2k + \frac{2k(k+1)}{T-k-1}, \quad Q_{\text{LB}}(m) = T(T+2)\sum_{\ell=1}^m \frac{\hat{\rho}_\ell^2}{T-\ell} \xrightarrow{d} \chi^2_{m-p-q}
$$
