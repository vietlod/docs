## 1. Methodological Meaning and Scope of Application

### 1.1. Econometric Foundation and Identification Mechanism

Models continuous nonlinear transitions between extreme regimes via Logistic LSTAR or Exponential ESTAR transition functions selected by Terasvirta LM tests. Across empirical econometrics, `star_smooth_transition` enables researchers to isolate structural parameters while controlling for confounding covariates and verifying identification assumptions via `base Stata nl` in Stata 18 MP, `minpack.lm` in R 4.x, and `scipy / statsmodels` in Python 3.11+.

### 1.2. Scope of Application and Sample Structure Requirements

Real exchange rates, business-cycle growth, or interest-rate spreads exhibiting gradual rather than abrupt regime adjustment. Mandatory post-estimation verification includes testing identification restrictions, evaluating goodness-of-fit metrics, and confirming numerical convergence across all three econometric engines.

### 1.3. Structural Equations and Asymptotic Covariance Specification

Core structural equation for `star_smooth_transition`:

$$
Y_t = \phi_1^\top W_t\left(1 - G(s_t; \gamma, c)\right) + \phi_2^\top W_t G(s_t; \gamma, c) + \varepsilon_t, \quad G_{\text{LSTAR}}(s_t;\gamma,c) = \left(1 + \exp\{-\gamma(s_t - c)\}\right)^{-1}
$$

Estimator objective function and sample moment conditions:

$$
\hat{\theta}_T = \arg\max_{\theta \in \Theta} \ell_T\left(Y_1, \dots, Y_T; \theta\right), \quad \Phi(L)(1-L)^d Y_t = \mu + \Theta(L)\varepsilon_t
$$

Asymptotic covariance matrix and post-estimation diagnostic metric:

$$
\text{AICc} = -2\ell_T(\hat{\theta}) + 2k + \frac{2k(k+1)}{T-k-1}, \quad Q_{\text{LB}}(m) = T(T+2)\sum_{\ell=1}^m \frac{\hat{\rho}_\ell^2}{T-\ell} \xrightarrow{d} \chi^2_{m-p-q}
$$
