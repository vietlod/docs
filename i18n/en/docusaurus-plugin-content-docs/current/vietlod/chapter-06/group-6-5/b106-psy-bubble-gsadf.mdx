## 1. Methodological Meaning and Scope of Application

### 1.1. Econometric Foundation and Identification Mechanism

Detects and date-stamps origination and collapse of multiple explosive speculative bubbles via double-supremum right-tailed ADF windows and wild bootstrap. Across empirical econometrics, `psy_bubble_gsadf` enables researchers to isolate structural parameters while controlling for confounding covariates and verifying identification assumptions via `radf` in Stata 18 MP, `exuber` in R 4.x, and `statsmodels` in Python 3.11+.

### 1.2. Scope of Application and Sample Structure Requirements

Equity, real-estate, commodity, exchange-rate, or credit price series with at least 25 observations. Mandatory post-estimation verification includes testing identification restrictions, evaluating goodness-of-fit metrics, and confirming numerical convergence across all three econometric engines.

### 1.3. Structural Equations and Asymptotic Covariance Specification

Core structural equation for `psy_bubble_gsadf`:

$$
\text{GSADF}(r_0) = \sup_{r_2 \in [r_0, 1], \; r_1 \in [0, r_2 - r_0]} \text{ADF}_{r_1}^{r_2}, \quad \text{BSADF}_{r_2}(r_0) = \sup_{r_1 \in [0, r_2 - r_0]} \text{ADF}_{r_1}^{r_2}
$$

Estimator objective function and sample moment conditions:

$$
\hat{\theta}_T = \arg\max_{\theta \in \Theta} \ell_T\left(Y_1, \dots, Y_T; \theta\right), \quad \Phi(L)(1-L)^d Y_t = \mu + \Theta(L)\varepsilon_t
$$

Asymptotic covariance matrix and post-estimation diagnostic metric:

$$
\text{AICc} = -2\ell_T(\hat{\theta}) + 2k + \frac{2k(k+1)}{T-k-1}, \quad Q_{\text{LB}}(m) = T(T+2)\sum_{\ell=1}^m \frac{\hat{\rho}_\ell^2}{T-\ell} \xrightarrow{d} \chi^2_{m-p-q}
$$
