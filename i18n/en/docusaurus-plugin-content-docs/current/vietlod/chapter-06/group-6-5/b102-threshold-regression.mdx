## 6.5.1.1. Methodological Meaning and Scope of Application

### 6.5.1.1.1. Econometric Foundation and Identification Mechanism

Splits cross-sectional or time-series regressions into distinct regimes around an unknown observable threshold gamma with inverted LR confidence sets. Across empirical econometrics, `threshold_regression` enables researchers to isolate structural parameters while controlling for confounding covariates and verifying identification assumptions via `Stata 18 threshold` in Stata 18 MP, `strucchange` in R 4.x, and `statsmodels` in Python 3.11+.

### 6.5.1.1.2. Scope of Application and Sample Structure Requirements

Cross-sectional or time-series samples with at least 25 observations testing regime switches governed by an observable threshold variable. Mandatory post-estimation verification includes testing identification restrictions, evaluating goodness-of-fit metrics, and confirming numerical convergence across all three econometric engines.

### 6.5.1.1.3. Structural Equations and Asymptotic Covariance Specification

Core structural equation for `threshold_regression`:

$$
Y_t = X_t^\top \beta_1 \mathbb{I}(q_t \le \gamma) + X_t^\top \beta_2 \mathbb{I}(q_t \gt \gamma) + \varepsilon_t, \quad \text{LR}_T(\gamma) = T\frac{\text{SSR}(\gamma) - \text{SSR}(\hat{\gamma})}{\text{SSR}(\hat{\gamma})}
$$

Estimator objective function and sample moment conditions:

$$
\hat{\theta}_T = \arg\max_{\theta \in \Theta} \ell_T\left(Y_1, \dots, Y_T; \theta\right), \quad \Phi(L)(1-L)^d Y_t = \mu + \Theta(L)\varepsilon_t
$$

Asymptotic covariance matrix and post-estimation diagnostic metric:

$$
\text{AICc} = -2\ell_T(\hat{\theta}) + 2k + \frac{2k(k+1)}{T-k-1}, \quad Q_{\text{LB}}(m) = T(T+2)\sum_{\ell=1}^m \frac{\hat{\rho}_\ell^2}{T-\ell} \xrightarrow{d} \chi^2_{m-p-q}
$$
