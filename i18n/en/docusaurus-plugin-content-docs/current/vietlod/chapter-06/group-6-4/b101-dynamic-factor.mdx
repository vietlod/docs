## 6.4.3.1. Methodological Meaning and Scope of Application

### 6.4.3.1.1. Econometric Foundation and Identification Mechanism

Estimates the Dynamic Factor Time-Series Estimator specification within the State-Space and Unobserved Components Models framework by optimizing the structural objective function and computing heteroskedasticity- and cluster-robust asymptotic standard errors. Across empirical econometrics, `dynamic_factor` enables researchers to isolate structural parameters while controlling for confounding covariates and verifying identification assumptions via `base Stata` in Stata 18 MP, `stats / vars` in R 4.x, and `statsmodels` in Python 3.11+.

### 6.4.3.1.2. Scope of Application and Sample Structure Requirements

Chronologically ordered macro-financial time series requiring stationarity, lag-order selection, and serial-correlation diagnostics. Mandatory post-estimation verification includes testing identification restrictions, evaluating goodness-of-fit metrics, and confirming numerical convergence across all three econometric engines.

### 6.4.3.1.3. Structural Equations and Asymptotic Covariance Specification

Core structural equation for `dynamic_factor`:

$$
Y_t = \Lambda F_t + e_t, \quad F_t = A_1 F_{t-1} + \dots + A_p F_{t-p} + \eta_t
$$

Estimator objective function and sample moment conditions:

$$
\hat{\theta}_T = \arg\max_{\theta \in \Theta} \ell_T\left(Y_1, \dots, Y_T; \theta\right), \quad \Phi(L)(1-L)^d Y_t = \mu + \Theta(L)\varepsilon_t
$$

Asymptotic covariance matrix and post-estimation diagnostic metric:

$$
\text{AICc} = -2\ell_T(\hat{\theta}) + 2k + \frac{2k(k+1)}{T-k-1}, \quad Q_{\text{LB}}(m) = T(T+2)\sum_{\ell=1}^m \frac{\hat{\rho}_\ell^2}{T-\ell} \xrightarrow{d} \chi^2_{m-p-q}
$$
