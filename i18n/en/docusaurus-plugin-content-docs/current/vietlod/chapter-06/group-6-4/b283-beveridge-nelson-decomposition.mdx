## 6.4.4.1. Methodological Meaning and Scope of Application

### 6.4.4.1.1. Econometric Foundation and Identification Mechanism

Defines the stochastic permanent trend as the limiting long-horizon ARIMA expectation lim_\{k-&gt;inf\}(E_t[y_\{t+k\}] - k*mu), allowing correlated permanent and cyclical innovations without two-sided look-ahead smoothing.

### 6.4.4.1.2. Scope of Application and Sample Structure Requirements

Difference-stationary I(1) output, consumption, and exchange-rate series requiring real-time one-sided business-cycle gap extraction free of Hodrick-Prescott endpoint distortion.

- **Mandatory Pre-Estimation Diagnostics**: `adf_test, kpss_test`
- **Mandatory Post-Estimation Diagnostics**: `ljung_box, aic`

### 6.4.4.1.3. Structural Equations and Asymptotic Covariance Specification

Core structural equation for `beveridge_nelson_decomposition`:

$$
\Phi(L)(1-L)^d Y_t = c + \Theta(L)\varepsilon_t + \sum_{j=0}^q \beta_j^\top X_{t-j}, \quad \varepsilon_t \sim \text{WN}(0, \sigma^2)
$$

Estimator objective function and sample moment conditions:

$$
\Delta Y_t = \alpha_0 + \rho Y_{t-1} + \theta^\top X_{t-1} + \sum_{j=1}^{p-1}\gamma_j \Delta Y_{t-j} + \sum_{j=0}^{q-1}\delta_j^\top \Delta X_{t-j} + u_t
$$

Asymptotic covariance matrix and robust inference metric:

$$
\widehat{\Omega}_{\text{HAC}} = \hat{\Gamma}_0 + \sum_{j=1}^m \left(1 - \frac{j}{m+1}\right)\left(\hat{\Gamma}_j + \hat{\Gamma}_j^\top\right)
$$
