## 1. Methodological Meaning and Scope of Application

### 1.1. Econometric Foundation and Identification Mechanism

Estimates state-dependent cointegrating elasticities and error-correction adjustment speeds across lower, median, and upper conditional quantiles. Across empirical econometrics, `qardl` enables researchers to isolate structural parameters while controlling for confounding covariates and verifying identification assumptions via `Stata sqreg` in Stata 18 MP, `quantreg` in R 4.x, and `statsmodels` in Python 3.11+.

### 1.2. Scope of Application and Sample Structure Requirements

Time series where equilibrium pass-through and speed of mean reversion differ between downturn, normal, and boom regimes. Mandatory post-estimation verification includes testing identification restrictions, evaluating goodness-of-fit metrics, and confirming numerical convergence across all three econometric engines.

### 1.3. Structural Equations and Asymptotic Covariance Specification

Core structural equation for `qardl`:

$$
Q_{\Delta Y_t}(\tau \mid \mathcal{F}_{t-1}) = \alpha(\tau) + \zeta(\tau)\left(Y_{t-1} - \beta(\tau)^\top X_{t-1}\right) + \sum_{j=1}^{p-1}\phi_j(\tau)\Delta Y_{t-j} + \sum_{j=0}^{q-1}\omega_j(\tau)^\top \Delta X_{t-j}
$$

Estimator objective function and sample moment conditions:

$$
\hat{\theta}_T = \arg\max_{\theta \in \Theta} \ell_T\left(Y_1, \dots, Y_T; \theta\right), \quad \Phi(L)(1-L)^d Y_t = \mu + \Theta(L)\varepsilon_t
$$

Asymptotic covariance matrix and post-estimation diagnostic metric:

$$
\text{AICc} = -2\ell_T(\hat{\theta}) + 2k + \frac{2k(k+1)}{T-k-1}, \quad Q_{\text{LB}}(m) = T(T+2)\sum_{\ell=1}^m \frac{\hat{\rho}_\ell^2}{T-\ell} \xrightarrow{d} \chi^2_{m-p-q}
$$
