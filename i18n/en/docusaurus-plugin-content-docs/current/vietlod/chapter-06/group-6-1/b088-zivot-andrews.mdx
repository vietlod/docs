## 6.1.5.1. Methodological Meaning and Scope of Application

### 6.1.5.1.1. Econometric Foundation and Identification Mechanism

Tests the unit-root null against stationarity around a single endogenously determined structural break in level, trend, or both. Across empirical econometrics, `zivot_andrews` enables researchers to isolate structural parameters while controlling for confounding covariates and verifying identification assumptions via `zandrews` in Stata 18 MP, `urca` in R 4.x, and `arch` in Python 3.11+.

### 6.1.5.1.2. Scope of Application and Sample Structure Requirements

Macroeconomic and financial series experiencing policy regime shifts, crises, or structural reforms where standard ADF under-rejects. Mandatory post-estimation verification includes testing identification restrictions, evaluating goodness-of-fit metrics, and confirming numerical convergence across all three econometric engines.

### 6.1.5.1.3. Structural Equations and Asymptotic Covariance Specification

Core structural equation for `zivot_andrews`:

$$
\Delta Y_t = \mu + \beta t + \theta DU_t(\lambda) + \gamma DT_t(\lambda) + \alpha Y_{t-1} + \sum_{j=1}^k c_j \Delta Y_{t-j} + \varepsilon_t, \quad t_{\text{ZA}} = \inf_{\lambda \in \Lambda} t_{\hat{\alpha}}(\lambda)
$$

Estimator objective function and sample moment conditions:

$$
\hat{\theta}_T = \arg\max_{\theta \in \Theta} \ell_T\left(Y_1, \dots, Y_T; \theta\right), \quad \Phi(L)(1-L)^d Y_t = \mu + \Theta(L)\varepsilon_t
$$

Asymptotic covariance matrix and post-estimation diagnostic metric:

$$
\text{AICc} = -2\ell_T(\hat{\theta}) + 2k + \frac{2k(k+1)}{T-k-1}, \quad Q_{\text{LB}}(m) = T(T+2)\sum_{\ell=1}^m \frac{\hat{\rho}_\ell^2}{T-\ell} \xrightarrow{d} \chi^2_{m-p-q}
$$
