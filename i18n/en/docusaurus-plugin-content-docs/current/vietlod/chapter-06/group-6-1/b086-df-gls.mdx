## 1. Methodological Meaning and Scope of Application

### 1.1. Econometric Foundation and Identification Mechanism

Detrends series under local-to-unity GLS alternatives and selects lags via Ng-Perron Modified AIC for substantially higher finite-sample power than ADF. Across empirical econometrics, `df_gls` enables researchers to isolate structural parameters while controlling for confounding covariates and verifying identification assumptions via `base Stata dfgls` in Stata 18 MP, `urca` in R 4.x, and `arch` in Python 3.11+.

### 1.2. Scope of Application and Sample Structure Requirements

Persistent macro series such as real exchange rates, real interest rates, and unemployment rates on moderate sample sizes. Mandatory post-estimation verification includes testing identification restrictions, evaluating goodness-of-fit metrics, and confirming numerical convergence across all three econometric engines.

### 1.3. Structural Equations and Asymptotic Covariance Specification

Core structural equation for `df_gls`:

$$
Y_t^d = Y_t - \hat{\beta}_{\bar{c}}^\top Z_t, \quad \Delta Y_t^d = \phi_0 Y_{t-1}^d + \sum_{j=1}^p \phi_j \Delta Y_{t-j}^d + e_t, \quad \bar{c} \in \{-7, -13.5\}
$$

Estimator objective function and sample moment conditions:

$$
\hat{\theta}_T = \arg\max_{\theta \in \Theta} \ell_T\left(Y_1, \dots, Y_T; \theta\right), \quad \Phi(L)(1-L)^d Y_t = \mu + \Theta(L)\varepsilon_t
$$

Asymptotic covariance matrix and post-estimation diagnostic metric:

$$
\text{AICc} = -2\ell_T(\hat{\theta}) + 2k + \frac{2k(k+1)}{T-k-1}, \quad Q_{\text{LB}}(m) = T(T+2)\sum_{\ell=1}^m \frac{\hat{\rho}_\ell^2}{T-\ell} \xrightarrow{d} \chi^2_{m-p-q}
$$
