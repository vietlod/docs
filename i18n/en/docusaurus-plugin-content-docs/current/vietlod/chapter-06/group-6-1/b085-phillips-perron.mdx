## 1. Methodological Meaning and Scope of Application

### 1.1. Econometric Foundation and Identification Mechanism

Applies nonparametric Newey-West Bartlett long-run variance corrections to Dickey-Fuller statistics for robustness to general serial correlation and heteroskedasticity. Across empirical econometrics, `phillips_perron` enables researchers to isolate structural parameters while controlling for confounding covariates and verifying identification assumptions via `base Stata pperron` in Stata 18 MP, `urca` in R 4.x, and `arch` in Python 3.11+.

### 1.2. Scope of Application and Sample Structure Requirements

Univariate economic and financial time series paired alongside ADF and KPSS in publication stationarity batteries. Mandatory post-estimation verification includes testing identification restrictions, evaluating goodness-of-fit metrics, and confirming numerical convergence across all three econometric engines.

### 1.3. Structural Equations and Asymptotic Covariance Specification

Core structural equation for `phillips_perron`:

$$
\Delta Y_t = \alpha + \rho Y_{t-1} + u_t, \quad Z_t = t_{\hat{\rho}}\sqrt{\frac{\hat{\gamma}_0}{\hat{\lambda}^2}} - \frac{T(\hat{\lambda}^2 - \hat{\gamma}_0)\text{SE}(\hat{\rho})}{2\hat{\lambda} s_u}
$$

Estimator objective function and sample moment conditions:

$$
\hat{\theta}_T = \arg\max_{\theta \in \Theta} \ell_T\left(Y_1, \dots, Y_T; \theta\right), \quad \Phi(L)(1-L)^d Y_t = \mu + \Theta(L)\varepsilon_t
$$

Asymptotic covariance matrix and post-estimation diagnostic metric:

$$
\text{AICc} = -2\ell_T(\hat{\theta}) + 2k + \frac{2k(k+1)}{T-k-1}, \quad Q_{\text{LB}}(m) = T(T+2)\sum_{\ell=1}^m \frac{\hat{\rho}_\ell^2}{T-\ell} \xrightarrow{d} \chi^2_{m-p-q}
$$
