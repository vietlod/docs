## 1. Methodological Meaning and Scope of Application

### 1.1. Econometric Foundation and Identification Mechanism

Simultaneously estimates G &gt;= 3 household/sector budget-share equations imposing microeconomic adding-up, degree-zero price homogeneity, and Slutsky symmetry, delivering expenditure, Marshallian, and Hicksian elasticities. Across empirical econometrics, `quaids_demand_system` enables researchers to isolate structural parameters while controlling for confounding covariates and verifying identification assumptions via `Stata 18 demandsys` in Stata 18 MP, `micEconAids` in R 4.x, and `linearmodels` in Python 3.11+.

### 1.2. Scope of Application and Sample Structure Requirements

Consumer or factor demand datasets with G &gt;= 3 budget shares w_1..w_G in [0,1] summing to 1, strictly positive prices p_1..p_G &gt; 0, and total expenditure m &gt; 0. Mandatory post-estimation verification includes testing identification restrictions, evaluating goodness-of-fit metrics, and confirming numerical convergence across all three econometric engines.

### 1.3. Structural Equations and Asymptotic Covariance Specification

Core structural equation for `quaids_demand_system`:

$$
w_i = \alpha_i + \sum_{j=1}^K \gamma_{ij}\ln p_j + \beta_i \ln\left[\frac{m}{a(p)}\right] + \frac{\lambda_i}{b(p)}\left\{\ln\left[\frac{m}{a(p)}\right]\right\}^2 + \varepsilon_i
$$

Estimator objective function and sample moment conditions:

$$
\hat{\theta}_{\text{SYS}} = \arg\min_{\theta \in \Theta} g_N(\theta)^\top \left(\hat{\Sigma}^{-1} \otimes P_Z\right) g_N(\theta), \quad p(\theta \mid Y, X) \propto L(Y \mid X, \theta)\,\pi(\theta)
$$

Asymptotic covariance matrix and post-estimation diagnostic metric:

$$
\text{LM}_{\text{BP}} = T \sum_{m=2}^M \sum_{n=1}^{m-1} r_{mn}^2 \xrightarrow{d} \chi^2_{M(M-1)/2}, \quad \hat{R}_{\text{Gelman-Rubin}} = \sqrt{\frac{\frac{L-1}{L}W + \frac{1}{L}B}{W}} \lt 1.01
$$
