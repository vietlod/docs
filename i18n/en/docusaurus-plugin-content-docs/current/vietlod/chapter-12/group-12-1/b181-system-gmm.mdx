## 1. Methodological Meaning and Scope of Application

### 1.1. Econometric Foundation and Identification Mechanism

Estimates the Dynamic System Generalized Method of Moments specification within the Simultaneous Equations and Structural Demand Systems framework by optimizing the structural objective function and computing heteroskedasticity- and cluster-robust asymptotic standard errors. Across empirical econometrics, `system_gmm` enables researchers to isolate structural parameters while controlling for confounding covariates and verifying identification assumptions via `base Stata` in Stata 18 MP, `systemfit / gmm` in R 4.x, and `linearmodels` in Python 3.11+.

### 1.2. Scope of Application and Sample Structure Requirements

Cross-sectional, pooled, or tabular microeconometric datasets requiring robust covariance estimation and specification testing. Mandatory post-estimation verification includes testing identification restrictions, evaluating goodness-of-fit metrics, and confirming numerical convergence across all three econometric engines.

### 1.3. Structural Equations and Asymptotic Covariance Specification

Core structural equation for `system_gmm`:

$$
\hat{\theta}_{\text{SysGMM}} = \arg\min_{\theta} g_N(\theta)^\top \hat{W}_{\text{opt}} g_N(\theta)
$$

Estimator objective function and sample moment conditions:

$$
\hat{\theta}_{\text{SYS}} = \arg\min_{\theta \in \Theta} g_N(\theta)^\top \left(\hat{\Sigma}^{-1} \otimes P_Z\right) g_N(\theta), \quad p(\theta \mid Y, X) \propto L(Y \mid X, \theta)\,\pi(\theta)
$$

Asymptotic covariance matrix and post-estimation diagnostic metric:

$$
\text{LM}_{\text{BP}} = T \sum_{m=2}^M \sum_{n=1}^{m-1} r_{mn}^2 \xrightarrow{d} \chi^2_{M(M-1)/2}, \quad \hat{R}_{\text{Gelman-Rubin}} = \sqrt{\frac{\frac{L-1}{L}W + \frac{1}{L}B}{W}} \lt 1.01
$$
