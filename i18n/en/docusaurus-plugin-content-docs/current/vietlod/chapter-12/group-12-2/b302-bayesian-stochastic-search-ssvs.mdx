## 12.2.4.1. Methodological Meaning and Scope of Application

### 12.2.4.1.1. Econometric Foundation and Identification Mechanism

Places hierarchical latent binary indicators gamma_j in \{0, 1\} over a narrow spike and diffuse Zellner-g slab prior to compute exact/Gibbs Posterior Inclusion Probabilities (PIP) and model-averaged slopes.

### 12.2.4.1.2. Scope of Application and Sample Structure Requirements

Cross-country growth regressions, policy determinant screening, and macro forecasting under model uncertainty where theory suggests many candidate covariates.

- **Mandatory Pre-Estimation Diagnostics**: `design_rank_prior_scale_precheck, vif_multicollinearity`
- **Mandatory Post-Estimation Diagnostics**: `posterior_predictive_r2, aic_bic_information_criteria`

### 12.2.4.1.3. Structural Equations and Asymptotic Covariance Specification

Core structural equation for `bayesian_stochastic_search_ssvs`:

$$
Y_g = X_g \beta_g + \varepsilon_g, \quad \mathbb{E}[\varepsilon \varepsilon^\top \mid X] = \Sigma \otimes I_N
$$

Estimator objective function and sample moment conditions:

$$
p(\theta \mid Y, X) = \frac{p(Y \mid X, \theta)\,\pi(\theta)}{\int_\Theta p(Y \mid X, \tilde{\theta})\,\pi(\tilde{\theta})\,d\tilde{\theta}}
$$

Asymptotic covariance matrix and robust inference metric:

$$
\hat{\beta}_{\text{3SLS}} = \left(Z^\top (\hat{\Sigma}^{-1} \otimes P_W) Z\right)^{-1} Z^\top (\hat{\Sigma}^{-1} \otimes P_W) y
$$
