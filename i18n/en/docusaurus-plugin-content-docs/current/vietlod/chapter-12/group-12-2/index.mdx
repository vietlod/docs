## 1. Methodological Overview and Structural Equations

- **Methodological Focus**: Provides consistent estimation and robust inference for **Bayesian Econometrics and Bayesian Model Averaging** specifications within **Simultaneous Systems, Demand Systems, Bayesian and Machine Learning Econometrics**.
- **Mandatory Post-Estimation Diagnostics**: Chẩn đoán hội tụ chuỗi (MCMC): tỷ số tiềm năng giảm thang đo Gelman–Rubin $\hat{R} \lt 1.01$, cỡ mẫu hiệu dụng $\text{ESS} \gt 400$, biểu đồ vết, tự tương quan chuỗi lấy mẫu và khoảng tin cậy mật độ hậu nghiệm cao nhất $95\%$ (HPD).

Core structural specification for Group 12.2:

$$
p(\beta, \sigma^2 \mid Y, X) \propto p(Y \mid X, \beta, \sigma^2)\,p(\beta \mid \sigma^2)\,p(\sigma^2)
$$

Estimator objective and moment conditions:

$$
\hat{\theta}_{\text{SYS}} = \arg\min_{\theta \in \Theta} g_N(\theta)^\top \left(\hat{\Sigma}^{-1} \otimes P_Z\right) g_N(\theta), \quad p(\theta \mid Y, X) \propto L(Y \mid X, \theta)\,\pi(\theta)
$$
