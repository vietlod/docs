## 12.2.1.1. Methodological Meaning and Scope of Application

### 12.2.1.1.1. Econometric Foundation and Identification Mechanism

Estimates the Conjugate Bayesian Linear Regression specification within the Bayesian Econometrics and Bayesian Model Averaging framework by optimizing the structural objective function and computing heteroskedasticity- and cluster-robust asymptotic standard errors. Across empirical econometrics, `bayesian_linear` enables researchers to isolate structural parameters while controlling for confounding covariates and verifying identification assumptions via `Stata 18 bayes` in Stata 18 MP, `MCMCpack / stats` in R 4.x, and `scipy / statsmodels` in Python 3.11+.

### 12.2.1.1.2. Scope of Application and Sample Structure Requirements

Cross-sectional, pooled, or tabular microeconometric datasets requiring robust covariance estimation and specification testing. Mandatory post-estimation verification includes testing identification restrictions, evaluating goodness-of-fit metrics, and confirming numerical convergence across all three econometric engines.

### 12.2.1.1.3. Structural Equations and Asymptotic Covariance Specification

Core structural equation for `bayesian_linear`:

$$
p(\beta, \sigma^2 \mid Y, X) \propto p(Y \mid X, \beta, \sigma^2)\,p(\beta \mid \sigma^2)\,p(\sigma^2)
$$

Estimator objective function and sample moment conditions:

$$
\hat{\theta}_{\text{SYS}} = \arg\min_{\theta \in \Theta} g_N(\theta)^\top \left(\hat{\Sigma}^{-1} \otimes P_Z\right) g_N(\theta), \quad p(\theta \mid Y, X) \propto L(Y \mid X, \theta)\,\pi(\theta)
$$

Asymptotic covariance matrix and post-estimation diagnostic metric:

$$
\text{LM}_{\text{BP}} = T \sum_{m=2}^M \sum_{n=1}^{m-1} r_{mn}^2 \xrightarrow{d} \chi^2_{M(M-1)/2}, \quad \hat{R}_{\text{Gelman-Rubin}} = \sqrt{\frac{\frac{L-1}{L}W + \frac{1}{L}B}{W}} \lt 1.01
$$
