## 1. Methodological Overview and Structural Equations

- **Methodological Focus**: Provides consistent estimation and robust inference for **Econometric Machine Learning** specifications within **Simultaneous Systems, Demand Systems, Bayesian and Machine Learning Econometrics**.
- **Mandatory Post-Estimation Diagnostics**: Sai số dự báo ngoài túi (OOB) (MSE) và $R^2$, độ quan trọng biến theo hoán vị ngoài mẫu, biểu đồ phụ thuộc riêng phần (PDP) và (ICE) cùng K-Fold (CV).

Core structural specification for Group 12.3:

$$
\hat{f}_{\text{RF}}(x) = \frac{1}{B}\sum_{b=1}^B T_b(x; \Theta_b)
$$

Estimator objective and moment conditions:

$$
\hat{\theta}_{\text{SYS}} = \arg\min_{\theta \in \Theta} g_N(\theta)^\top \left(\hat{\Sigma}^{-1} \otimes P_Z\right) g_N(\theta), \quad p(\theta \mid Y, X) \propto L(Y \mid X, \theta)\,\pi(\theta)
$$
