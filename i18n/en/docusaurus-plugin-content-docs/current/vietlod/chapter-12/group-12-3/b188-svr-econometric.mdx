## 1. Methodological Meaning and Scope of Application

### 1.1. Econometric Foundation and Identification Mechanism

Nonparametric reproducing-kernel Hilbert space regression with epsilon-insensitive loss, K-fold out-of-sample R2 validation, permutation importance, and finite-difference Average Partial Effects. Across empirical econometrics, `svr_econometric` enables researchers to isolate structural parameters while controlling for confounding covariates and verifying identification assumptions via `svmachines` in Stata 18 MP, `e1071` in R 4.x, and `scikit-learn` in Python 3.11+.

### 1.2. Scope of Application and Sample Structure Requirements

Cross-sectional or macro-financial tabular datasets requiring outlier-robust nonlinear approximation and out-of-sample predictive benchmarking. Mandatory post-estimation verification includes testing identification restrictions, evaluating goodness-of-fit metrics, and confirming numerical convergence across all three econometric engines.

### 1.3. Structural Equations and Asymptotic Covariance Specification

Core structural equation for `svr_econometric`:

$$
\min_{w, b, \xi, \xi^\ast} \frac{1}{2}\|w\|_2^2 + C\sum_{i=1}^N (\xi_i + \xi_i^\ast) \quad \text{s.t.} \quad |Y_i - \langle w, \phi(X_i)\rangle - b| \le \epsilon + \xi_i^\ast
$$

Estimator objective function and sample moment conditions:

$$
\hat{\theta}_{\text{SYS}} = \arg\min_{\theta \in \Theta} g_N(\theta)^\top \left(\hat{\Sigma}^{-1} \otimes P_Z\right) g_N(\theta), \quad p(\theta \mid Y, X) \propto L(Y \mid X, \theta)\,\pi(\theta)
$$

Asymptotic covariance matrix and post-estimation diagnostic metric:

$$
\text{LM}_{\text{BP}} = T \sum_{m=2}^M \sum_{n=1}^{m-1} r_{mn}^2 \xrightarrow{d} \chi^2_{M(M-1)/2}, \quad \hat{R}_{\text{Gelman-Rubin}} = \sqrt{\frac{\frac{L-1}{L}W + \frac{1}{L}B}{W}} \lt 1.01
$$
