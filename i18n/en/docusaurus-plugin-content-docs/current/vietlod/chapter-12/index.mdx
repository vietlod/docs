## 12.1. Central Research Question and Core Mathematical Specification

- **Central Research Question**: How to jointly estimate multi-equation structural systems, consumer expenditure shares, Bayesian posterior distributions, and interpretable machine learning models?
- **Chapter Architecture**: Encompasses `4` specialized methodological parts from `12.1` to `12.4` and `12` empirical model lessons validated on Vietnamese datasets from [EcoData](https://ecodata.io.vn) and [Finco](https://www.finco.vn).

Core structural equation for Chapter 12:

$$
w_{ik} = \alpha_k + \sum_{j=1}^G \gamma_{kj}\ln p_{ij} + \beta_k \ln\left[\frac{m_i}{a(p_i)}\right] + \frac{\lambda_k}{b(p_i)}\left\{\ln\left[\frac{m_i}{a(p_i)}\right]\right\}^2 + u_{ik}
$$
