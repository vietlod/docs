## 12.4.1.1. Methodological Meaning and Scope of Application

### 12.4.1.1.1. Econometric Foundation and Identification Mechanism

Trains a deterministic PyTorch CPU Long Short-Term Memory recurrent network on strictly chronological rolling lookback windows and evaluates out-of-sample accuracy against an AR benchmark via Diebold-Mariano test. Across empirical econometrics, `lstm_forecaster` enables researchers to isolate structural parameters while controlling for confounding covariates and verifying identification assumptions via `Stata PyTorch bridge` in Stata 18 MP, `forecast` in R 4.x, and `torch` in Python 3.11+.

### 12.4.1.1.2. Scope of Application and Sample Structure Requirements

Ordered macroeconomic or financial time series — T &gt;= 24 — requiring nonlinear multi-step sequence forecasting without future data leakage. Mandatory post-estimation verification includes testing identification restrictions, evaluating goodness-of-fit metrics, and confirming numerical convergence across all three econometric engines.

### 12.4.1.1.3. Structural Equations and Asymptotic Covariance Specification

Core structural equation for `lstm_forecaster`:

$$
f_t = \sigma(W_f X_t + U_f h_{t-1} + b_f), \quad c_t = f_t \odot c_{t-1} + i_t \odot \tanh(W_c X_t + U_c h_{t-1} + b_c), \quad h_t = o_t \odot \tanh(c_t)
$$

Estimator objective function and sample moment conditions:

$$
\hat{\theta}_{\text{SYS}} = \arg\min_{\theta \in \Theta} g_N(\theta)^\top \left(\hat{\Sigma}^{-1} \otimes P_Z\right) g_N(\theta), \quad p(\theta \mid Y, X) \propto L(Y \mid X, \theta)\,\pi(\theta)
$$

Asymptotic covariance matrix and post-estimation diagnostic metric:

$$
\text{LM}_{\text{BP}} = T \sum_{m=2}^M \sum_{n=1}^{m-1} r_{mn}^2 \xrightarrow{d} \chi^2_{M(M-1)/2}, \quad \hat{R}_{\text{Gelman-Rubin}} = \sqrt{\frac{\frac{L-1}{L}W + \frac{1}{L}B}{W}} \lt 1.01
$$
