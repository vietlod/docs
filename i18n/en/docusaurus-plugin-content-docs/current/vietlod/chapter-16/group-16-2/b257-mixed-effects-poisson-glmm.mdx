## 16.2.2.1. Methodological Meaning and Scope of Application

### 16.2.2.1.1. Econometric Foundation and Identification Mechanism

Models clustered non-negative count data with log-normal random cluster intercepts exp(X_ij*beta + u_i) via Gauss-Hermite marginal likelihood integration, accommodating extra-Poisson cluster overdispersion.

### 16.2.2.1.2. Scope of Application and Sample Structure Requirements

Panel or clustered event counts such as patents per firm, clinic visits per household, or regional enterprise births exhibiting unobserved multiplicative cluster heterogeneity.

- **Mandatory Pre-Estimation Diagnostics**: `cluster_hierarchy_balance_precheck, overdispersion_precheck`
- **Mandatory Post-Estimation Diagnostics**: `glmm_overdispersion_pearson, icc_variance_partition`

### 16.2.2.1.3. Structural Equations and Asymptotic Covariance Specification

Core structural equation for `mixed_effects_poisson_glmm`:

$$
Y_{ij} = X_{ij}^\top \beta + Z_{ij}^\top u_j + \varepsilon_{ij}, \quad u_j \sim \mathcal{N}(0, \Sigma_u), \quad \varepsilon_{ij} \sim \mathcal{N}(0, \sigma_\varepsilon^2)
$$

Estimator objective function and sample moment conditions:

$$
\hat{\beta}_{\text{WLS}} = \left(\sum_{h=1}^H \sum_{c=1}^{C_h} \sum_{i=1}^{n_{hc}} w_{hci} X_{hci} X_{hci}^\top\right)^{-1} \left(\sum_{h=1}^H \sum_{c=1}^{C_h} \sum_{i=1}^{n_{hc}} w_{hci} X_{hci} Y_{hci}\right)
$$

Asymptotic covariance matrix and robust inference metric:

$$
\widehat{\text{Var}}_{\text{TSL}}(\hat{\beta}) = \hat{D}^{-1} \left(\sum_{h=1}^H \frac{C_h}{C_h - 1}\sum_{c=1}^{C_h} (e_{hc} - \bar{e}_h)(e_{hc} - \bar{e}_h)^\top\right) \hat{D}^{-1}, \quad \text{ICC} = \frac{\sigma_u^2}{\sigma_u^2 + \sigma_\varepsilon^2}
$$
