## 16.1.1. Methodological Overview and Structural Equations

- **Methodological Focus**: Consistent estimation and design-based / model-based inference for **Hierarchical Linear and Random-Coefficients Mixed Models** within **Multilevel, Mixed-Effects and Complex Survey Econometrics**.
- **Mandatory Post-Estimation Diagnostics**: Intraclass Correlation Coefficient ICC, boundary Chibar2 likelihood-ratio test, Kish Design Effect DEFF, and DuMouchel–Duncan survey weight exogeneity test.

$$
Y_{ij} = X_{ij}^\top \beta + Z_{ij}^\top u_j + \varepsilon_{ij}, \quad u_j \sim \mathcal{N}(0, \Sigma_u), \quad \varepsilon_{ij} \sim \mathcal{N}(0, \sigma_\varepsilon^2)
$$

$$
\hat{\beta}_{\text{WLS}} = \left(\sum_{h=1}^H \sum_{c=1}^{C_h} \sum_{i=1}^{n_{hc}} w_{hci} X_{hci} X_{hci}^\top\right)^{-1} \left(\sum_{h=1}^H \sum_{c=1}^{C_h} \sum_{i=1}^{n_{hc}} w_{hci} X_{hci} Y_{hci}\right)
$$
