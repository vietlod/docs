## 16.1. Central Research Question and Core Mathematical Specification

- **Central Research Question**: How to estimate hierarchical variance components, generalized mixed-effects outcomes, complex stratified PSU survey variances, and Fay–Herriot small-area poverty/income estimates when units are nested within clusters and sampled with unequal probabilities?
- **Chapter Architecture**: Encompasses `3` methodological parts from `16.1` to `16.3` and `12` tri-engine model lessons.

$$
Y_{ij} = X_{ij}^\top \beta + Z_{ij}^\top u_j + \varepsilon_{ij}, \quad u_j \sim \mathcal{N}(0, \Sigma_u), \quad \varepsilon_{ij} \sim \mathcal{N}(0, \sigma_\varepsilon^2)
$$
