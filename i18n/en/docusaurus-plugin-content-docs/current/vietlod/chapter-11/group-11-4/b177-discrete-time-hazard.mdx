## 11.4.1.1. Methodological Meaning and Scope of Application

### 11.4.1.1.1. Econometric Foundation and Identification Mechanism

Exact discrete-time representation of an underlying continuous proportional hazards process on interval-grouped spells via person-period expansion and complementary log-log GLM. Across empirical econometrics, `discrete_time_hazard` enables researchers to isolate structural parameters while controlling for confounding covariates and verifying identification assumptions via `base Stata cloglog` in Stata 18 MP, `discSurv / stats` in R 4.x, and `statsmodels` in Python 3.11+.

### 11.4.1.1.2. Scope of Application and Sample Structure Requirements

Grouped annual, quarterly, or survey-wave spell durations with heavy ties — duration &gt;= 1, binary event in [0, 1] . Mandatory post-estimation verification includes testing identification restrictions, evaluating goodness-of-fit metrics, and confirming numerical convergence across all three econometric engines.

### 11.4.1.1.3. Structural Equations and Asymptotic Covariance Specification

Core structural equation for `discrete_time_hazard`:

$$
h_{it} = \text{P}(T_i = t \mid T_i \ge t, X_{it}) = 1 - \exp\left(-\exp(\alpha_t + X_{it}^\top \beta)\right) \iff \ln\left(-\ln(1 - h_{it})\right) = \alpha_t + X_{it}^\top \beta
$$

Estimator objective function and sample moment conditions:

$$
\ell_{\text{partial}}(\beta) = \sum_{i=1}^N \delta_i \left[X_i^\top \beta - \ln\left(\sum_{j \in \mathcal{R}(t_i)} \exp(X_j^\top \beta)\right)\right], \quad S(t \mid X_i) = \exp\left(-H_0(t)\exp(X_i^\top \beta)\right)
$$

Asymptotic covariance matrix and post-estimation diagnostic metric:

$$
r_{ik}^{\text{Schoenfeld}} = X_{ik} - \frac{\sum_{j \in \mathcal{R}(t_i)} X_{jk}\exp(X_j^\top \hat{\beta})}{\sum_{j \in \mathcal{R}(t_i)} \exp(X_j^\top \hat{\beta})}, \quad \text{HR}_k = \exp(\hat{\beta}_k)
$$
