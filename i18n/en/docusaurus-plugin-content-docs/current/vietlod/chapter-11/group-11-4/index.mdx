## 1. Methodological Overview and Structural Equations

- **Methodological Focus**: Provides consistent estimation and robust inference for **Discrete-Time Hazard and Competing-Risks Models** specifications within **Survival, Event History and Competing-Risks Analysis**.
- **Mandatory Post-Estimation Diagnostics**: Tỷ số nguy cơ $\exp(\hat{\beta})$ tương đương chính xác tham số Cox liên tục gốc, kiểm định LR dạng hàm nguy cơ cơ sở theo thời gian, deviance AIC/BIC..

Core structural specification for Group 11.4:

$$
h_{it} = \text{P}(T_i = t \mid T_i \ge t, X_{it}) = 1 - \exp\left(-\exp(\alpha_t + X_{it}^\top \beta)\right) \iff \ln\left(-\ln(1 - h_{it})\right) = \alpha_t + X_{it}^\top \beta
$$

Estimator objective and moment conditions:

$$
\ell_{\text{partial}}(\beta) = \sum_{i=1}^N \delta_i \left[X_i^\top \beta - \ln\left(\sum_{j \in \mathcal{R}(t_i)} \exp(X_j^\top \beta)\right)\right], \quad S(t \mid X_i) = \exp\left(-H_0(t)\exp(X_i^\top \beta)\right)
$$
