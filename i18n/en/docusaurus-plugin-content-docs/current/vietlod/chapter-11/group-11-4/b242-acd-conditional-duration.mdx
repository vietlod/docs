## 11.4.3.1. Methodological Meaning and Scope of Application

### 11.4.3.1.1. Econometric Foundation and Identification Mechanism

Models irregularly spaced inter-event waiting times x_i = psi_i * epsilon_i with dynamic conditional expected duration psi_i = E[x_i | F_\{i-1\}] and Bauwens-Giot log-ACD persistence.

### 11.4.3.1.2. Scope of Application and Sample Structure Requirements

Strictly positive inter-trade durations, unemployment spell clustering, or credit event arrival times exhibiting serial duration clustering.

- **Mandatory Pre-Estimation Diagnostics**: `event_per_variable_censoring_precheck, vif_multicollinearity`
- **Mandatory Post-Estimation Diagnostics**: `engle_russell_acd_serial, ljung_box_autocorrelation`

### 11.4.3.1.3. Structural Equations and Asymptotic Covariance Specification

Core structural equation for `acd_conditional_duration`:

$$
h(t \mid X_i) = h_0(t)\exp(X_i^\top \beta), \quad S(t \mid X_i) = \exp\left(-\int_0^t h(u \mid X_i)\,du\right)
$$

Estimator objective function and sample moment conditions:

$$
\ell_{\text{PL}}(\beta) = \sum_{i: \delta_i = 1} \left[X_i^\top \beta - \ln\left(\sum_{j \in \mathcal{R}(t_i)} \exp(X_j^\top \beta)\right)\right]
$$

Asymptotic covariance matrix and robust inference metric:

$$
\text{CIF}_k(t \mid X_i) = \mathbb{P}(T_i \le t, \epsilon_i = k \mid X_i) = 1 - \exp\left(-\Lambda_{k0}(t)\exp(X_i^\top \gamma_k)\right)
$$
