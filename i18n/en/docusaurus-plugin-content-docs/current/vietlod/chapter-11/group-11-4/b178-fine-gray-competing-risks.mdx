## 1. Methodological Meaning and Scope of Application

### 1.1. Econometric Foundation and Identification Mechanism

Directly estimates covariate effects on the Cumulative Incidence Function (CIF) via Inverse Probability of Censoring Weighted (IPCW) subdistribution partial likelihood when competing events preclude the primary failure. Across empirical econometrics, `fine_gray_competing_risks` enables researchers to isolate structural parameters while controlling for confounding covariates and verifying identification assumptions via `base Stata stcrreg` in Stata 18 MP, `cmprsk` in R 4.x, and `lifelines` in Python 3.11+.

### 1.2. Scope of Application and Sample Structure Requirements

Duration data with duration &gt; 0 and multi-state event indicator in [0 = censored, 1 = primary failure, 2 = competing risk] such as firm bankruptcy vs M&A or loan default vs prepayment. Mandatory post-estimation verification includes testing identification restrictions, evaluating goodness-of-fit metrics, and confirming numerical convergence across all three econometric engines.

### 1.3. Structural Equations and Asymptotic Covariance Specification

Core structural equation for `fine_gray_competing_risks`:

$$
\bar{h}_1(t \mid X_i) = \lim_{\Delta t \downarrow 0}\frac{\text{P}\left(t \le T_i \lt t+\Delta t, \epsilon_i = 1 \;\middle|\; T_i \ge t \cup (T_i \le t \cap \epsilon_i \neq 1), X_i\right)}{\Delta t} = \bar{h}_{1,0}(t)\exp(X_i^\top \gamma)
$$

Estimator objective function and sample moment conditions:

$$
\ell_{\text{partial}}(\beta) = \sum_{i=1}^N \delta_i \left[X_i^\top \beta - \ln\left(\sum_{j \in \mathcal{R}(t_i)} \exp(X_j^\top \beta)\right)\right], \quad S(t \mid X_i) = \exp\left(-H_0(t)\exp(X_i^\top \beta)\right)
$$

Asymptotic covariance matrix and post-estimation diagnostic metric:

$$
r_{ik}^{\text{Schoenfeld}} = X_{ik} - \frac{\sum_{j \in \mathcal{R}(t_i)} X_{jk}\exp(X_j^\top \hat{\beta})}{\sum_{j \in \mathcal{R}(t_i)} \exp(X_j^\top \hat{\beta})}, \quad \text{HR}_k = \exp(\hat{\beta}_k)
$$
