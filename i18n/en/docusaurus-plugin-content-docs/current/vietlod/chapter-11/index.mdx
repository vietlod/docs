## 1. Central Research Question and Core Mathematical Specification

- **Central Research Question**: How to model duration until an economic or financial event under right censoring, time-varying covariates, unobserved frailty, and competing exit risks?
- **Chapter Architecture**: Encompasses `4` specialized methodological groups and `8` empirical models validated on Vietnamese datasets from [EcoData](https://ecodata.io.vn) and [Finco](https://www.finco.vn).

Core structural equation for Chapter 11:

$$
h(t \mid X_i) = h_0(t)\exp(X_i^\top \beta), \quad S(t \mid X_i) = \exp\left(-\int_0^t h(u \mid X_i)\,du\right)
$$

## 3. Four-Step Workflow for Selecting and Executing Models in Chapter 11

<Steps>

### Step 1. Select the Appropriate Methodological Group Across the 4 Groups

Match your economic hypothesis and sample structure against the identification requirements from Group `11.1` through Group `11.4`.

### Step 2. Prepare Standardized Datasets From EcoData or Finco

Download the standardized `.csv` practice file for your target estimator from `/src/ecoaz/data/` or extract live empirical series from [EcoData](https://ecodata.io.vn) and [Finco](https://www.finco.vn).

### Step 3. Estimate Parameters and Run Diagnostics in Stata 18, R 4.x, and Python 3.11+

Execute the tri-engine scripts provided in each model guide and verify the mandatory post-estimation diagnostics: **Log-Rank & Wilcoxon Test, Grambsch–Therneau Schoenfeld Proportional Hazards Test, Cox–Snell Residuals, Gray's CIF Equality Test**.

### Step 4. Verify Three-Software Convergence and Export Publication Tables on EcoLab

Confirm numerical convergence across Stata, R, and Python using the three-panel empirical figure and generate journal-grade tables on [EcoLab](https://ecolab.io.vn).
