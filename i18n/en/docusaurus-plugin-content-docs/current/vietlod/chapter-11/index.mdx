## 11.1. Central Research Question and Core Mathematical Specification

- **Central Research Question**: How to model duration until an economic or financial event under right censoring, time-varying covariates, unobserved frailty, and competing exit risks?
- **Chapter Architecture**: Encompasses `4` specialized methodological parts from `11.1` to `11.4` and `8` empirical model lessons validated on Vietnamese datasets from [EcoData](https://ecodata.io.vn) and [Finco](https://www.finco.vn).

Core structural equation for Chapter 11:

$$
h(t \mid X_i) = h_0(t)\exp(X_i^\top \beta), \quad S(t \mid X_i) = \exp\left(-\int_0^t h(u \mid X_i)\,du\right)
$$
