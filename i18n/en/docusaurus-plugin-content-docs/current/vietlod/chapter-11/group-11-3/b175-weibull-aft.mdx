## 1. Methodological Meaning and Scope of Application

### 1.1. Econometric Foundation and Identification Mechanism

Estimates the Weibull Accelerated Failure Time — AFT specification within the Parametric Accelerated Failure Time Models framework by optimizing the structural objective function and computing heteroskedasticity- and cluster-robust asymptotic standard errors. Across empirical econometrics, `weibull_aft` enables researchers to isolate structural parameters while controlling for confounding covariates and verifying identification assumptions via `base Stata` in Stata 18 MP, `survival` in R 4.x, and `lifelines` in Python 3.11+.

### 1.2. Scope of Application and Sample Structure Requirements

Cross-sectional, pooled, or tabular microeconometric datasets requiring robust covariance estimation and specification testing. Mandatory post-estimation verification includes testing identification restrictions, evaluating goodness-of-fit metrics, and confirming numerical convergence across all three econometric engines.

### 1.3. Structural Equations and Asymptotic Covariance Specification

Core structural equation for `weibull_aft`:

$$
\ln(T_i) = X_i^\top \beta + \sigma \varepsilon_i, \quad f(\varepsilon) = \exp(\varepsilon - e^\varepsilon)
$$

Estimator objective function and sample moment conditions:

$$
\ell_{\text{partial}}(\beta) = \sum_{i=1}^N \delta_i \left[X_i^\top \beta - \ln\left(\sum_{j \in \mathcal{R}(t_i)} \exp(X_j^\top \beta)\right)\right], \quad S(t \mid X_i) = \exp\left(-H_0(t)\exp(X_i^\top \beta)\right)
$$

Asymptotic covariance matrix and post-estimation diagnostic metric:

$$
r_{ik}^{\text{Schoenfeld}} = X_{ik} - \frac{\sum_{j \in \mathcal{R}(t_i)} X_{jk}\exp(X_j^\top \hat{\beta})}{\sum_{j \in \mathcal{R}(t_i)} \exp(X_j^\top \hat{\beta})}, \quad \text{HR}_k = \exp(\hat{\beta}_k)
$$
