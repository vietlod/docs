## 1. Central Research Question and Core Mathematical Specification

- **Central Research Question**: How to decompose wage and welfare gaps into endowment and structural return effects across the mean and the entire unconditional quantile distribution?
- **Chapter Architecture**: Encompasses `1` specialized methodological groups and `3` empirical models validated on Vietnamese datasets from [EcoData](https://ecodata.io.vn) and [Finco](https://www.finco.vn).

Core structural equation for Chapter 13:

$$
\text{RIF}(y; q_\tau) = q_\tau + \frac{\tau - \mathbb{I}(y \le q_\tau)}{f_Y(q_\tau)}, \quad \Delta_O^\tau = \Delta_X^\tau + \Delta_S^\tau
$$
