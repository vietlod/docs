## 1. Methodological Meaning and Scope of Application

### 1.1. Econometric Foundation and Identification Mechanism

Estimates marginal effects of covariate shifts on unconditional population quantiles Q_tau — tau in [0.10, 0.50, 0.90] , interdecile Q90-Q10 spread, or the Gini inequality index via kernel-density RIF transformation. Across empirical econometrics, `rif_regression` enables researchers to isolate structural parameters while controlling for confounding covariates and verifying identification assumptions via `rifhdreg` in Stata 18 MP, `rifreg` in R 4.x, and `statsmodels` in Python 3.11+.

### 1.2. Scope of Application and Sample Structure Requirements

Cross-sectional or panel microdata evaluating distributional and inequality impacts of policy covariates across the entire population distribution. Mandatory post-estimation verification includes testing identification restrictions, evaluating goodness-of-fit metrics, and confirming numerical convergence across all three econometric engines.

### 1.3. Structural Equations and Asymptotic Covariance Specification

Core structural equation for `rif_regression`:

$$
\text{RIF}(y; q_\tau, F_Y) = q_\tau + \frac{\tau - \mathbb{I}(y \le q_\tau)}{f_Y(q_\tau)}, \quad \text{E}\left[\text{RIF}(Y; \nu, F_Y) \;\middle|\; X\right] = X^\top \gamma_\nu
$$

Estimator objective function and sample moment conditions:

$$
\Delta_\nu = \nu(F_{Y_A}) - \nu(F_{Y_B}) = \left(\bar{X}_A - \bar{X}_B\right)^\top \hat{\gamma}_\nu^\ast + \bar{X}_A^\top\left(\hat{\gamma}_{\nu,A} - \hat{\gamma}_\nu^\ast\right) + \bar{X}_B^\top\left(\hat{\gamma}_\nu^\ast - \hat{\gamma}_{\nu,B}\right)
$$

Asymptotic covariance matrix and post-estimation diagnostic metric:

$$
\widehat{\text{Var}}\left(\hat{\Delta}_\nu^X\right) = (\bar{X}_A - \bar{X}_B)^\top \widehat{\text{Var}}(\hat{\gamma}_\nu^\ast)(\bar{X}_A - \bar{X}_B) + \hat{\gamma}_\nu^{\ast\top}\left(\widehat{\text{Var}}(\bar{X}_A) + \widehat{\text{Var}}(\bar{X}_B)\right)\hat{\gamma}_\nu^\ast
$$
