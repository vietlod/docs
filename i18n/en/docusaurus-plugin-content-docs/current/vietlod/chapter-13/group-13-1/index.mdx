## 1. Methodological Overview and Structural Equations

- **Methodological Focus**: Provides consistent estimation and robust inference for **Inequality Decomposition and Distributional Regression** specifications within **Income Distribution and Inequality Decomposition**.
- **Mandatory Post-Estimation Diagnostics**: Kiểm định Wald ý nghĩa của Total Gap, Explained, Unexplained và Interaction; đóng góp chi tiết từng biến $X_k$ kèm SE chuẩn hóa Yun (2005)..

Core structural specification for Group 13.1:

$$
\bar{Y}_A - \bar{Y}_B = \underbrace{(\bar{X}_A - \bar{X}_B)^\top \hat{\beta}^\ast}_{\text{Hiệu ứng đặc điểm}} + \underbrace{\bar{X}_A^\top(\hat{\beta}_A - \hat{\beta}^\ast) + \bar{X}_B^\top(\hat{\beta}^\ast - \hat{\beta}_B)}_{\text{Hiệu ứng hệ số}}
$$

Estimator objective and moment conditions:

$$
\Delta_\nu = \nu(F_{Y_A}) - \nu(F_{Y_B}) = \left(\bar{X}_A - \bar{X}_B\right)^\top \hat{\gamma}_\nu^\ast + \bar{X}_A^\top\left(\hat{\gamma}_{\nu,A} - \hat{\gamma}_\nu^\ast\right) + \bar{X}_B^\top\left(\hat{\gamma}_\nu^\ast - \hat{\gamma}_{\nu,B}\right)
$$
