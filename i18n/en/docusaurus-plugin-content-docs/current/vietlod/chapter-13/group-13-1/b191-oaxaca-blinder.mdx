## 13.1.1.1. Methodological Meaning and Scope of Application

### 13.1.1.1.1. Econometric Foundation and Identification Mechanism

Decomposes the mean outcome differential E[Y_A] - E[Y_B] between two groups into Endowments — explained composition , Coefficients — unexplained structure , and Interaction components with variable-level Delta-method SE. Across empirical econometrics, `oaxaca_blinder` enables researchers to isolate structural parameters while controlling for confounding covariates and verifying identification assumptions via `oaxaca` in Stata 18 MP, `oaxaca` in R 4.x, and `statsmodels` in Python 3.11+.

### 13.1.1.1.2. Scope of Application and Sample Structure Requirements

Microdata — wage, productivity, consumption, credit — with a continuous outcome Y, covariates X, and a binary two-group indicator — group or treatment . Mandatory post-estimation verification includes testing identification restrictions, evaluating goodness-of-fit metrics, and confirming numerical convergence across all three econometric engines.

### 13.1.1.1.3. Structural Equations and Asymptotic Covariance Specification

Core structural equation for `oaxaca_blinder`:

$$
\bar{Y}_A - \bar{Y}_B = \underbrace{(\bar{X}_A - \bar{X}_B)^\top \hat{\beta}^\ast}_{\text{Hiệu ứng đặc điểm}} + \underbrace{\bar{X}_A^\top(\hat{\beta}_A - \hat{\beta}^\ast) + \bar{X}_B^\top(\hat{\beta}^\ast - \hat{\beta}_B)}_{\text{Hiệu ứng hệ số}}
$$

Estimator objective function and sample moment conditions:

$$
\Delta_\nu = \nu(F_{Y_A}) - \nu(F_{Y_B}) = \left(\bar{X}_A - \bar{X}_B\right)^\top \hat{\gamma}_\nu^\ast + \bar{X}_A^\top\left(\hat{\gamma}_{\nu,A} - \hat{\gamma}_\nu^\ast\right) + \bar{X}_B^\top\left(\hat{\gamma}_\nu^\ast - \hat{\gamma}_{\nu,B}\right)
$$

Asymptotic covariance matrix and post-estimation diagnostic metric:

$$
\widehat{\text{Var}}\left(\hat{\Delta}_\nu^X\right) = (\bar{X}_A - \bar{X}_B)^\top \widehat{\text{Var}}(\hat{\gamma}_\nu^\ast)(\bar{X}_A - \bar{X}_B) + \hat{\gamma}_\nu^{\ast\top}\left(\widehat{\text{Var}}(\bar{X}_A) + \widehat{\text{Var}}(\bar{X}_B)\right)\hat{\gamma}_\nu^\ast
$$
