## 13.1.4.1. Methodological Meaning and Scope of Application

### 13.1.4.1.1. Econometric Foundation and Identification Mechanism

Integrates conditional distribution F_\{Y|X\}(y|x) and quantile Q_\{Y|X\}(tau|x) processes over covariate distributions to separate structure effects from composition effects with Kolmogorov-Smirnov uniform bands.

### 13.1.4.1.2. Scope of Application and Sample Structure Requirements

Wage-gap, household-consumption inequality, and welfare counterfactual decompositions across entire outcome distributions rather than solely at the mean.

- **Mandatory Pre-Estimation Diagnostics**: `group_sample_balance_precheck, vif_multicollinearity`
- **Mandatory Post-Estimation Diagnostics**: `breusch_pagan_heteroskedasticity, wald_linear_restriction`

### 13.1.4.1.3. Structural Equations and Asymptotic Covariance Specification

Core structural equation for `chernozhukov_counterfactual_dist`:

$$
\Delta_O^\nu = \nu(F_{Y_B}) - \nu(F_{Y_A}) = \underbrace{\left(\nu(F_{Y_B}) - \nu(F_{Y_C})\right)}_{\Delta_S^\nu\text{ (Structure)}} + \underbrace{\left(\nu(F_{Y_C}) - \nu(F_{Y_A})\right)}_{\Delta_X^\nu\text{ (Composition)}}
$$

Estimator objective function and sample moment conditions:

$$
\text{RIF}(y; \nu, F_Y) = \nu(F_Y) + \text{IF}(y; \nu, F_Y), \quad \mathbb{E}[\text{RIF}(Y; \nu, F_Y) \mid X] = X^\top \gamma_\nu
$$

Asymptotic covariance matrix and robust inference metric:

$$
I_\alpha(Y) = \frac{1}{\alpha(\alpha - 1)}\left[\frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^N \left(\frac{Y_i}{\bar{Y}}\right)^\alpha - 1\right]
$$
