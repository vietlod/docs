## 13.1.3.1. Methodological Meaning and Scope of Application

### 13.1.3.1.1. Econometric Foundation and Identification Mechanism

Combines DiNardo-Fortin-Lemieux counterfactual propensity reweighting with RIF regressions at quantiles Q_tau or Gini to separate Pure Composition, Specification Error, Pure Wage/Return Structure, and Reweighting Error. Across empirical econometrics, `oaxaca_rif_decomposition` enables researchers to isolate structural parameters while controlling for confounding covariates and verifying identification assumptions via `oaxaca_rif` in Stata 18 MP, `rifreg + oaxaca` in R 4.x, and `statsmodels` in Python 3.11+.

### 13.1.3.1.2. Scope of Application and Sample Structure Requirements

Two-group microdata comparing distributional gaps at upper/lower tails — Q10, Q50, Q90 — or Gini inequality. Mandatory post-estimation verification includes testing identification restrictions, evaluating goodness-of-fit metrics, and confirming numerical convergence across all three econometric engines.

### 13.1.3.1.3. Structural Equations and Asymptotic Covariance Specification

Core structural equation for `oaxaca_rif_decomposition`:

$$
\Delta_\nu = \nu(F_{Y_A}) - \nu(F_{Y_B}) = \underbrace{\left(\nu(F_{Y_A}) - \nu(F_{Y_C})\right)}_{\Delta_\nu^S\text{ — Cấu trúc}} + \underbrace{\left(\nu(F_{Y_C}) - \nu(F_{Y_B})\right)}_{\Delta_\nu^X\text{ — Đặc điểm}}
$$

Estimator objective function and sample moment conditions:

$$
\Delta_\nu = \nu(F_{Y_A}) - \nu(F_{Y_B}) = \left(\bar{X}_A - \bar{X}_B\right)^\top \hat{\gamma}_\nu^\ast + \bar{X}_A^\top\left(\hat{\gamma}_{\nu,A} - \hat{\gamma}_\nu^\ast\right) + \bar{X}_B^\top\left(\hat{\gamma}_\nu^\ast - \hat{\gamma}_{\nu,B}\right)
$$

Asymptotic covariance matrix and post-estimation diagnostic metric:

$$
\widehat{\text{Var}}\left(\hat{\Delta}_\nu^X\right) = (\bar{X}_A - \bar{X}_B)^\top \widehat{\text{Var}}(\hat{\gamma}_\nu^\ast)(\bar{X}_A - \bar{X}_B) + \hat{\gamma}_\nu^{\ast\top}\left(\widehat{\text{Var}}(\bar{X}_A) + \widehat{\text{Var}}(\bar{X}_B)\right)\hat{\gamma}_\nu^\ast
$$
