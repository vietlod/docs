## 1. Methodological Meaning and Scope of Application

### 1.1. Econometric Foundation and Identification Mechanism

Gold-standard bilateral trade gravity and non-negative flow estimator consistent under arbitrary heteroskedasticity and zero flows. Across empirical econometrics, `ppml_hdfe` enables researchers to isolate structural parameters while controlling for confounding covariates and verifying identification assumptions via `ppmlhdfe` in Stata 18 MP, `fixest` in R 4.x, and `pyfixest` in Python 3.11+.

### 1.2. Scope of Application and Sample Structure Requirements

Non-negative continuous or count outcome Y &gt;= 0 including exact zeros, with optional multi-way fixed effects. Mandatory post-estimation verification includes testing identification restrictions, evaluating goodness-of-fit metrics, and confirming numerical convergence across all three econometric engines.

### 1.3. Structural Equations and Asymptotic Covariance Specification

Core structural equation for `ppml_hdfe`:

$$
Y_{ijt} = \exp\left(X_{ijt}^\top \beta + \mu_{it} + \nu_{jt} + \gamma_{ij}\right)\eta_{ijt}, \quad \sum_{i,j,t} \left(Y_{ijt} - \exp(X_{ijt}^\top \hat{\beta} + \hat{\mu}_{it} + \hat{\nu}_{jt} + \hat{\gamma}_{ij})\right)X_{ijt} = 0
$$

Estimator objective function and sample moment conditions:

$$
\hat{\theta}_{\text{QMLE}} = \arg\max_{\theta \in \Theta} \sum_{i=1}^N \ln f\left(Y_i \mid X_i, Z_i; \theta\right), \quad \text{E}[Y_i \mid X_i] = g^{-1}(X_i^\top \beta)
$$

Asymptotic covariance matrix and post-estimation diagnostic metric:

$$
\widehat{\text{Var}}_{\text{QMLE}}(\hat{\theta}) = \hat{H}_N^{-1}\left(\sum_{i=1}^N s_i(\hat{\theta}) s_i(\hat{\theta})^\top\right)\hat{H}_N^{-1}, \quad \text{Var}(Y_i \mid X_i) = \mu_i + \alpha \mu_i^p
$$
