## 4.1.1. Methodological Overview and Structural Equations

- **Methodological Focus**: Provides consistent estimation and robust inference for **Baseline Count and Trade Gravity Models** specifications within **Count Data, Trade Gravity and Limited Dependent Variable Models**.
- **Mandatory Post-Estimation Diagnostics**: Kiểm định quá phân tán của Cameron–Trivedi và Deviance/Pearson goodness-of-fit, sai số chuẩn vững (QMLE) và tỷ số tần suất biến cố (IRR).

Core structural specification for Part 4.1:

$$
P(Y_i = y \mid X_i) = \frac{\exp(-\mu_i)\mu_i^y}{y!}, \quad \mu_i = \exp(X_i^\top \beta)
$$

Estimator objective and moment conditions:

$$
\hat{\theta}_{\text{QMLE}} = \arg\max_{\theta \in \Theta} \sum_{i=1}^N \ln f\left(Y_i \mid X_i, Z_i; \theta\right), \quad \text{E}[Y_i \mid X_i] = g^{-1}(X_i^\top \beta)
$$
