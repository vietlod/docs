## 4.1.5.1. Methodological Meaning and Scope of Application

### 4.1.5.1.1. Econometric Foundation and Identification Mechanism

Estimates power-variance exponential dispersion GLMs Var(Y|X) = phi * mu^p (1 &lt; p &lt;= 3) with canonical/log links for right-skewed semicontinuous expenditures, insurance losses, and strictly positive outcomes.

### 4.1.5.1.2. Scope of Application and Sample Structure Requirements

Skewed non-negative or strictly positive continuous economic outcomes such as medical spending, claim severity, rainfall, or firm R&D intensity with power-law mean-variance scaling.

- **Mandatory Pre-Estimation Diagnostics**: `overdispersion_precheck, vif_multicollinearity`
- **Mandatory Post-Estimation Diagnostics**: `pearson_dispersion_gof, aic_bic_information_criteria`

### 4.1.5.1.3. Structural Equations and Asymptotic Covariance Specification

Core structural equation for `inverse_gaussian_tweedie_glm`:

$$
\mathbb{E}[Y_i \mid X_i] = \exp(X_i^\top \beta), \quad Y_i^\ast = X_i^\top \beta + u_i
$$

Estimator objective function and sample moment conditions:

$$
\ell_N(\theta) = \sum_{i=1}^N \ln f(Y_i \mid X_i; \theta), \quad \frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^N \frac{\partial \ell_i(\hat{\theta})}{\partial \theta} = 0
$$

Asymptotic covariance matrix and robust inference metric:

$$
\widehat{\text{AME}}_k = \frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^N \frac{\partial \widehat{\mathbb{E}}[Y_i \mid X_i]}{\partial X_{ik}}, \quad \widehat{\text{Var}}(\hat{\theta}) = \hat{A}^{-1}\hat{B}\hat{A}^{-1}
$$
