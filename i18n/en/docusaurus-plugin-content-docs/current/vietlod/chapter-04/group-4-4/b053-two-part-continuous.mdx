## 4.4.2.1. Methodological Meaning and Scope of Application

### 4.4.2.1.1. Econometric Foundation and Identification Mechanism

Combines Logit participation for zero mass with Gamma Log-link GLM for heavy-tailed positive expenditures and combined delta-method AME. Across empirical econometrics, `two_part_continuous` enables researchers to isolate structural parameters while controlling for confounding covariates and verifying identification assumptions via `twopm` in Stata 18 MP, `twopartm` in R 4.x, and `statsmodels` in Python 3.11+.

### 4.4.2.1.2. Scope of Application and Sample Structure Requirements

Non-negative semicontinuous expenditure or investment Y &gt;= 0 with substantial zeros and right-skewed positive values. Mandatory post-estimation verification includes testing identification restrictions, evaluating goodness-of-fit metrics, and confirming numerical convergence across all three econometric engines.

### 4.4.2.1.3. Structural Equations and Asymptotic Covariance Specification

Core structural equation for `two_part_continuous`:

$$
\text{E}[Y_i \mid X_i] = \text{P}(Y_i \gt 0 \mid X_i) \cdot \text{E}[Y_i \mid Y_i \gt 0, X_i] = \Lambda(X_i^\top \gamma) \cdot \exp(X_i^\top \beta)
$$

Estimator objective function and sample moment conditions:

$$
\hat{\theta}_{\text{QMLE}} = \arg\max_{\theta \in \Theta} \sum_{i=1}^N \ln f\left(Y_i \mid X_i, Z_i; \theta\right), \quad \text{E}[Y_i \mid X_i] = g^{-1}(X_i^\top \beta)
$$

Asymptotic covariance matrix and post-estimation diagnostic metric:

$$
\widehat{\text{Var}}_{\text{QMLE}}(\hat{\theta}) = \hat{H}_N^{-1}\left(\sum_{i=1}^N s_i(\hat{\theta}) s_i(\hat{\theta})^\top\right)\hat{H}_N^{-1}, \quad \text{Var}(Y_i \mid X_i) = \mu_i + \alpha \mu_i^p
$$
