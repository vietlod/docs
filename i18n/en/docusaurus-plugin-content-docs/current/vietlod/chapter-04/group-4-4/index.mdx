## 4.4.1. Methodological Overview and Structural Equations

- **Methodological Focus**: Provides consistent estimation and robust inference for **Corner Solution and Fractional Response Models** specifications within **Count Data, Trade Gravity and Limited Dependent Variable Models**.
- **Mandatory Post-Estimation Diagnostics**: Kiểm định LR lồng nhau Cragg Double-Hurdle vs Tobit — $\text{LR} = -2[\ell_{\text{Tobit}} - (\ell_{\text{Probit}} + \ell_{\text{Trunc}})]$, AME $P(Y\gt 0)$ & $E[Y\mid Y\gt 0]$..

Core structural specification for Part 4.4:

$$
Y_i = D_i \cdot Y_i^\ast, \quad D_i = \mathbb{I}(Z_i^\top \gamma + u_i \gt 0), \quad Y_i^\ast = \max(0, X_i^\top \beta + \varepsilon_i), \quad (u_i, \varepsilon_i) \sim \mathcal{N}_2(0, \Sigma)
$$

Estimator objective and moment conditions:

$$
\hat{\theta}_{\text{QMLE}} = \arg\max_{\theta \in \Theta} \sum_{i=1}^N \ln f\left(Y_i \mid X_i, Z_i; \theta\right), \quad \text{E}[Y_i \mid X_i] = g^{-1}(X_i^\top \beta)
$$
