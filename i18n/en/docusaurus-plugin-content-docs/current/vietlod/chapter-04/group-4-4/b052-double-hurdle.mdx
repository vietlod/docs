## 4.4.1.1. Methodological Meaning and Scope of Application

### 4.4.1.1.1. Econometric Foundation and Identification Mechanism

Generalizes Type-I Tobit for corner-solution outcomes Y &gt;= 0 by allowing distinct parameter vectors for participation and positive expenditure. Across empirical econometrics, `double_hurdle` enables researchers to isolate structural parameters while controlling for confounding covariates and verifying identification assumptions via `Stata churdle` in Stata 18 MP, `mhurdle` in R 4.x, and `statsmodels / scipy` in Python 3.11+.

### 4.4.1.1.2. Scope of Application and Sample Structure Requirements

Non-negative continuous outcome Y &gt;= 0 with a point mass at zero. Mandatory post-estimation verification includes testing identification restrictions, evaluating goodness-of-fit metrics, and confirming numerical convergence across all three econometric engines.

### 4.4.1.1.3. Structural Equations and Asymptotic Covariance Specification

Core structural equation for `double_hurdle`:

$$
Y_i = D_i \cdot Y_i^\ast, \quad D_i = \mathbb{I}(Z_i^\top \gamma + u_i \gt 0), \quad Y_i^\ast = \max(0, X_i^\top \beta + \varepsilon_i), \quad (u_i, \varepsilon_i) \sim \mathcal{N}_2(0, \Sigma)
$$

Estimator objective function and sample moment conditions:

$$
\hat{\theta}_{\text{QMLE}} = \arg\max_{\theta \in \Theta} \sum_{i=1}^N \ln f\left(Y_i \mid X_i, Z_i; \theta\right), \quad \text{E}[Y_i \mid X_i] = g^{-1}(X_i^\top \beta)
$$

Asymptotic covariance matrix and post-estimation diagnostic metric:

$$
\widehat{\text{Var}}_{\text{QMLE}}(\hat{\theta}) = \hat{H}_N^{-1}\left(\sum_{i=1}^N s_i(\hat{\theta}) s_i(\hat{\theta})^\top\right)\hat{H}_N^{-1}, \quad \text{Var}(Y_i \mid X_i) = \mu_i + \alpha \mu_i^p
$$
