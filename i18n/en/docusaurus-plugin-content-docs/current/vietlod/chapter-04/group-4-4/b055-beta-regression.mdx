## 4.4.4.1. Methodological Meaning and Scope of Application

### 4.4.4.1.1. Econometric Foundation and Identification Mechanism

Models continuous rates, shares, and proportions strictly inside — 0, 1 — with logit mean link and log precision parameter. Across empirical econometrics, `beta_regression` enables researchers to isolate structural parameters while controlling for confounding covariates and verifying identification assumptions via `Stata betareg` in Stata 18 MP, `betareg` in R 4.x, and `statsmodels` in Python 3.11+.

### 4.4.4.1.2. Scope of Application and Sample Structure Requirements

Continuous dependent variable strictly inside the open unit interval — 0, 1 . Mandatory post-estimation verification includes testing identification restrictions, evaluating goodness-of-fit metrics, and confirming numerical convergence across all three econometric engines.

### 4.4.4.1.3. Structural Equations and Asymptotic Covariance Specification

Core structural equation for `beta_regression`:

$$
f(y_i; \mu_i, \phi) = \frac{\Gamma(\phi)}{\Gamma(\mu_i \phi)\Gamma((1-\mu_i)\phi)} y_i^{\mu_i\phi - 1}(1-y_i)^{(1-\mu_i)\phi - 1}, \quad g(\mu_i) = X_i^\top \beta, \quad y_i \in (0, 1)
$$

Estimator objective function and sample moment conditions:

$$
\hat{\theta}_{\text{QMLE}} = \arg\max_{\theta \in \Theta} \sum_{i=1}^N \ln f\left(Y_i \mid X_i, Z_i; \theta\right), \quad \text{E}[Y_i \mid X_i] = g^{-1}(X_i^\top \beta)
$$

Asymptotic covariance matrix and post-estimation diagnostic metric:

$$
\widehat{\text{Var}}_{\text{QMLE}}(\hat{\theta}) = \hat{H}_N^{-1}\left(\sum_{i=1}^N s_i(\hat{\theta}) s_i(\hat{\theta})^\top\right)\hat{H}_N^{-1}, \quad \text{Var}(Y_i \mid X_i) = \mu_i + \alpha \mu_i^p
$$
