## 1. Methodological Meaning and Scope of Application

### 1.1. Econometric Foundation and Identification Mechanism

Decouples the binary decision to cross zero from the zero-truncated count distribution governing positive outcomes. Across empirical econometrics, `hurdle_count` enables researchers to isolate structural parameters while controlling for confounding covariates and verifying identification assumptions via `Stata churdle` in Stata 18 MP, `pscl` in R 4.x, and `statsmodels` in Python 3.11+.

### 1.2. Scope of Application and Sample Structure Requirements

Non-negative integer count data containing both zeros and positive counts. Mandatory post-estimation verification includes testing identification restrictions, evaluating goodness-of-fit metrics, and confirming numerical convergence across all three econometric engines.

### 1.3. Structural Equations and Asymptotic Covariance Specification

Core structural equation for `hurdle_count`:

$$
\text{P}(Y_i = 0 \mid Z_i) = 1 - F_1(Z_i^\top \gamma), \quad \text{P}(Y_i = y \mid X_i, Z_i) = F_1(Z_i^\top \gamma)\frac{f_2(y \mid X_i^\top \beta)}{1 - f_2(0 \mid X_i^\top \beta)}, \quad y \ge 1
$$

Estimator objective function and sample moment conditions:

$$
\hat{\theta}_{\text{QMLE}} = \arg\max_{\theta \in \Theta} \sum_{i=1}^N \ln f\left(Y_i \mid X_i, Z_i; \theta\right), \quad \text{E}[Y_i \mid X_i] = g^{-1}(X_i^\top \beta)
$$

Asymptotic covariance matrix and post-estimation diagnostic metric:

$$
\widehat{\text{Var}}_{\text{QMLE}}(\hat{\theta}) = \hat{H}_N^{-1}\left(\sum_{i=1}^N s_i(\hat{\theta}) s_i(\hat{\theta})^\top\right)\hat{H}_N^{-1}, \quad \text{Var}(Y_i \mid X_i) = \mu_i + \alpha \mu_i^p
$$
