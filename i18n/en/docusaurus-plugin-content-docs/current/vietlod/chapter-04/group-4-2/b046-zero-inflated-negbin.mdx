## 4.2.2.1. Methodological Meaning and Scope of Application

### 4.2.2.1.1. Econometric Foundation and Identification Mechanism

Estimates the Zero-Inflated Negative Binomial — ZINB specification within the Zero-Inflated and Hurdle Count Models framework by optimizing the structural objective function and computing heteroskedasticity- and cluster-robust asymptotic standard errors. Across empirical econometrics, `zero_inflated_negbin` enables researchers to isolate structural parameters while controlling for confounding covariates and verifying identification assumptions via `base Stata` in Stata 18 MP, `pscl` in R 4.x, and `statsmodels` in Python 3.11+.

### 4.2.2.1.2. Scope of Application and Sample Structure Requirements

Cross-sectional, pooled, or tabular microeconometric datasets requiring robust covariance estimation and specification testing. Mandatory post-estimation verification includes testing identification restrictions, evaluating goodness-of-fit metrics, and confirming numerical convergence across all three econometric engines.

### 4.2.2.1.3. Structural Equations and Asymptotic Covariance Specification

Core structural equation for `zero_inflated_negbin`:

$$
\text{E}[Y_i \mid X_i, Z_i] = (1 - \pi_i)\mu_i, \quad \text{Var}(Y_i) = (1-\pi_i)\mu_i(1 + \alpha\mu_i + \pi_i\mu_i)
$$

Estimator objective function and sample moment conditions:

$$
\hat{\theta}_{\text{QMLE}} = \arg\max_{\theta \in \Theta} \sum_{i=1}^N \ln f\left(Y_i \mid X_i, Z_i; \theta\right), \quad \text{E}[Y_i \mid X_i] = g^{-1}(X_i^\top \beta)
$$

Asymptotic covariance matrix and post-estimation diagnostic metric:

$$
\widehat{\text{Var}}_{\text{QMLE}}(\hat{\theta}) = \hat{H}_N^{-1}\left(\sum_{i=1}^N s_i(\hat{\theta}) s_i(\hat{\theta})^\top\right)\hat{H}_N^{-1}, \quad \text{Var}(Y_i \mid X_i) = \mu_i + \alpha \mu_i^p
$$
