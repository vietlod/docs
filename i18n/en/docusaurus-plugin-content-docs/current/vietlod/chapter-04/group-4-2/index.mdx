## 1. Methodological Overview and Structural Equations

- **Methodological Focus**: Provides consistent estimation and robust inference for **Zero-Inflated and Hurdle Count Models** specifications within **Count Data, Trade Gravity and Limited Dependent Variable Models**.
- **Mandatory Post-Estimation Diagnostics**: So sánh tiêu chuẩn (AIC) và (BIC) và kiểm định Vuong hiệu chỉnh Wilson (2015) giữa (ZIP) với Poisson chuẩn, kiểm định quá phân tán so với (ZINB).

Core structural specification for Group 4.2:

$$
P(Y_i = 0) = \pi_i + (1 - \pi_i)e^{-\mu_i}, \quad P(Y_i = y) = (1 - \pi_i)\frac{e^{-\mu_i}\mu_i^y}{y!}
$$

Estimator objective and moment conditions:

$$
\hat{\theta}_{\text{QMLE}} = \arg\max_{\theta \in \Theta} \sum_{i=1}^N \ln f\left(Y_i \mid X_i, Z_i; \theta\right), \quad \text{E}[Y_i \mid X_i] = g^{-1}(X_i^\top \beta)
$$
