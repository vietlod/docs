## 1. Methodological Meaning and Scope of Application

### 1.1. Econometric Foundation and Identification Mechanism

Jointly estimates binary selection into treatment and regime-specific outcome equations under correlated unobservables to recover ATT and ATU. Across empirical econometrics, `endogenous_switching` enables researchers to isolate structural parameters while controlling for confounding covariates and verifying identification assumptions via `movestay` in Stata 18 MP, `sampleSelection` in R 4.x, and `statsmodels / scipy` in Python 3.11+.

### 1.2. Scope of Application and Sample Structure Requirements

Continuous outcome Y, binary regime/treatment indicator D in [0, 1], and outcome covariates plus optional exclusion instruments. Mandatory post-estimation verification includes testing identification restrictions, evaluating goodness-of-fit metrics, and confirming numerical convergence across all three econometric engines.

### 1.3. Structural Equations and Asymptotic Covariance Specification

Core structural equation for `endogenous_switching`:

$$
D_i = \mathbb{I}(Z_i^\top \gamma + u_i \gt 0), \quad Y_{1i} = X_i^\top \beta_1 + \varepsilon_{1i} \;\text{nếu } D_i=1, \quad Y_{0i} = X_i^\top \beta_0 + \varepsilon_{0i} \;\text{nếu } D_i=0
$$

Estimator objective function and sample moment conditions:

$$
\hat{\theta}_{\text{QMLE}} = \arg\max_{\theta \in \Theta} \sum_{i=1}^N \ln f\left(Y_i \mid X_i, Z_i; \theta\right), \quad \text{E}[Y_i \mid X_i] = g^{-1}(X_i^\top \beta)
$$

Asymptotic covariance matrix and post-estimation diagnostic metric:

$$
\widehat{\text{Var}}_{\text{QMLE}}(\hat{\theta}) = \hat{H}_N^{-1}\left(\sum_{i=1}^N s_i(\hat{\theta}) s_i(\hat{\theta})^\top\right)\hat{H}_N^{-1}, \quad \text{Var}(Y_i \mid X_i) = \mu_i + \alpha \mu_i^p
$$
