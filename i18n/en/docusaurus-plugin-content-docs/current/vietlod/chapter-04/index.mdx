## 1. Central Research Question and Core Mathematical Specification

- **Central Research Question**: How to consistently estimate count outcomes, bilateral trade flows with excess zeros, censored thresholds, and endogenous sample selection?
- **Chapter Architecture**: Encompasses `4` specialized methodological groups and `15` empirical models validated on Vietnamese datasets from [EcoData](https://ecodata.io.vn) and [Finco](https://www.finco.vn).

Core structural equation for Chapter 04:

$$
\text{E}[Y_{ij} \mid X_{ij}] = \exp(X_{ij}^\top \beta + \mu_i + \nu_j)
$$

## 3. Four-Step Workflow for Selecting and Executing Models in Chapter 04

<Steps>

### Step 1. Select the Appropriate Methodological Group Across the 4 Groups

Match your economic hypothesis and sample structure against the identification requirements from Group `4.1` through Group `4.4`.

### Step 2. Prepare Standardized Datasets From EcoData or Finco

Download the standardized `.csv` practice file for your target estimator from `/src/ecoaz/data/` or extract live empirical series from [EcoData](https://ecodata.io.vn) and [Finco](https://www.finco.vn).

### Step 3. Estimate Parameters and Run Diagnostics in Stata 18, R 4.x, and Python 3.11+

Execute the tri-engine scripts provided in each model guide and verify the mandatory post-estimation diagnostics: **Cameron–Trivedi Overdispersion Test, Vuong/AIC-BIC Zero-Inflation Test, Silva–Tenreyro RESET, Inverse Mills Ratio (IMR) Test, Modified Park Test**.

### Step 4. Verify Three-Software Convergence and Export Publication Tables on EcoLab

Confirm numerical convergence across Stata, R, and Python using the three-panel empirical figure and generate journal-grade tables on [EcoLab](https://ecolab.io.vn).

</Steps>
