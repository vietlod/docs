## 4.1. Central Research Question and Core Mathematical Specification

- **Central Research Question**: How to consistently estimate count outcomes, bilateral trade flows with excess zeros, censored thresholds, and endogenous sample selection?
- **Chapter Architecture**: Encompasses `4` specialized methodological parts from `4.1` to `4.4` and `15` empirical model lessons validated on Vietnamese datasets from [EcoData](https://ecodata.io.vn) and [Finco](https://www.finco.vn).

Core structural equation for Chapter 4:

$$
\text{E}[Y_{ij} \mid X_{ij}] = \exp(X_{ij}^\top \beta + \mu_i + \nu_j)
$$
