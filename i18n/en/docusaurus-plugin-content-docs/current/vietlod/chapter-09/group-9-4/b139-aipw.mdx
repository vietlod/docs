## 1. Methodological Meaning and Scope of Application

### 1.1. Econometric Foundation and Identification Mechanism

Estimates the Doubly Robust Augmented IPW — AIPW specification within the Inverse Probability Weighting, Synthetic Control and Causal ML framework by optimizing the structural objective function and computing heteroskedasticity- and cluster-robust asymptotic standard errors. Across empirical econometrics, `aipw` enables researchers to isolate structural parameters while controlling for confounding covariates and verifying identification assumptions via `base Stata` in Stata 18 MP, `AIPW / stats` in R 4.x, and `doubleml / statsmodels` in Python 3.11+.

### 1.2. Scope of Application and Sample Structure Requirements

Cross-sectional, pooled, or tabular microeconometric datasets requiring robust covariance estimation and specification testing. Mandatory post-estimation verification includes testing identification restrictions, evaluating goodness-of-fit metrics, and confirming numerical convergence across all three econometric engines.

### 1.3. Structural Equations and Asymptotic Covariance Specification

Core structural equation for `aipw`:

$$
\hat{\tau}_{\text{AIPW}} = \frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^N \left[\hat{\mu}_1(X_i) - \hat{\mu}_0(X_i) + \frac{D_i(Y_i - \hat{\mu}_1(X_i))}{\hat{e}(X_i)} - \frac{(1-D_i)(Y_i - \hat{\mu}_0(X_i))}{1 - \hat{e}(X_i)}\right]
$$

Estimator objective function and sample moment conditions:

$$
\tau_{\text{ATT}} = \text{E}\left[Y_{it}(1) - Y_{it}(0) \;\middle|\; D_i = 1\right], \quad \text{E}_N\left[\psi\left(W_i; \hat{\tau}, \hat{\eta}\right)\right] = 0
$$

Asymptotic covariance matrix and post-estimation diagnostic metric:

$$
\sqrt{N}\left(\hat{\tau} - \tau_0\right) \xrightarrow{d} \mathcal{N}\left(0, V_\psi\right), \quad V_\psi = \text{E}\left[\psi\left(W_i; \tau_0, \eta_0\right)^2\right], \quad W_{\text{pre}} = \hat{\beta}_{\text{pre}}^\top \hat{\Sigma}_{\text{pre}}^{-1}\hat{\beta}_{\text{pre}} \xrightarrow{d} \chi^2_{K_{\text{pre}}}
$$
