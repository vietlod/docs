## 1. Methodological Meaning and Scope of Application

### 1.1. Econometric Foundation and Identification Mechanism

Augments classical simplex donor weights with a Ridge outcome-model bias correction to eliminate pre-treatment mismatch when treated units lie near or outside the donor convex hull. Across empirical econometrics, `augmented_synthetic_control` enables researchers to isolate structural parameters while controlling for confounding covariates and verifying identification assumptions via `synth / fect` in Stata 18 MP, `Synth / fect` in R 4.x, and `pysyncon` in Python 3.11+.

### 1.2. Scope of Application and Sample Structure Requirements

Comparative policy case studies with 1 or few treated units and T_0 &gt;= 4 pre-intervention periods. Mandatory post-estimation verification includes testing identification restrictions, evaluating goodness-of-fit metrics, and confirming numerical convergence across all three econometric engines.

### 1.3. Structural Equations and Asymptotic Covariance Specification

Core structural equation for `augmented_synthetic_control`:

$$
\hat{Y}_{1T}(0)^{\text{aug}} = \sum_{D_i=0}\hat{w}_i^{\text{scm}}Y_{iT} + \left(\hat{m}(X_1) - \sum_{D_i=0}\hat{w}_i^{\text{scm}}\hat{m}(X_i)\right)
$$

Estimator objective function and sample moment conditions:

$$
\tau_{\text{ATT}} = \text{E}\left[Y_{it}(1) - Y_{it}(0) \;\middle|\; D_i = 1\right], \quad \text{E}_N\left[\psi\left(W_i; \hat{\tau}, \hat{\eta}\right)\right] = 0
$$

Asymptotic covariance matrix and post-estimation diagnostic metric:

$$
\sqrt{N}\left(\hat{\tau} - \tau_0\right) \xrightarrow{d} \mathcal{N}\left(0, V_\psi\right), \quad V_\psi = \text{E}\left[\psi\left(W_i; \tau_0, \eta_0\right)^2\right], \quad W_{\text{pre}} = \hat{\beta}_{\text{pre}}^\top \hat{\Sigma}_{\text{pre}}^{-1}\hat{\beta}_{\text{pre}} \xrightarrow{d} \chi^2_{K_{\text{pre}}}
$$
