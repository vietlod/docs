## 1. Methodological Overview and Structural Equations

- **Methodological Focus**: Provides consistent estimation and robust inference for **Inverse Probability Weighting, Synthetic Control and Causal ML** specifications within **Causal Inference and Modern Policy Evaluation**.
- **Mandatory Post-Estimation Diagnostics**: Kiểm định cân bằng hiệp phương sai có trọng số, kiểm tra xác suất cực đoan gần $0$ hoặc $1$ và sai số chuẩn (GMM) hoặc Bootstrap.

Core structural specification for Group 9.4:

$$
\hat{\tau}_{\text{IPW}} = \frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^N \left[\frac{D_i Y_i}{\hat{e}(X_i)} - \frac{(1 - D_i)Y_i}{1 - \hat{e}(X_i)}\right]
$$

Estimator objective and moment conditions:

$$
\tau_{\text{ATT}} = \text{E}\left[Y_{it}(1) - Y_{it}(0) \;\middle|\; D_i = 1\right], \quad \text{E}_N\left[\psi\left(W_i; \hat{\tau}, \hat{\eta}\right)\right] = 0
$$
