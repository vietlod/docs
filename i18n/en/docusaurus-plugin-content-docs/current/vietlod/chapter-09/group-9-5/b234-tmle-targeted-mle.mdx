## 9.5.9.1. Methodological Meaning and Scope of Application

### 9.5.9.1.1. Econometric Foundation and Identification Mechanism

Updates initial outcome regression predictions along a clever-covariate fluctuation submodel H(D,W) so the Efficient Influence Function estimating equation is solved exactly with double robustness.

### 9.5.9.1.2. Scope of Application and Sample Structure Requirements

Observational causal inference under conditional unconfoundedness requiring substitution-principle bounds preservation and semiparametric efficiency.

- **Mandatory Pre-Estimation Diagnostics**: `propensity_overlap_positivity, covariate_balance_smd`
- **Mandatory Post-Estimation Diagnostics**: `tmle_eif_score_zero, rosenbaum_bounds_check`

### 9.5.9.1.3. Structural Equations and Asymptotic Covariance Specification

Core structural equation for `tmle_targeted_mle`:

$$
\tau_{\text{ATT}}(g, t) = \mathbb{E}\left[Y_{it}(g) - Y_{it}(\infty) \mid G_i = g\right], \quad Y_i = D_i Y_i(1) + (1 - D_i) Y_i(0)
$$

Estimator objective function and sample moment conditions:

$$
\hat{\tau}_{\text{DR}} = \frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^N \left[\hat{\mu}_1(X_i) - \hat{\mu}_0(X_i) + \frac{D_i(Y_i - \hat{\mu}_1(X_i))}{\hat{e}(X_i)} - \frac{(1-D_i)(Y_i - \hat{\mu}_0(X_i))}{1 - \hat{e}(X_i)}\right]
$$

Asymptotic covariance matrix and robust inference metric:

$$
\sqrt{N}(\hat{\tau} - \tau_0) \xrightarrow{d} \mathcal{N}\left(0, \mathbb{E}[\varphi_i^2]\right), \quad \text{CI}_{1-\alpha} = \hat{\tau} \pm z_{1-\alpha/2}\widehat{\text{SE}}(\hat{\tau})
$$

# df: a pandas DataFrame holding your selected variables
import numpy as np, statsmodels.api as sm
d = (df["treat"].values > 0.5).astype(float); W = sm.add_constant(df[['treat', 'x1']])
g = np.clip(sm.Logit(d, W).fit(disp=False).predict(W), 0.05, 0.95)
q = sm.OLS(df["y"].values, np.column_stack([W.values, d])).fit()
H = d / g - (1 - d) / (1 - g)
eps = float((H @ (df["y"].values - q.fittedvalues)) / (H @ H))
print("TMLE fluctuation epsilon:", eps)
