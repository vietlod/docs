## 9.5.14.1. Methodological Meaning and Scope of Application

### 9.5.14.1.1. Econometric Foundation and Identification Mechanism

Constructs propensity odds counterfactual weights Psi(X) = [P(D=1|X)/P(D=1)] / [P(D=0|X)/P(D=0)] and scale-invariant Athey-Imbens quantile mappings to recover mean and distributional policy effects.

### 9.5.14.1.2. Scope of Application and Sample Structure Requirements

Minimum-wage, de-unionization, tax-reform, and distributional policy evaluations across repeated cross-sections or panel groups where impacts vary across outcome quantiles.

- **Mandatory Pre-Estimation Diagnostics**: `covariate_balance_smd, propensity_overlap_positivity`
- **Mandatory Post-Estimation Diagnostics**: `placebo_falsification, pre_trends_joint_wald`

### 9.5.14.1.3. Structural Equations and Asymptotic Covariance Specification

Core structural equation for `changes_in_quantiles_dfl_dinardo`:

$$
\tau_{\text{ATT}}(g, t) = \mathbb{E}\left[Y_{it}(g) - Y_{it}(\infty) \mid G_i = g\right], \quad Y_i = D_i Y_i(1) + (1 - D_i) Y_i(0)
$$

Estimator objective function and sample moment conditions:

$$
\hat{\tau}_{\text{DR}} = \frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^N \left[\hat{\mu}_1(X_i) - \hat{\mu}_0(X_i) + \frac{D_i(Y_i - \hat{\mu}_1(X_i))}{\hat{e}(X_i)} - \frac{(1-D_i)(Y_i - \hat{\mu}_0(X_i))}{1 - \hat{e}(X_i)}\right]
$$

Asymptotic covariance matrix and robust inference metric:

$$
\sqrt{N}(\hat{\tau} - \tau_0) \xrightarrow{d} \mathcal{N}\left(0, \mathbb{E}[\varphi_i^2]\right), \quad \text{CI}_{1-\alpha} = \hat{\tau} \pm z_{1-\alpha/2}\widehat{\text{SE}}(\hat{\tau})
$$
