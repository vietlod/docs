## 1. Methodological Meaning and Scope of Application

### 1.1. Econometric Foundation and Identification Mechanism

Restores full heterogeneity robustness to two-way fixed effects by saturating cohort-by-post-time cells alongside cohort-demeaned covariate interactions. Across empirical econometrics, `wooldridge_etwfe` enables researchers to isolate structural parameters while controlling for confounding covariates and verifying identification assumptions via `Stata 18 xthdidregress / jwdid` in Stata 18 MP, `etwfe` in R 4.x, and `pyfixest` in Python 3.11+.

### 1.2. Scope of Application and Sample Structure Requirements

Staggered panel DID designs with time-varying covariates testing cohort-time effect heterogeneity. Mandatory post-estimation verification includes testing identification restrictions, evaluating goodness-of-fit metrics, and confirming numerical convergence across all three econometric engines.

### 1.3. Structural Equations and Asymptotic Covariance Specification

Core structural equation for `wooldridge_etwfe`:

$$
Y_{it} = \alpha_i + \lambda_t + \sum_{g=q}^T \sum_{s=g}^T \tau_{g,s}\left(D_{ig}\cdot f_s\right) + \sum_{g=q}^T \sum_{s=g}^T \left(D_{ig}\cdot f_s \cdot \dot{X}_i\right)^\top \xi_{g,s} + \varepsilon_{it}
$$

Estimator objective function and sample moment conditions:

$$
\tau_{\text{ATT}} = \text{E}\left[Y_{it}(1) - Y_{it}(0) \;\middle|\; D_i = 1\right], \quad \text{E}_N\left[\psi\left(W_i; \hat{\tau}, \hat{\eta}\right)\right] = 0
$$

Asymptotic covariance matrix and post-estimation diagnostic metric:

$$
\sqrt{N}\left(\hat{\tau} - \tau_0\right) \xrightarrow{d} \mathcal{N}\left(0, V_\psi\right), \quad V_\psi = \text{E}\left[\psi\left(W_i; \tau_0, \eta_0\right)^2\right], \quad W_{\text{pre}} = \hat{\beta}_{\text{pre}}^\top \hat{\Sigma}_{\text{pre}}^{-1}\hat{\beta}_{\text{pre}} \xrightarrow{d} \chi^2_{K_{\text{pre}}}
$$
