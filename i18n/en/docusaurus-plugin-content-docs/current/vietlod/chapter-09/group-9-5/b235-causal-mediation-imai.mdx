## 9.5.10.1. Methodological Meaning and Scope of Application

### 9.5.10.1.1. Econometric Foundation and Identification Mechanism

Decomposes total treatment effects into Average Causal Mediation Effect (ACME) and Average Direct Effect (ADE) under sequential ignorability with Sobel/quasi-Bayesian variance and rho sensitivity.

### 9.5.10.1.2. Scope of Application and Sample Structure Requirements

Policy and behavioral mechanism studies decomposing how much of a treatment impact operates through an observed intermediate channel M versus direct pathways.

- **Mandatory Pre-Estimation Diagnostics**: `covariate_balance_smd, vif_multicollinearity`
- **Mandatory Post-Estimation Diagnostics**: `imai_sobel_mediation, breusch_pagan_heteroskedasticity`

### 9.5.10.1.3. Structural Equations and Asymptotic Covariance Specification

Core structural equation for `causal_mediation_imai`:

$$
\tau_{\text{ATT}}(g, t) = \mathbb{E}\left[Y_{it}(g) - Y_{it}(\infty) \mid G_i = g\right], \quad Y_i = D_i Y_i(1) + (1 - D_i) Y_i(0)
$$

Estimator objective function and sample moment conditions:

$$
\hat{\tau}_{\text{DR}} = \frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^N \left[\hat{\mu}_1(X_i) - \hat{\mu}_0(X_i) + \frac{D_i(Y_i - \hat{\mu}_1(X_i))}{\hat{e}(X_i)} - \frac{(1-D_i)(Y_i - \hat{\mu}_0(X_i))}{1 - \hat{e}(X_i)}\right]
$$

Asymptotic covariance matrix and robust inference metric:

$$
\sqrt{N}(\hat{\tau} - \tau_0) \xrightarrow{d} \mathcal{N}\left(0, \mathbb{E}[\varphi_i^2]\right), \quad \text{CI}_{1-\alpha} = \hat{\tau} \pm z_{1-\alpha/2}\widehat{\text{SE}}(\hat{\tau})
$$
