## 9.5.3.1. Methodological Meaning and Scope of Application

### 9.5.3.1.1. Econometric Foundation and Identification Mechanism

Fits unit, time, and covariate effects exclusively on untreated observations Omega_0 to impute counterfactual Y — 0 — and aggregate unbiased, asymptotically efficient ATTs. Across empirical econometrics, `borusyak_imputation_did` enables researchers to isolate structural parameters while controlling for confounding covariates and verifying identification assumptions via `did_imputation` in Stata 18 MP, `did2s` in R 4.x, and `pyfixest` in Python 3.11+.

### 9.5.3.1.2. Scope of Application and Sample Structure Requirements

Balanced or unbalanced staggered DID panels eliminating negative TWFE weights while achieving Gauss-Markov efficiency. Mandatory post-estimation verification includes testing identification restrictions, evaluating goodness-of-fit metrics, and confirming numerical convergence across all three econometric engines.

### 9.5.3.1.3. Structural Equations and Asymptotic Covariance Specification

Core structural equation for `borusyak_imputation_did`:

$$
\hat{Y}_{it}(0) = \hat{\alpha}_i + \hat{\lambda}_t + X_{it}^\top \hat{\gamma} \;\text{trên }\Omega_0=\{D_{it}=0\}, \quad \hat{\tau}_w = \sum_{(i,t)\in\Omega_1} w_{it}\left(Y_{it} - \hat{Y}_{it}(0)\right)
$$

Estimator objective function and sample moment conditions:

$$
\tau_{\text{ATT}} = \text{E}\left[Y_{it}(1) - Y_{it}(0) \;\middle|\; D_i = 1\right], \quad \text{E}_N\left[\psi\left(W_i; \hat{\tau}, \hat{\eta}\right)\right] = 0
$$

Asymptotic covariance matrix and post-estimation diagnostic metric:

$$
\sqrt{N}\left(\hat{\tau} - \tau_0\right) \xrightarrow{d} \mathcal{N}\left(0, V_\psi\right), \quad V_\psi = \text{E}\left[\psi\left(W_i; \tau_0, \eta_0\right)^2\right], \quad W_{\text{pre}} = \hat{\beta}_{\text{pre}}^\top \hat{\Sigma}_{\text{pre}}^{-1}\hat{\beta}_{\text{pre}} \xrightarrow{d} \chi^2_{K_{\text{pre}}}
$$
