## 1. Methodological Meaning and Scope of Application

### 1.1. Econometric Foundation and Identification Mechanism

Constructs robust partial-identification confidence intervals when post-treatment trend violations are bounded by M_bar times the worst pre-treatment trend deviation. Across empirical econometrics, `honest_did_sensitivity` enables researchers to isolate structural parameters while controlling for confounding covariates and verifying identification assumptions via `honestdid` in Stata 18 MP, `HonestDiD` in R 4.x, and `scipy / statsmodels` in Python 3.11+.

### 1.2. Scope of Application and Sample Structure Requirements

Mandatory sensitivity audit for event-study and staggered DID designs quantifying the exact breakdown threshold M_bar* where causal significance vanishes. Mandatory post-estimation verification includes testing identification restrictions, evaluating goodness-of-fit metrics, and confirming numerical convergence across all three econometric engines.

### 1.3. Structural Equations and Asymptotic Covariance Specification

Core structural equation for `honest_did_sensitivity`:

$$
\Delta_{\text{RM}}(\bar{M}) = \left\{\delta : \forall t \ge 0, \; |\delta_{t+1} - \delta_t| \le \bar{M} \cdot \max_{s \lt 0}|\delta_{s+1} - \delta_s|\right\}, \quad \theta \in \mathcal{S}(\Delta_{\text{RM}}(\bar{M}))
$$

Estimator objective function and sample moment conditions:

$$
\tau_{\text{ATT}} = \text{E}\left[Y_{it}(1) - Y_{it}(0) \;\middle|\; D_i = 1\right], \quad \text{E}_N\left[\psi\left(W_i; \hat{\tau}, \hat{\eta}\right)\right] = 0
$$

Asymptotic covariance matrix and post-estimation diagnostic metric:

$$
\sqrt{N}\left(\hat{\tau} - \tau_0\right) \xrightarrow{d} \mathcal{N}\left(0, V_\psi\right), \quad V_\psi = \text{E}\left[\psi\left(W_i; \tau_0, \eta_0\right)^2\right], \quad W_{\text{pre}} = \hat{\beta}_{\text{pre}}^\top \hat{\Sigma}_{\text{pre}}^{-1}\hat{\beta}_{\text{pre}} \xrightarrow{d} \chi^2_{K_{\text{pre}}}
$$
