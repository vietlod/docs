## 9.5.1. Methodological Overview and Structural Equations

- **Methodological Focus**: Provides consistent estimation and robust inference for **Modern Heterogeneity-Robust Staggered Difference-in-Differences** specifications within **Causal Inference and Modern Policy Evaluation**.
- **Mandatory Post-Estimation Diagnostics**: Tổng tỷ trọng và ước lượng DID trung bình theo 3 nhóm, tỷ phần trọng số bị ô nhiễm động học..

Core structural specification for Part 9.5:

$$
\hat{\beta}_{\text{TWFE}} = \sum_{k \neq U} s_{kU}\hat{\beta}_{kU}^{2\times 2} + \sum_{k \lt \ell} \left(s_{k\ell}^k \hat{\beta}_{k\ell}^{2\times 2, k} + s_{k\ell}^\ell \hat{\beta}_{k\ell}^{2\times 2, \ell}\right), \quad \sum s_{ab} = 1
$$

Estimator objective and moment conditions:

$$
\tau_{\text{ATT}} = \text{E}\left[Y_{it}(1) - Y_{it}(0) \;\middle|\; D_i = 1\right], \quad \text{E}_N\left[\psi\left(W_i; \hat{\tau}, \hat{\eta}\right)\right] = 0
$$
