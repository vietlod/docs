## 9.5.6.1. Methodological Meaning and Scope of Application

### 9.5.6.1.1. Econometric Foundation and Identification Mechanism

Combines horizon-by-horizon local projections with clean-control sample pruning so already-treated units never contaminate dynamic treatment effect estimates. Across empirical econometrics, `lp_did` enables researchers to isolate structural parameters while controlling for confounding covariates and verifying identification assumptions via `lpdid` in Stata 18 MP, `fixest` in R 4.x, and `pyfixest` in Python 3.11+.

### 9.5.6.1.2. Scope of Application and Sample Structure Requirements

Staggered macro, labor, or corporate panels with outcome dynamics requiring clean dynamic event-study paths across h in [-K, H]. Mandatory post-estimation verification includes testing identification restrictions, evaluating goodness-of-fit metrics, and confirming numerical convergence across all three econometric engines.

### 9.5.6.1.3. Structural Equations and Asymptotic Covariance Specification

Core structural equation for `lp_did`:

$$
Y_{i,t+h} - Y_{i,t-1} = \delta_t^h + \beta_h^{\text{LP-DID}}\Delta D_{it} + X_{it}^\top \gamma_h + e_{i,t+h}, \quad \text{trên }\Delta D_{it}=1 \;\text{hoặc}\; D_{i,t+h}=0
$$

Estimator objective function and sample moment conditions:

$$
\tau_{\text{ATT}} = \text{E}\left[Y_{it}(1) - Y_{it}(0) \;\middle|\; D_i = 1\right], \quad \text{E}_N\left[\psi\left(W_i; \hat{\tau}, \hat{\eta}\right)\right] = 0
$$

Asymptotic covariance matrix and post-estimation diagnostic metric:

$$
\sqrt{N}\left(\hat{\tau} - \tau_0\right) \xrightarrow{d} \mathcal{N}\left(0, V_\psi\right), \quad V_\psi = \text{E}\left[\psi\left(W_i; \tau_0, \eta_0\right)^2\right], \quad W_{\text{pre}} = \hat{\beta}_{\text{pre}}^\top \hat{\Sigma}_{\text{pre}}^{-1}\hat{\beta}_{\text{pre}} \xrightarrow{d} \chi^2_{K_{\text{pre}}}
$$
