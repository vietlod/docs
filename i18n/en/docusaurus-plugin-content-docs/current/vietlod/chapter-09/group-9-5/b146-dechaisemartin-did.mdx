## 1. Methodological Meaning and Scope of Application

### 1.1. Econometric Foundation and Identification Mechanism

Compares units switching treatment status against clean control units holding the same baseline treatment status unchanged, allowing policies to turn on and off. Across empirical econometrics, `dechaisemartin_did` enables researchers to isolate structural parameters while controlling for confounding covariates and verifying identification assumptions via `did_multiplegt_dyn` in Stata 18 MP, `DIDmultiplegtDYN` in R 4.x, and `linearmodels` in Python 3.11+.

### 1.2. Scope of Application and Sample Structure Requirements

Panel policies such as tariffs, subsidies, lockdowns, or credit caps that can turn on, turn off, or vary in discrete intensity. Mandatory post-estimation verification includes testing identification restrictions, evaluating goodness-of-fit metrics, and confirming numerical convergence across all three econometric engines.

### 1.3. Structural Equations and Asymptotic Covariance Specification

Core structural equation for `dechaisemartin_did`:

$$
\text{DID}_{\text{M}} = \sum_{t=2}^T \left(\frac{N_{1,0,t}}{N_S}\text{DID}_{+,t} + \frac{N_{0,1,t}}{N_S}\text{DID}_{-,t}\right)
$$

Estimator objective function and sample moment conditions:

$$
\tau_{\text{ATT}} = \text{E}\left[Y_{it}(1) - Y_{it}(0) \;\middle|\; D_i = 1\right], \quad \text{E}_N\left[\psi\left(W_i; \hat{\tau}, \hat{\eta}\right)\right] = 0
$$

Asymptotic covariance matrix and post-estimation diagnostic metric:

$$
\sqrt{N}\left(\hat{\tau} - \tau_0\right) \xrightarrow{d} \mathcal{N}\left(0, V_\psi\right), \quad V_\psi = \text{E}\left[\psi\left(W_i; \tau_0, \eta_0\right)^2\right], \quad W_{\text{pre}} = \hat{\beta}_{\text{pre}}^\top \hat{\Sigma}_{\text{pre}}^{-1}\hat{\beta}_{\text{pre}} \xrightarrow{d} \chi^2_{K_{\text{pre}}}
$$
