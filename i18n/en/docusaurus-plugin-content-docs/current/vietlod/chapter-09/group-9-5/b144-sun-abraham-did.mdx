## 1. Methodological Meaning and Scope of Application

### 1.1. Econometric Foundation and Identification Mechanism

Estimates saturated cohort-specific dynamic treatment effects relative to never-treated or last-treated controls and aggregates them by empirical cohort shares. Across empirical econometrics, `sun_abraham_did` enables researchers to isolate structural parameters while controlling for confounding covariates and verifying identification assumptions via `eventstudyinteract` in Stata 18 MP, `fixest` in R 4.x, and `pyfixest` in Python 3.11+.

### 1.2. Scope of Application and Sample Structure Requirements

Staggered panel policy rollouts with heterogeneous treatment effects across adoption cohorts and dynamic post-treatment horizons. Mandatory post-estimation verification includes testing identification restrictions, evaluating goodness-of-fit metrics, and confirming numerical convergence across all three econometric engines.

### 1.3. Structural Equations and Asymptotic Covariance Specification

Core structural equation for `sun_abraham_did`:

$$
Y_{it} = \alpha_i + \lambda_t + \sum_{e \notin C} \sum_{\ell \neq -1} \delta_{e,\ell}\left(\mathbb{I}\{E_i = e\}\cdot D_{it}^\ell\right) + \varepsilon_{it}, \quad \hat{\nu}_g = \sum_{\ell \in g}\sum_e \hat{w}_{e,\ell}\hat{\delta}_{e,\ell}
$$

Estimator objective function and sample moment conditions:

$$
\tau_{\text{ATT}} = \text{E}\left[Y_{it}(1) - Y_{it}(0) \;\middle|\; D_i = 1\right], \quad \text{E}_N\left[\psi\left(W_i; \hat{\tau}, \hat{\eta}\right)\right] = 0
$$

Asymptotic covariance matrix and post-estimation diagnostic metric:

$$
\sqrt{N}\left(\hat{\tau} - \tau_0\right) \xrightarrow{d} \mathcal{N}\left(0, V_\psi\right), \quad V_\psi = \text{E}\left[\psi\left(W_i; \tau_0, \eta_0\right)^2\right], \quad W_{\text{pre}} = \hat{\beta}_{\text{pre}}^\top \hat{\Sigma}_{\text{pre}}^{-1}\hat{\beta}_{\text{pre}} \xrightarrow{d} \chi^2_{K_{\text{pre}}}
$$
