## 9.5.8.1. Methodological Meaning and Scope of Application

### 9.5.8.1.1. Econometric Foundation and Identification Mechanism

Combines ridge-regularized simplex donor unit weights and pre-treatment time weights inside a two-way fixed-effects regression for doubly robust policy evaluation. Across empirical econometrics, `synthetic_did` enables researchers to isolate structural parameters while controlling for confounding covariates and verifying identification assumptions via `sdid` in Stata 18 MP, `synthdid / fect` in R 4.x, and `pysyncon` in Python 3.11+.

### 9.5.8.1.2. Scope of Application and Sample Structure Requirements

Comparative case studies or block/staggered policy panels with one or multiple treated units and never-treated donor controls. Mandatory post-estimation verification includes testing identification restrictions, evaluating goodness-of-fit metrics, and confirming numerical convergence across all three econometric engines.

### 9.5.8.1.3. Structural Equations and Asymptotic Covariance Specification

Core structural equation for `synthetic_did`:

$$
(\hat{\tau}^{\text{sdid}}, \hat{\mu}, \hat{\alpha}, \hat{\beta}) = \arg\min_{\tau,\mu,\alpha,\beta} \sum_{i=1}^N \sum_{t=1}^T \left(Y_{it} - \mu - \alpha_i - \beta_t - W_{it}\tau\right)^2 \hat{\omega}_i^{\text{sdid}}\hat{\lambda}_t^{\text{sdid}}
$$

Estimator objective function and sample moment conditions:

$$
\tau_{\text{ATT}} = \text{E}\left[Y_{it}(1) - Y_{it}(0) \;\middle|\; D_i = 1\right], \quad \text{E}_N\left[\psi\left(W_i; \hat{\tau}, \hat{\eta}\right)\right] = 0
$$

Asymptotic covariance matrix and post-estimation diagnostic metric:

$$
\sqrt{N}\left(\hat{\tau} - \tau_0\right) \xrightarrow{d} \mathcal{N}\left(0, V_\psi\right), \quad V_\psi = \text{E}\left[\psi\left(W_i; \tau_0, \eta_0\right)^2\right], \quad W_{\text{pre}} = \hat{\beta}_{\text{pre}}^\top \hat{\Sigma}_{\text{pre}}^{-1}\hat{\beta}_{\text{pre}} \xrightarrow{d} \chi^2_{K_{\text{pre}}}
$$
