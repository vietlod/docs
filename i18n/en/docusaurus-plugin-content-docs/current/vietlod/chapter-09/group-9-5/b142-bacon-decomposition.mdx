## 1. Methodological Meaning and Scope of Application

### 1.1. Econometric Foundation and Identification Mechanism

Decomposes the canonical staggered TWFE coefficient into exact 2x2 DID comparisons across Treated vs Untreated, Early vs Late, and forbidden Late vs Already-Treated pairs. Across empirical econometrics, `bacon_decomposition` enables researchers to isolate structural parameters while controlling for confounding covariates and verifying identification assumptions via `bacondecomp` in Stata 18 MP, `bacondecomp` in R 4.x, and `linearmodels` in Python 3.11+.

### 1.2. Scope of Application and Sample Structure Requirements

Balanced or staggered multi-period panel DID designs diagnosing negative-weight and dynamic-effect contamination in standard TWFE. Mandatory post-estimation verification includes testing identification restrictions, evaluating goodness-of-fit metrics, and confirming numerical convergence across all three econometric engines.

### 1.3. Structural Equations and Asymptotic Covariance Specification

Core structural equation for `bacon_decomposition`:

$$
\hat{\beta}_{\text{TWFE}} = \sum_{k \neq U} s_{kU}\hat{\beta}_{kU}^{2\times 2} + \sum_{k \lt \ell} \left(s_{k\ell}^k \hat{\beta}_{k\ell}^{2\times 2, k} + s_{k\ell}^\ell \hat{\beta}_{k\ell}^{2\times 2, \ell}\right), \quad \sum s_{ab} = 1
$$

Estimator objective function and sample moment conditions:

$$
\tau_{\text{ATT}} = \text{E}\left[Y_{it}(1) - Y_{it}(0) \;\middle|\; D_i = 1\right], \quad \text{E}_N\left[\psi\left(W_i; \hat{\tau}, \hat{\eta}\right)\right] = 0
$$

Asymptotic covariance matrix and post-estimation diagnostic metric:

$$
\sqrt{N}\left(\hat{\tau} - \tau_0\right) \xrightarrow{d} \mathcal{N}\left(0, V_\psi\right), \quad V_\psi = \text{E}\left[\psi\left(W_i; \tau_0, \eta_0\right)^2\right], \quad W_{\text{pre}} = \hat{\beta}_{\text{pre}}^\top \hat{\Sigma}_{\text{pre}}^{-1}\hat{\beta}_{\text{pre}} \xrightarrow{d} \chi^2_{K_{\text{pre}}}
$$
