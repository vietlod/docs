## 9.1. Central Research Question and Core Mathematical Specification

- **Central Research Question**: How to identify Average Treatment Effects using matching, staggered Difference-in-Differences, regression discontinuity, synthetic controls, and Double Machine Learning?
- **Chapter Architecture**: Encompasses `5` specialized methodological parts from `9.1` to `9.5` and `37` empirical model lessons validated on Vietnamese datasets from [EcoData](https://ecodata.io.vn) and [Finco](https://www.finco.vn).

Core structural equation for Chapter 9:

$$
\text{ATT}(g, t) = \text{E}[Y_{it}(g) - Y_{it}(\infty) \mid G_{ig} = 1]
$$
