## 1. Central Research Question and Core Mathematical Specification

- **Central Research Question**: How to identify Average Treatment Effects using matching, staggered Difference-in-Differences, regression discontinuity, synthetic controls, and Double Machine Learning?
- **Chapter Architecture**: Encompasses `5` specialized methodological groups and `25` empirical models validated on Vietnamese datasets from [EcoData](https://ecodata.io.vn) and [Finco](https://www.finco.vn).

Core structural equation for Chapter 09:

$$
\text{ATT}(g, t) = \text{E}[Y_{it}(g) - Y_{it}(\infty) \mid G_{ig} = 1]
$$

## 3. Four-Step Workflow for Selecting and Executing Models in Chapter 09

<Steps>

### Step 1. Select the Appropriate Methodological Group Across the 5 Groups

Match your economic hypothesis and sample structure against the identification requirements from Group `9.1` through Group `9.5`.

### Step 2. Prepare Standardized Datasets From EcoData or Finco

Download the standardized `.csv` practice file for your target estimator from `/src/ecoaz/data/` or extract live empirical series from [EcoData](https://ecodata.io.vn) and [Finco](https://www.finco.vn).

### Step 3. Estimate Parameters and Run Diagnostics in Stata 18, R 4.x, and Python 3.11+

Execute the tri-engine scripts provided in each model guide and verify the mandatory post-estimation diagnostics: **Joint Pre-Trend Wald Test, Goodman-Bacon Negative Weight Diagnostic, Rambachan–Roth HonestDiD Breakdown M, Cattaneo rddensity Manipulation Test, SMD Balance Check**.

### Step 4. Verify Three-Software Convergence and Export Publication Tables on EcoLab

Confirm numerical convergence across Stata, R, and Python using the three-panel empirical figure and generate journal-grade tables on [EcoLab](https://ecolab.io.vn).

</Steps>
