## 1. Methodological Meaning and Scope of Application

### 1.1. Econometric Foundation and Identification Mechanism

Estimates the Dynamic Leads-and-Lags Event-Study DID specification within the Canonical Difference-in-Differences and Event Study framework by optimizing the structural objective function and computing heteroskedasticity- and cluster-robust asymptotic standard errors. Across empirical econometrics, `event_study_did` enables researchers to isolate structural parameters while controlling for confounding covariates and verifying identification assumptions via `Stata reghdfe / xtreg` in Stata 18 MP, `fixest` in R 4.x, and `linearmodels / statsmodels` in Python 3.11+.

### 1.2. Scope of Application and Sample Structure Requirements

Longitudinal panel datasets tracking $N$ cross-sectional units across $T$ time periods with unobserved unit and time effects. Mandatory post-estimation verification includes testing identification restrictions, evaluating goodness-of-fit metrics, and confirming numerical convergence across all three econometric engines.

### 1.3. Structural Equations and Asymptotic Covariance Specification

Core structural equation for `event_study_did`:

$$
Y_{it} = \alpha_i + \lambda_t + \sum_{\ell \neq -1} \beta_\ell \mathbb{I}(t - G_i = \ell) + X_{it}^\top \gamma + \varepsilon_{it}
$$

Estimator objective function and sample moment conditions:

$$
\tau_{\text{ATT}} = \text{E}\left[Y_{it}(1) - Y_{it}(0) \;\middle|\; D_i = 1\right], \quad \text{E}_N\left[\psi\left(W_i; \hat{\tau}, \hat{\eta}\right)\right] = 0
$$

Asymptotic covariance matrix and post-estimation diagnostic metric:

$$
\sqrt{N}\left(\hat{\tau} - \tau_0\right) \xrightarrow{d} \mathcal{N}\left(0, V_\psi\right), \quad V_\psi = \text{E}\left[\psi\left(W_i; \tau_0, \eta_0\right)^2\right], \quad W_{\text{pre}} = \hat{\beta}_{\text{pre}}^\top \hat{\Sigma}_{\text{pre}}^{-1}\hat{\beta}_{\text{pre}} \xrightarrow{d} \chi^2_{K_{\text{pre}}}
$$
