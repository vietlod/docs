## 9.1.1. Methodological Overview and Structural Equations

- **Methodological Focus**: Provides consistent estimation and robust inference for **Propensity Matching and Covariate Balancing** specifications within **Causal Inference and Modern Policy Evaluation**.
- **Mandatory Post-Estimation Diagnostics**: Kiểm tra miền giao thoa chung, chênh lệch chuẩn hóa trung bình các biến đồng biến sau ghép cặp, tỷ số phương sai và phân tích độ nhạy Rosenbaum Bounds.

Core structural specification for Part 9.1:

$$
\text{ATT}_{\text{PSM}} = \text{E}_{e(X)\mid D=1}\Big\{\text{E}[Y_i \mid D_i=1, e(X_i)] - \text{E}[Y_i \mid D_i=0, e(X_i)]\Big\}
$$

Estimator objective and moment conditions:

$$
\tau_{\text{ATT}} = \text{E}\left[Y_{it}(1) - Y_{it}(0) \;\middle|\; D_i = 1\right], \quad \text{E}_N\left[\psi\left(W_i; \hat{\tau}, \hat{\eta}\right)\right] = 0
$$
