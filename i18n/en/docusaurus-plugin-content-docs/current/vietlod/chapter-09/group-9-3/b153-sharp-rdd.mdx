## 9.3.1.1. Methodological Meaning and Scope of Application

### 9.3.1.1.1. Econometric Foundation and Identification Mechanism

Estimates the Sharp Regression Discontinuity Design specification within the Regression Discontinuity, Kink, Bunching and Marginal Effects framework by optimizing the structural objective function and computing heteroskedasticity- and cluster-robust asymptotic standard errors. Across empirical econometrics, `sharp_rdd` enables researchers to isolate structural parameters while controlling for confounding covariates and verifying identification assumptions via `Stata rdrobust` in Stata 18 MP, `rdrobust / stats` in R 4.x, and `rdrobust / statsmodels` in Python 3.11+.

### 9.3.1.1.2. Scope of Application and Sample Structure Requirements

Cross-sectional, pooled, or tabular microeconometric datasets requiring robust covariance estimation and specification testing. Mandatory post-estimation verification includes testing identification restrictions, evaluating goodness-of-fit metrics, and confirming numerical convergence across all three econometric engines.

### 9.3.1.1.3. Structural Equations and Asymptotic Covariance Specification

Core structural equation for `sharp_rdd`:

$$
\tau_{\text{SRD}} = \lim_{x \downarrow c}\text{E}[Y_i \mid X_i = x] - \lim_{x \uparrow c}\text{E}[Y_i \mid X_i = x]
$$

Estimator objective function and sample moment conditions:

$$
\tau_{\text{ATT}} = \text{E}\left[Y_{it}(1) - Y_{it}(0) \;\middle|\; D_i = 1\right], \quad \text{E}_N\left[\psi\left(W_i; \hat{\tau}, \hat{\eta}\right)\right] = 0
$$

Asymptotic covariance matrix and post-estimation diagnostic metric:

$$
\sqrt{N}\left(\hat{\tau} - \tau_0\right) \xrightarrow{d} \mathcal{N}\left(0, V_\psi\right), \quad V_\psi = \text{E}\left[\psi\left(W_i; \tau_0, \eta_0\right)^2\right], \quad W_{\text{pre}} = \hat{\beta}_{\text{pre}}^\top \hat{\Sigma}_{\text{pre}}^{-1}\hat{\beta}_{\text{pre}} \xrightarrow{d} \chi^2_{K_{\text{pre}}}
$$
