## 9.3.7.1. Methodological Meaning and Scope of Application

### 9.3.7.1.1. Econometric Foundation and Identification Mechanism

Estimates distributional quantile jumps at a running-variable threshold via local linear quantile smoothing, recovering local quantile treatment effects across lower, median, and upper tails.

### 9.3.7.1.2. Scope of Application and Sample Structure Requirements

Sharp or fuzzy cutoff-based eligibility designs where policy impacts differ sharply across the conditional outcome distribution rather than shifting only the mean.

- **Mandatory Pre-Estimation Diagnostics**: `mccrary_density_manipulation, covariate_continuity_at_cutoff`
- **Mandatory Post-Estimation Diagnostics**: `frandsen_local_q50_jump, rdd_bandwidth_sensitivity`

### 9.3.7.1.3. Structural Equations and Asymptotic Covariance Specification

Core structural equation for `quantile_rdd_frandsen`:

$$
\tau_{\text{ATT}}(g, t) = \mathbb{E}\left[Y_{it}(g) - Y_{it}(\infty) \mid G_i = g\right], \quad Y_i = D_i Y_i(1) + (1 - D_i) Y_i(0)
$$

Estimator objective function and sample moment conditions:

$$
\hat{\tau}_{\text{DR}} = \frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^N \left[\hat{\mu}_1(X_i) - \hat{\mu}_0(X_i) + \frac{D_i(Y_i - \hat{\mu}_1(X_i))}{\hat{e}(X_i)} - \frac{(1-D_i)(Y_i - \hat{\mu}_0(X_i))}{1 - \hat{e}(X_i)}\right]
$$

Asymptotic covariance matrix and robust inference metric:

$$
\sqrt{N}(\hat{\tau} - \tau_0) \xrightarrow{d} \mathcal{N}\left(0, \mathbb{E}[\varphi_i^2]\right), \quad \text{CI}_{1-\alpha} = \hat{\tau} \pm z_{1-\alpha/2}\widehat{\text{SE}}(\hat{\tau})
$$
