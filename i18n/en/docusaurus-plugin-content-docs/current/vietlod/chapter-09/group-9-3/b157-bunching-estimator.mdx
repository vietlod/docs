## 1. Methodological Meaning and Scope of Application

### 1.1. Econometric Foundation and Identification Mechanism

Fits an integration-constrained counterfactual polynomial histogram excluding the manipulation window around threshold z* to quantify normalized excess bunching mass b and behavioral elasticity e. Across empirical econometrics, `bunching_estimator` enables researchers to isolate structural parameters while controlling for confounding covariates and verifying identification assumptions via `bunchit` in Stata 18 MP, `bunching` in R 4.x, and `statsmodels` in Python 3.11+.

### 1.2. Scope of Application and Sample Structure Requirements

Micro-level income, firm-size, tax, or credit distributions exhibiting behavioral clustering at statutory kink or notch cutoffs. Mandatory post-estimation verification includes testing identification restrictions, evaluating goodness-of-fit metrics, and confirming numerical convergence across all three econometric engines.

### 1.3. Structural Equations and Asymptotic Covariance Specification

Core structural equation for `bunching_estimator`:

$$
B = \int_{z^\ast - \delta_L}^{z^\ast + \delta_U}\left(h_0(z) - \hat{h}_0^{\text{cf}}(z)\right)dz, \quad b = \frac{B}{h_0^{\text{cf}}(z^\ast)}, \quad e \approx \frac{b}{z^\ast \cdot \ln\left(\frac{1 - t_0}{1 - t_1}\right)}
$$

Estimator objective function and sample moment conditions:

$$
\tau_{\text{ATT}} = \text{E}\left[Y_{it}(1) - Y_{it}(0) \;\middle|\; D_i = 1\right], \quad \text{E}_N\left[\psi\left(W_i; \hat{\tau}, \hat{\eta}\right)\right] = 0
$$

Asymptotic covariance matrix and post-estimation diagnostic metric:

$$
\sqrt{N}\left(\hat{\tau} - \tau_0\right) \xrightarrow{d} \mathcal{N}\left(0, V_\psi\right), \quad V_\psi = \text{E}\left[\psi\left(W_i; \tau_0, \eta_0\right)^2\right], \quad W_{\text{pre}} = \hat{\beta}_{\text{pre}}^\top \hat{\Sigma}_{\text{pre}}^{-1}\hat{\beta}_{\text{pre}} \xrightarrow{d} \chi^2_{K_{\text{pre}}}
$$
