## 1. Methodological Meaning and Scope of Application

### 1.1. Econometric Foundation and Identification Mechanism

Selects data-driven MSE-optimal bandwidths, removes higher-order smoothing bias, and adjusts standard errors for bias-estimation uncertainty alongside density testing. Across empirical econometrics, `rdrobust_sharp_fuzzy` enables researchers to isolate structural parameters while controlling for confounding covariates and verifying identification assumptions via `rdrobust` in Stata 18 MP, `rdrobust` in R 4.x, and `rdrobust` in Python 3.11+.

### 1.2. Scope of Application and Sample Structure Requirements

Policy cutoffs on a continuous running variable under either Sharp deterministic assignment or Fuzzy imperfect compliance. Mandatory post-estimation verification includes testing identification restrictions, evaluating goodness-of-fit metrics, and confirming numerical convergence across all three econometric engines.

### 1.3. Structural Equations and Asymptotic Covariance Specification

Core structural equation for `rdrobust_sharp_fuzzy`:

$$
\hat{\tau}_{\text{RBC}} = \hat{\tau}_p(h) - h^{p+1}\hat{\mathcal{B}}_{p,q}(h, b), \quad T_{\text{RBC}} = \frac{\hat{\tau}_{\text{RBC}}}{\sqrt{\widehat{\text{Var}}(\hat{\tau}_p(h)) + \widehat{\text{Var}}(h^{p+1}\hat{\mathcal{B}}_{p,q})}}
$$

Estimator objective function and sample moment conditions:

$$
\tau_{\text{ATT}} = \text{E}\left[Y_{it}(1) - Y_{it}(0) \;\middle|\; D_i = 1\right], \quad \text{E}_N\left[\psi\left(W_i; \hat{\tau}, \hat{\eta}\right)\right] = 0
$$

Asymptotic covariance matrix and post-estimation diagnostic metric:

$$
\sqrt{N}\left(\hat{\tau} - \tau_0\right) \xrightarrow{d} \mathcal{N}\left(0, V_\psi\right), \quad V_\psi = \text{E}\left[\psi\left(W_i; \tau_0, \eta_0\right)^2\right], \quad W_{\text{pre}} = \hat{\beta}_{\text{pre}}^\top \hat{\Sigma}_{\text{pre}}^{-1}\hat{\beta}_{\text{pre}} \xrightarrow{d} \chi^2_{K_{\text{pre}}}
$$
