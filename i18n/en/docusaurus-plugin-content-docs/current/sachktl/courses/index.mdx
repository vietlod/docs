## Step-by-Step Course Enrollment and Completion Workflow

<Steps>
  <div className="step-item">
    <h3>Step 1: Authenticate via TNSAI Single Sign-On on the Course Catalog</h3>
    <p>Visit <a href="https://sachktl.tnsai.vn/khoa-hoc/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">sachktl.tnsai.vn/khoa-hoc/</a>, select your target course level, and enroll free of charge using your unified TNSAI account.</p>
  </div>
  <div className="step-item">
    <h3>Step 2: Progress Sequentially Through Thematic Sessions and Model Lectures</h3>
    <p>Each session corresponds to a quantitative methodological group and includes video instruction, mathematical formulations, Stata/R/Python scripts, empirical CSV datasets from <a href="https://ecodata.io.vn" target="_blank" rel="noopener">EcoData</a> and <a href="https://www.finco.vn" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Finco</a>, and three-panel verification charts.</p>
  </div>
  <div className="step-item">
    <h3>Step 3: Replicate Estimations and Generate Tables on EcoLab</h3>
    <p>Download the practice dataset in each lecture for local replication or launch automated estimation on <a href="https://ecolab.io.vn" target="_blank" rel="noopener">EcoLab</a> to produce publication-grade regression tables.</p>
  </div>
</Steps>
