# Overview of the SachKTL Textbook Library and Course Center

<Available plans={["Academic Free", "Researcher Pro", "Institutional VIP"]} />

**SachKTL** at [sachktl.tnsai.vn](https://sachktl.tnsai.vn) is the flagship econometrics textbook library and applied data analysis course center within the [TNSAI](https://www.tnsai.vn) quantitative research ecosystem. The platform unites **30 specialized textbooks** spanning **503 methodological chapters** and **2,650 detailed lessons**, alongside **4 structured hands-on courses covering 201 econometric models** across `Stata 18 MP`, `R 4.x`, `Python 3.11+`, and `IBM SPSS 29`.

## Step-by-Step Workflow for Leveraging SachKTL in Academic Research

<Steps>
  <div className="step-item">
    <h3>1. Authenticate via TNSAI Single Sign-On and Select Your Learning Track</h3>
    <p>Visit <a href="https://sachktl.tnsai.vn" target="_blank" rel="noopener">sachktl.tnsai.vn</a> and sign in with your unified <a href="https://www.tnsai.vn" target="_blank" rel="noopener">TNSAI</a> account. The authentication bridge automatically redirects you back to your active textbook, chapter, or course page.</p>
  </div>
  <div className="step-item">
    <h3>2. Study Methodological Foundations Across 30 Textbooks or Enroll in 4 Courses</h3>
    <p>Select the textbook matching your thesis, dissertation, or journal manuscript topic from the 30-book directory below, or enroll free of charge in the <a href="https://sachktl.tnsai.vn/khoa-hoc/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">4 structured applied econometrics courses</a>.</p>
  </div>
  <div className="step-item">
    <h3>3. Prepare Empirical Datasets From EcoData and Finco</h3>
    <p>Pair methodological concepts from SachKTL with standardized Vietnamese macroeconomic and corporate data from <a href="https://ecodata.io.vn" target="_blank" rel="noopener">EcoData</a> and <a href="https://www.finco.vn" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Finco</a>, or download ready-to-run CSV files from <a href="https://vietlod.tnsai.vn" target="_blank" rel="noopener">VietLOD</a>.</p>
  </div>
  <div className="step-item">
    <h3>4. Execute Multi-Platform Code and Generate Publication Tables on EcoLab</h3>
    <p>Run estimation and diagnostic scripts in Stata 18 MP, R 4.x, Python 3.11+, or IBM SPSS 29 following our how-to guides, then automate publication-ready reporting on <a href="https://ecolab.io.vn" target="_blank" rel="noopener">EcoLab</a>.</p>
  </div>
</Steps>
